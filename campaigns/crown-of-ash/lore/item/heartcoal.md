# The Heartcoal
**Homebrew artifact.** A lump of something like coal, the size of a big man's fist, ridged and veined like a clenched muscle. It is always warm, as warm as a living body. In darkness a dull red glow pulses deep inside it, slow as a sleeper's heartbeat. It smells faintly of hot iron and old blood. For thirty-eight years it sat in the socket of the Crown of Ash, and on the night King Ivoard died someone pried it out.

## What people believe
- **The Ember Law** (proclaimed by Hask in 1453 DR): no one may wear the Crown of Ash unless the Coal kindles for them. The Watch swore to keep the peace "until the Coal chooses," the Pyrewardens' rite requires it, and most commoners believe in it.
- Hask brought it with him, and it kindled only for him. People say it "knows its king."
- It burns thieves. (Queen Ylia's "fever" and the cupbearer's bandaged hand feed this story.)

## What has happened to it (as far as Kharun knows)
- **Who took it:** at the noon council on day 10, the king's cupbearer Isine Kettle testified that she pried it from the crown with the king's own knife on the night he died, obeying his last words, *"Break it."*
- **The false kindling:** on the night of day 2 the Pyrewardens crowned Kitwin with grains in the empty socket and proclaimed him "kindled" at dawn. At noon on day 3 the Watch's captains ruled that the Coal had not chosen him.
- **The forge:** on the evening of day 9, Ashward elders searching Okton's forge found the scorched hollow under the hearth. Okton admitted the Coal had been kept there to stop the Choir feeding it, and said it is gone "somewhere the Choir cannot reach." He told the Lord-Marshal the same: "It was. It is not now. I will not say where."
- **Officially:** its whereabouts are unknown. Under the regency (see lamplit-council) Ossmaar is governed "until the Coal is broken or found."

## DM secrets
**Where it is.** Sealed in the vault under Dunmarrow's keep since day 8 (see dunmarrow): inside an iron-lined casket, behind the vault's barred inner iron door, with the outer plug re-walled in old mortar and rubble. Chadickith, Gwenis and Kita carried it out of Kharun by the Saltgate postern on the night of the real run, past Lorard's pickets and a Choir scout (killed on the Gallows Road). Iron and brine keep it quiet for days. Okton could not say what weeks would do, and whoever carries it long will hear it. Ulric Vane saw a coffin-shaped iron box go into the keep and took it for the entombment of one of the Order's dead.

**Who knows what.** The Order knows the vault. Halda knows it left by the Saltgate. Oselle knows only that it is out of Kharun and that the Watch opened a door for it. The Ashward elders know it was in the forge. Rook's orderly overheard "the Coal can't choose," so Rook (and through him perhaps the Choir) suspects it left the city. The Choir does not know the site.

**Properties (homebrew; hidden story mechanics, approved).**
- **No sentience and no voice, only appetite.** It cannot be attuned by choice.
- **Heat and pulse:** it runs hot against whoever carries it. A host of the burn near it feels the rage stir and hears its pulse (Chadickith's eyes caught red and his rage stirred carrying it; Isine heard the pulse through the forge floor). A death close by makes it surge.
- **It defends itself:** anyone who tries to break it or pry it from a setting takes 2d6 fire damage. Carried wrapped in cloth, it is merely warm.
- **Touching it** infects outright (see ashblood). Isine touched it barehanded and lived because she was willing.
- **Kindling:** in the crown on a living head it kindled for Hask and, in secret during the Quiet Year, for Kitwin. For others it stays a dull ember. (The v1 stage names in older notes, Touched, Kindled and Glad, are retired. Whether it stirs for a Banked is open; see order-of-the-banked-fire.)
- **Feeding:** uncrowned, it drinks the deaths of anyone who dies anointed with its pyre-ash, which is how the Velun kings fed it through the Sky Gallows. Crowned, it binds to its wearer, drinks every death done in the wearer's name, and gives back vigour and a heavy, unnerving presence. Hask aged no further than fifty until his final year. Left unfed, it slowly burns a crowned wearer from within.
- **The beacon** that drew the Gallowsbird died with the roc on the night of day 2.
- **Indestructible by ordinary means.** Hammers glance off it, and mundane fire only feeds it.
- **The Last Ember casket:** at some point the Choir took dust from it (Okton never knew). Mixed with pyre-ash, those grains fill the iron casket in the Pyrehouse crypt; they can make the crown glow, and they make the Choir's wafers.

**Origin (DM-confirmed).** It is the burned-down heart of **Yaga-Shura**, the fire giant Bhaalspawn who besieged Saradush in Tethyr during the Bhaalspawn Crisis (1369 DR). Yaga-Shura removed his own heart and kept it wrapped in magical flame. When the heart was extinguished, the fire didn't go out completely: a coal of the Lord of Murder's essence survived inside. Chadickith does not know this.

**How it came to Ossmaar.**
- After the siege, a Bhaalist survivor of Yaga-Shura's camp carried the coal east. Around **1398 DR** he sold it to **King Uzhar Velun** of Ossmaar, joined the Pyrewardens, and founded their secret inner circle, the Glad Choir (see pyrewardens).
- The Velun kings kept it in a cold-iron reliquary in the Pyrehouse crypt and called it **the Last Ember**. Each Velun heir was shown it at sixteen. Around 1401 Uzhar raised the Sky Gallows, and the Pyrewardens began the Ash Anointing, to feed it.
- Uzhar's son Tazhar fed it too sparingly, so the Choir went looking for a hungrier master. They found the sellsword Ivoard Hask, told him what lay in the crypt, and opened the Pyrehouse to him on the Night of Cinders (1453).
- Hask had Okton reforge the reliquary's cold iron into the Crown of Ash with the coal at its brow, invented the Ember Law, and let everyone believe the coal was his own.
- Since Bhaal's return to godhood in recent years, the coal has grown hungrier, which may be why Hask lost his struggle with it.

**Destroying it.** Only a fire giant's forge, fed with a fire giant's own heart's blood, can unmake what fire giant fire made. Okton can do it, at a cost to himself. It stops all new infections; existing hosts keep the burn, and the Quenching still works. It would also carry out the dead king's true last order. Okton means to do it once the Order is strong enough to survive the aftermath.

**The option nobody has taken.** Crown someone with no coal at all, or with a forged one, and break the Ember Law by fiat. Okton could forge a convincing false coal. Oselle already has one in ember-glass (see oselle-velun). The Choir would know it was a lie.
