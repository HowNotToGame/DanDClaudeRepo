# The Heartcoal
**Homebrew artifact.** A lump of something like coal, the size of a big man's fist, ridged and veined like a clenched muscle. It is always warm, as warm as a living body. In darkness a dull red glow pulses deep inside it, slow as a sleeper's heartbeat. It smells faintly of hot iron and old blood. For thirty-eight years it sat in the socket of the Crown of Ash, and on the night King Ivoard died someone pried it out.

## What people believe
- **The Ember Law** (proclaimed by Hask in 1453 DR): no one may wear the Crown of Ash unless the Coal kindles for them. The Watch swore to serve "the Coal's king," the Pyrewardens' rite requires it, and most commoners believe in it. Without it, every coronation is a sham.
- Hask brought it with him, and it kindled only for him. People say it "knows its king."
- It burns thieves. (Queen Ylia's "fever" and the cupbearer's bandaged hand feed this story.)

## Why each power wants it
- **Lorard** needs it to open Kharun's gates without a sack.
- **Oselle** needs it kept from the other two, ideally locked in her strongbox and carried by somebody else.
- **Kitwin and the Pyrewardens** need it to complete a rite the boy believes is his by right.
- **House Mostana** has a buyer in Calimshan who will pay a fortune for it.
- **The Kharun Watch** would open the gates for whoever holds it, or sell it.

## DM secrets
**Properties (homebrew).** The beacon, the Ashblood save and the burn are approved hidden story mechanics.
- **No sentience and no voice, only appetite.** It cannot be attuned by choice.
- **Warmth:** whoever carries it feels a pleasant warmth whenever a creature dies within 30 ft. To a raging Barbarian the warmth feels like joy.
- **Beacon:** while it is out of cold iron, each death within 30 ft lets the Gallowsbird fix its location and arrive within 1d4 days (see gallowsbird).
- **It defends itself:** anyone who tries to break it or pry it from a setting takes 2d6 fire damage. Carried wrapped in cloth, it is merely warm.
- **Kindling:** set in the Crown of Ash on a living head, it flares bright red only if the wearer is Kindled or Glad (see ashblood). For a Touched wearer, such as one of the Banked, it gives a single slow pulse. For anyone else it stays a dull ember.
- **Feeding:** uncrowned, it drinks the deaths of anyone who dies anointed with its pyre-ash, which is how the Velun kings fed it through the Sky Gallows. Crowned, it binds to its wearer, drinks every death done in the wearer's name, and gives back vigour and a heavy, unnerving presence. In the body, Hask aged no further than fifty until his final year. Left unfed, it slowly burns a crowned wearer from within.
- **Indestructible by ordinary means.** Hammers glance off it, and mundane fire only feeds it.

**Origin (DM-confirmed).** It is the burned-down heart of **Yaga-Shura**, the fire giant Bhaalspawn who besieged Saradush in Tethyr during the Bhaalspawn Crisis (1369 DR). Yaga-Shura removed his own heart and kept it wrapped in magical flame. When the heart was extinguished, the fire didn't go out completely: a coal of the Lord of Murder's essence survived inside.

**How it came to Ossmaar.**
- After the siege, a Bhaalist survivor of Yaga-Shura's camp carried the coal east. Around **1398 DR** he sold it to **King Uzhar Velun** of Ossmaar, joined the Pyrewardens, and founded their secret inner circle, the Glad Choir (see pyrewardens).
- The Velun kings kept it in a cold-iron reliquary in the Pyrehouse crypt and called it **the Last Ember**. Each Velun heir was shown it at sixteen. Around 1401 Uzhar raised the Sky Gallows, and the Pyrewardens began the Ash Anointing, to feed it.
- Uzhar's son Tazhar fed it too sparingly, so the Glad Choir went looking for a hungrier master. They found the sellsword Ivoard Hask, told him what lay in the crypt, and opened the Pyrehouse to him on the Night of Cinders (1453).
- Hask had Okton reforge the reliquary's cold iron into the Crown of Ash with the coal at its brow, invented the Ember Law, and let everyone believe the coal was his own.
- Since Bhaal's return to godhood in recent years, the coal has grown hungrier and Ashblood has spread faster, which may be why Hask lost his struggle with it.

**Destroying it.** Only a fire giant's forge, fed with a fire giant's own heart's blood, can unmake what fire giant fire made. Okton can do it, at a cost to himself that grows with his infection. Destruction ends all Ashblood: the Glad die, the Kindled are hollowed, the Touched are freed, and no new Banked can ever be made (see ashblood and order-of-the-banked-fire). It would also carry out the dead king's true last order.

**The option nobody has thought of.** Crown someone with no coal at all, or with a forged one, and break the Ember Law by fiat. Okton could forge a convincing false coal. The Pyrewardens' inner circle would know it was a lie.

**Who took it.** Isine Kettle, the king's cupbearer, obeying his last words. She burned her palm doing it.

## REWORK (v2)
The Coal is the source of Ashblood's overflow. Touching it kills anyone of class level 3 or lower (or CR 1/2 or lower) and infects anyone stronger. If it is destroyed, no new infections are possible, but existing hosts keep the burn and the Quenching still works.
