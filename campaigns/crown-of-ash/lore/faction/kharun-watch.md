# The Kharun Watch ("the Redcollars")
About three hundred watchmen in blackened half-plate and red-brass gorgets, all forged at Okton's forge. Ivoard Hask founded the Watch in 1454 DR. It keeps Kharun's gates, streets, gaol and harbour chain, and until last year it also manned the Sky Gallows. Hired swords like Chadickith pick up Watch work when there's more trouble than there are watchmen.

## Now
On the night the king died, Watch-Captain Halda Barrowe sealed the gates and declared that the Watch would "keep the peace until the Coal chooses." She is now the most courted woman in Ossmaar. All three heirs have sent her gifts, and she has kept every one.

## Key people
- **Watch-Captain Halda Barrowe** (human, 50s): iron-grey braid, broken nose, a voice like gravel. She hired Chadickith for his first paid job on Okton's word. *Wants:* Kharun not to burn, her watchmen paid, and a pension somewhere far away. *Fears:* Lorard's soldiers inside the walls. *Quirk:* chews clove-sticks and points with them, and calls everyone "lad" or "lass" regardless of size, species or rank.
- **Sergeant Wilash Redhand** (half-orc, 40s): Chadickith's old sparring partner, who taught him to fight dirty and "small." Gruff and loyal, with a laugh like a dropped anvil. *Wants:* his younger brother, a soldier in the Ash Companies, alive when this is over.

## For the PC
- The Watch is a source of paid work, gate passes, gaol access and gossip.
- It is the swing vote. Whoever the Watch opens the gates for probably wins without a siege.

## DM secrets
- **Halda commanded the Sky Gallows details for fifteen years** and personally anointed hundreds of the condemned with pyre-ash. She has nightmares she tells no one about, and she is very faintly Touched.
- **Halda is selling the Watch.** She is negotiating with all three heirs and House Mostana at once. Her real price is enough gold to pay the Watch for a year, plus passage across the Lake for herself. If she gets the coal, she will sell it to the highest bidder and call it keeping the peace.
- **Redhand is Lorard's informer.** He passes Watch news to the Lord-Marshal's quartermaster to keep his brother safe. He likes Chadickith, but if it comes down to his brother or the boy, he will sell the boy out.
- If Okton turns against the Watch, the Watch turns against Okton's ward. Halda knows which side of that arithmetic she's on.
