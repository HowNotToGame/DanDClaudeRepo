# The Pyrewardens (the Order of the Last Ember)
Undertakers, executioners' chaplains, keepers of the dead. A realm that executed so many needed someone to make sure the dead stayed dead, so the Pyrewardens burn every corpse in Kharun to keep anything from rising. They are formally sworn to **Kelemvor**, Lord of the Dead, and most of the rank and file are sincere, overworked and quietly decent. The order numbers about sixty wardens, with twice as many acolytes and lay ash-bearers.

## Public role
- They run the Pyrehouse and the Great Pyre, tend the dying in a pauper infirmary, and keep the Long Smoke burning.
- They are custodians of the **Crown of Ash** and of the Ember Law coronation rite.
- They read the last rites at executions. Until the Quiet Year they performed the "Ash Anointing" of the condemned at the Sky Gallows.
- They back **Kitwin**, whom they raised, on the High Pyrewarden's testimony that the king named him.

## Key people
- **High Pyrewarden Tamius Coldwell** (human, 60s): tall and stooped, his face hidden behind a veil of fine steel chain. Speaks only in the liturgical "we." *Wants:* Kitwin crowned with the Coal and the death-tithe restored. *Fears:* a Doomguide from Kelemvor's true church arriving to ask why the Pyrehouse ash makes people smile. *Quirk:* sifts ash through his fingers while he talks and reads it like tea leaves.

## DM secrets
- **The Glad Choir:** an inner circle of about a dozen senior wardens, led by Coldwell. It was founded around 1398 DR by the Bhaalist survivor of Saradush who sold the coal to King Uzhar Velun. The order's formal name, the Last Ember, was once the Choir's name for the coal; ordinary wardens think it means the pyre. The Choir served the Velun kings for half a century. Then, judging the last of them too sparing with his executions, it opened the Pyrehouse to Hask on the Night of Cinders. They know what the Heartcoal is and worship the Lord of Murder through it; Bhaal is the god behind their Kelemvorite masks. They introduced the Ash Anointing around 1401 to spread the coal's taint and keep the Long Smoke burning for the same reason. Once they realised whose son Kitwin was, they deliberately raised him under the smoke.
- **Coldwell invented the deathbed naming.** He reached the king's chamber after Hask was already dead. Isine Kettle can prove it.
- They have agents in the Ashward hunting Isine, and they know she took the coal.
- If they learn of Okton's blood ritual, they will want his blood to make Blood Hunters of their own with no temper at all (see order-of-the-banked-fire).
- **The Ember Rolls:** the Choir's ledger of every anointed death since about 1401, kept in the Pyrehouse crypt. Its early pages bear the seals of Kings Uzhar and Tazhar Velun, which makes it proof of the Velun dynasty's guilt (see oselle-velun).
- The ordinary wardens know nothing. Exposing the Glad Choir would topple Kitwin's claim. It would also leave Kharun, facing a summer of siege and plague, with no one willing to burn its dead.

## REWORK (v2): the Choir's faith
The Choir believes the burn can be **mastered and shared**: their communion of ash, their hymns and the casket of grains (the Last Ember) are a rival to Okton's Quenching. Most of the willing die; the survivors become "Singers" who hold the rage under song. Garn's calm smile was a man holding back a storm.
