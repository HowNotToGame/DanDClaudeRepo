# The Order of the Banked Fire
Its members are called **the Banked**. The player chose the name. Okton's gloss on it: a banked fire is one buried in its own ash, kept alive but held low. That is what a hunter must do with the heat in the blood.

## Player canon
"Okton has also been infected, but to a lesser degree. For a while, he has been working on himself, trying to find a way to defeat the beast. Finally, a ritual has been created. Using his blood, he may now create hunters of our order." Chadickith speaks of "our order," so he belongs to it.

## What it is
An order of Blood Hunters founded by Okton at his forge in Steamside. Its first purpose, the Gallowsbird, is done: Okton killed the roc at the Great Pyre on the night of day 2. Its work now is the burn (see ashblood) and the Heartcoal that spills it.
- **The Quenching** is how a person becomes one of the Banked. The candidate is bled, then quenched in Okton's heated blood, the way steel is quenched in oil. It is agony, and not everyone lives. Okton will not attempt it without a lucid, freely given yes.
- **The class:** the Banked are Blood Hunters of the Order of the Pale Moon, the only order Okton can give. The table uses the Alternate Blood Hunter (laserllama v2.2); reference notes are in Books/alternate-blood-hunter-notes.md. The in-world Order of the Banked Fire is an organisation, not one of the class's own orders.
- **The mechanics** of the Quenching, children, a convert's race, beasts and advancement are held in the campaign's locked rules block, not here.

## Goal and duties
Okton's long goal is to deal with the Heartcoal: destroy it to stop new infections, but only when the Order is strong enough to survive the aftermath. (Destroying it will not end existing infections, and the Quenching will still work.) Until then the Order serves four duties:
- **contain** the burn,
- **shelter** the infected,
- **deny** the Choir,
- **protect** the secret of where the Coal lies.

## Training (about 5 to 13 months)
- **Raw (month 1):** learning to bleed the heat off and to name the rage.
- **Forged (months 2 to 7):** forge discipline: dawn breathing, anvil work, standing a night's watch beside a banked hearth without feeding it, plus martial drill.
- **Tempered (a further 2 to 6 months):** senior members take them out on missions until they can be trusted to hold the line alone.
- **Chadickith** is Raw. When he finishes the Raw month he skips Forged, because Okton has been forging him his whole life, and goes straight on to Tempered missions.

- **How they came:** Chadickith was quenched as he lay dying in the roc fight (night of day 2), the first of Okton's subjects to live. On day 3, in the Collarhouse cellar, Okton quenched five Watchmen raging from the roc's blood. Four were Frenzied and willing; Gwenis was Berserk and converted in a lucid moment. They keep their Watch collars and are carried on the rolls as "ill."
- **Two still lost:** Watchmen **Joris Marsh** and **Gwenet Marlow** are Berserk in the Collarhouse cellar, under Halda's roof. Okton will not quench them without a lucid yes, and sits with them waiting for one.
- **Close to the Order:** Warden **Kaselle Redfern** of the Pyrewardens, who exposed the Choir's dead-house killings and guarded the forge, is travelling to Dunmarrow with Cassmund. **Isine Kettle** wants the Quenching once she is strong enough, in about a week, at the new hall if she can travel. **Ulric Vane**, Dunmarrow's gravekeeper, keeps the graves on the Order's ground.

## Where the Order stands
- **The forge (Kharun):** the Order's first house, with Okton and Chadickith. It now holds a **trial burn ward**: three cots in the vent-heated cellar for one month under Halda's conditions, with Isine as nurse-in-charge. The patients are Branda, Gwenmund and Tam Cobb (see gwenmund-cobb). Gwenmund has declined the Quenching for now; Branda is to be asked, clear-headed, within two days.
- **Dunmarrow:** the razed castle town in the high Cinder Hills, chosen as the Order's hall (see dunmarrow). Gwenis and Kita are there; Hesby, Samson, Cassmund and Kaselle left Kharun on the morning of day 10, separately and an hour apart, and should arrive about five or six days after leaving.
- **Public standing:** to most of Kharun the Order is a rumour. The giant's forge keeps a sick-ward under Watch protection, the Ashward elders know the Coal once lay there and is gone, and the Choir whispers that "the giant makes monsters of the sick." Halda and her command know of the Banked.

## A convert's life
Hesby Bramble is the example. He keeps his Watch collar and post, wakes at four, drills before shift and sleeps little. He carries a small blade for bleeding off the heat. His wife has been told only that he is ill. He cannot drink, because spirits make the heat harder to hold. He feels Okton at a distance like a banked fire in the next room. Even a clean kill takes effort to put down.

**Why they join instead of going home:** there is no normal life to return to. The burn does not leave, a carrier at home is a danger to children and kin, and the Order is the only place that teaches how to hold it. The pull toward Okton keeps them near, and purpose and belonging matter to men who were one bad night from lost. They may leave, but most do not.

## What the Order does
Quarantine and hospice for the infected; hunting infected beasts, the Berserk and the Choir's singers; quiet service to the Watch (Halda's cellar is already an Order hospital); and guarding the Coal's location.

## DM secrets
- **The Heartcoal lies in the Dunmarrow vault,** sealed on day 8 behind an inner iron door and a re-walled plug (see heartcoal). The real route out of Kharun (the Saltgate postern) was known only to the runners (Chadickith, Gwenis, Kita), Okton and Halda.
- **Carrying the Coal is dangerous to a Banked.** Near it Chadickith's rage stirred, his eyes caught red and he heard its pulse, and the Coal surged in him when he killed the Choir scout on the Gallows Road. Kita carried it easily.
- **Every Banked is a host at Stirring,** with the Banked's advantages. A Banked can bleed the rage off with a Vital Sacrifice (see ashblood).
- **The ritual is a prize in itself.** If Lorard learns what it can do, he will want an army of Blood Hunters. The Choir would want Okton's blood to make Banked with no temper at all, and their whisper about "monsters of the sick" suggests they have guessed something. Oselle would want the ritual destroyed, and the giant with it.
- **The Ember Law trap (a v1 idea, still open under v2):** the Coal kindled for Kitwin, who carries the burn. If it would stir for a Banked in the crown too, then by Hask's own law that Banked is a claimant. The Choir would do almost anything to get one into the crown.
- **What it costs Okton** is set in the locked rules block; he has quenched six who lived and one who died, and his craft rank is 3.
- **Beasts:** Chadickith's Giant Owl is not on the level-7 list in the locked rules block, so at level 7 he must choose one of those forms. Flag it when he gets there.
- **Retired v1 material:** the doom clock (Okton sliding toward the gladness), the unspoken charge that the Banked must one day kill him, "no new Banked once the Coal is destroyed", and Okton refusing to quench Chadickith are all void.
