# The Order of the Banked Fire
Its members are called **the Banked**. The player chose the name.

## Player canon
"Okton has also been infected, but to a lesser degree. For a while, he has been working on himself, trying to find a way to defeat the beast. Finally, a ritual has been created. Using his blood, he may now create hunters of our order." Chadickith speaks of "our order," so he belongs to it.

## What it is
A tiny, very new order founded by Okton at his forge in Steamside. Its purpose is to hunt the Gallowsbird and, beyond it, the gladness (Ashblood) the roc carries. Okton's gloss on the name: a banked fire is one buried in its own ash, kept alive but held low. That is what a hunter must do with the warmth in the blood.

- **Founder and blood-source:** Okton.
- **The ritual (the Quenching):** the way a person becomes one of the Banked, and **the way a person becomes a Blood Hunter.** The table uses the Critical Role Blood Hunter class (D&D Beyond, 2020 version), so the Banked are Blood Hunters with class levels. In general terms they are martial hunters who draw power from their own blood, empowering their weapons and turning their blood against their prey at a cost to their own vitality. Use the class as published on D&D Beyond; its rules are not in RoboDM's data. This is third-party content the table approved on top of the strict 2014 rules. The in-world Order of the Banked Fire is an organisation, not one of the class's own subclass "orders," and a member could follow any of them.
- **Chadickith:** sworn to the order as Okton's ward and the roc's oldest living enemy, **but he has not undergone the Quenching** (player-confirmed). He has no Blood Hunter levels. Taking the Quenching later would mean multiclassing into Blood Hunter under the table's multiclassing rules, so check the class's prerequisites.
- **Other members:** by default, nobody has been quenched yet. The ritual is newly finished. See the options below.
- **Public standing:** almost no one knows the order exists. The Watch knows Okton has "been working on something." Rumour in Steamside says the giant has been bleeding into his quench-tubs.
- **What it wants:** the Gallowsbird dead, and after that the gladness ended wherever it is found.

## DM secrets
These are story consequences layered on top of the Blood Hunter class. They never alter class features.

**The rite.** The candidate is bled, then quenched in Okton's blood, heated at the forge. It is agony. A fire giant's blood carries fire and strength, and it also carries Okton's Ashblood.

**Costs and links**
- **Taint:** every one of the Banked is **Touched** from the moment of quenching, though the giant's fire tempers the taint toward fury rather than joy. The temper holds as long as the Banked doesn't kill for pleasure and keeps away from the loose Heartcoal. Joyful killing and time spent near the coal wear it down, using the Ashblood track and the approved DC 10 Wisdom save in ashblood.
- **The price to Okton:** his blood only works when the gladness in it is lit, so before every Quenching he feeds his own warmth at pyres and deathbeds. **Each of the Banked he makes moves Okton a step deeper.** The order grows by spending its founder.
- **Blood calls to blood:** the Banked feel Okton's state at a distance as he nears the edge, which gives them an early warning. If he goes Glad, they will feel the pull to follow.
- **The pull runs both ways:** Glad creatures, the Gallowsbird above all, can feel a Banked who is raging or bleeding for power. A Banked carrying the loose Heartcoal is a bonfire on a dark hill. The coal itself pulls at them like a lodestone: pleasant, then necessary.

**Ties to the Crown of Ash**
- All Ashblood flows from the Heartcoal, so every Banked is a thread back to it.
- **The Ember Law trap:** the coal stirs (a single slow red pulse) in the hands of a Touched Banked. For a **Kindled** Banked it kindles fully. A Banked who slips far enough becomes, by Hask's own law, a rightful claimant to the throne, and the Pyrewardens' Glad Choir would do almost anything to get one into the crown.
- **The ritual is a prize in itself.** If Lorard learns of it, he will want an army of Blood Hunters, and each one would cost Okton a step toward Glad. The Glad Choir would want Okton's blood to make Banked with no temper at all. Oselle would want the ritual destroyed, and the giant with it.
- **Destroying the Heartcoal:** existing Banked keep their Blood Hunter abilities, because the giant's blood is theirs now. But the taint and the links burn out, and **no new Banked can ever be made**, because Okton's blood only works when the gladness in it is lit. The order cannot grow without the thing it hunts. Okton suspects this and hasn't said it.

**Okton's unspoken charge.** The order's last duty, which Okton has not yet spoken: when its founder turns Glad, the Banked kill him. Save this line for the moment it hurts most.

**Chadickith and the Quenching.** Okton has put him off for years, saying he isn't ready. The truth is that Okton can't bear to give his son the gladness he caught from a crown he forged. Offering the Quenching during the campaign, most likely when the coal and the roc arrive together, is a strong decision point. It brings Blood Hunter levels and a share in the order's work, and it costs both Chadickith and Okton. *Flavour:* his Storm Herald (Desert) aura is his own. If he is ever quenched, the giant's fire can be described in it, with no change to the numbers.

**Optional first Banked** (ask the player before adding any companion):
- Sergeant Wilash Redhand of the Watch, who wants the strength to protect his brother but is also Lorard's informer.
- A grandchild of a Hadrun survivor, raised on the story.
- A deserter from the Ash Companies who has seen the gladness in battle.
- *Optional grim note:* Okton's first test of the rite, last winter, on a dying Watch veteran, held for nine days. Then the man died smiling. Okton has refined it since, and no one else knows.

## REWORK (v2, player-approved)
- The **Quenching** is Okton's non-deadly conversion of the overflow into a Blood Hunter's discipline; see `ashblood` for the survival check and the cost to Okton (1 max HP and 1 year per Quenching). The Order aims to grow into a large organization over time as Okton's craft rank rises.
- **Members:** Okton (founder, progenitor: level 18 Pale Moon Blood Hunter), Chadickith (the second ever quenched, and the first to live). First test: a dying Watch veteran who volunteered last winter, held nine days, and died in a rage, teeth bared; Okton refined the rite afterward. Craft rank: 1.
- **Not a doom order:** Okton is stable and disciplined. There is no founder-goes-berserk clock and no unspoken charge to kill him.
- The Banked can bleed rage off with a Vital Sacrifice (drops one stage for 24 hours).
- Rivals: the Choir believes it can master the burn through its own communion of ash and hymns (see `pyrewardens`).

## FINAL (v2.1, player-approved): goals, training, work, life
**Goal.** Okton's long goal is to deal with the Heartcoal: destroy it to stop new infections, but only when the Order is strong enough to survive the aftermath (it will not end existing infections, and the Quenching will still work). Until then the Order serves four duties: **contain** the burn, **shelter** the infected, **deny** the Choir, and **protect** the secret of where the Coal lies.

**Training (about 5 to 13 months).**
- **Raw (month 1):** learning to bleed the heat off and to name the rage.
- **Forged (months 2 to 7):** forge discipline: dawn breathing, anvil work, standing a night's watch beside a banked hearth without feeding it, plus martial drill.
- **Tempered (a further 2 to 6 months):** senior members take them out on missions until they can be trusted to hold the line alone. Ranks are raw, forged, tempered.

**What the Order does.** Quarantine and hospice for the infected; hunting infected beasts, the Berserk and the Choir's singers; quiet service to the Watch (Halda's cellar is already an Order hospital); and guarding the Coal's location.

**A convert's life (Hesby Bramble as the example).** He keeps his Watch collar and post, wakes at four, drills before shift and sleeps little. He carries a small blade for bleeding off the heat. His wife has been told only that he is ill. He cannot drink, because spirits make the heat harder to hold. He feels Okton at a distance like a banked fire in the next room. Even a clean kill takes effort to put down.

**Why they join instead of going home.** There is no normal life to return to: the burn does not leave, a carrier at home is a danger to children and kin, and the Order is the only place that teaches how to hold it. The pull toward Okton keeps them near, and purpose and belonging matter to men who were one bad night from lost. They may leave, but most do not.
**Chadickith's standing.** He is **Raw** (the Quenching was only last night). When he finishes the Raw month he skips the Forged stage entirely, because Okton has been forging him his whole life; he goes straight on to the Tempered missions.

## Beast Form progression (player canon, Pale Moon)
The beast is chosen by the blood and **upgrades permanently** at set levels; there is no going back to an earlier form.
- **Levels 3 to 6:** a beast of CR 1 or lower, chosen at conversion.
- **Level 7:** Giant Vulture, Giant Swan, Giant Raven, Giant Eagle, Hippogriff or Griffin (chosen by personality). Giant Swan and Giant Raven are homebrew (adapt from Giant Vulture and Giant Eagle).
- **Level 13:** Owlbear or Manticore (chosen by fighting style).
- **Level 18:** the Roc (Okton alone is already a Roc, as the progenitor exception).
- **Rule for new Banked:** always ask the player for the convert's **race** and **beast**.
- Note: Chadickith's Giant Owl is not on the level 7 list, so at level 7 he must choose one of those six forms (flag this when he gets there).
