# The Lamplit Council
Ossmaar's interim regency, adopted at the noon council in the Collarhouse long hall on day 10. The vote was three to one: Halda, Lorard and Oselle for, Coldwell against. It governs "until the Heartcoal is broken or found and a king is named." Oselle proposed it; Chadickith, a witness at Halda's table and not a lamp, pressed it into fairer shape.

## The thirteen lamps (adopted)
- **Five guild lamps:** Oilwrights (Master Wilmund Redfern), Alumners (Master Samwyn Quarry), Dyers (Master Tobbton Greenbottle, halfling), Brimstone Brokers (Master Orsikgar Gorunn, dwarf), Moneychangers (Master Bryados Weary, tiefling).
- **Three old-house lamps:** Lady Norelle Whitlock, Lord Junard Ashdown, Wynley Fairweather.
- **Two Watch lamps:** Watch-Captain Halda Barrowe and Captain Marda Ironside.
- **One Marshal's lamp:** Lord-Marshal Lorard Hask, or an officer he names.
- **One Pyrehouse lamp:** a warden named by the High Pyrewarden. Coldwell dissented, and the seat is unfilled until he names someone.
- **One Ashward elder:** chosen by the street, not yet named.

## Voting
Laws pass by lamp-vote. Each lamp carries one vote; a guild's vote may be weighted, up to a cap of two, by the coin it holds in the Keep treasury. The Lady holds the royal seal and a **final reading** on every law: a delay only, once, of up to a tenday. Nine of the thirteen lamps, counted as lamps rather than weighted votes, can overrule it. (She conceded both the delay-only reading and the two-vote cap in public, under Chadickith's questioning.)

## Who holds what
- **Oselle Velun:** the seal, the chancery, the mint, the treasury (about 41,000 gp at the last audit) and the Keep guard (150).
- **Lorard Hask:** command of all arms (the field army of 1,200 plus 340 in the March garrisons), paid from the treasury on time; 300 of his men inside the walls, the rest camped outside. The field army's pay runs about 7,200 gp a month, so the treasury covers roughly five months.
- **The Kharun Watch (Halda):** the gates, the city, the Collarhouse and the gaol.
- **The Pyrehouse:** the rites and the burning of the dead, with no claim to the crown.

## The elder seats (draft for the first sitting)
Chadickith proposed regional elders at the noon council. The first wording came out blunt and Coldwell sneered at it, but Halda, Lorard and Oselle drafted a plan, and a straw vote went three to one in favour (Coldwell against). It is not law until the first sitting votes it.
- **Five new elder lamps:** Lowfields, Cinder Hills, Ossar Shore, South March and **Kharun commons** (Steamside, the Quay and the middle-terrace households that are neither guild nor Ashward). The Ashward keeps its own elder. Total: **eighteen lamps**; the override becomes **thirteen of eighteen**, and a tied vote fails.
- **Election:** each hearth casts one vote at its village moot, with the head of the hearth voting. The 1488 Hearth-Count is the roll, with hill steadings added on the reeve's oath. No guild member may vote or stand in the Kharun commons moot (Oselle's condition).
- **Eligibility:** a hearth-holder resident ten years; no serving officer, priest, warden or guild member; a two-year term, with no consecutive terms.
- **Guarding the vote:** each polling moot is watched by one warden (or the reeve's man where there is no Watch) and one Marshal's sergeant, standing where each can see the other. In the draft the Watch takes Kharun and the Lowfields and Lorard's sergeants take the March, hills and shore. Halda has not yet finally committed the Watch.
- **Interim:** for the first sitting, each region's reeves name an elder pro tem by acclamation; the real vote follows within thirty days.
- **Coldwell's objection:** "hearths can be bought with bread." The answer recorded: bread is also what the Keep's granaries are handing out, and a vote bought with the Ashward's own grain is a vote the Ashward may still keep.
- **Names being talked about** (guesses by Okton and Isine, nothing more): for the Ashward, old **Wynot Northcott**, a retired pit foreman and rag-picker who has buried half his street (he lowered his torch in the crowd at the forge); for Kharun commons, **Rosa Hollis**, a Steamside bathhouse keeper who settles quarrels. They expect Coldwell to push a pious cobbler, **Yarby Cobb**, and Lorard's sergeants a retired veteran.

## The first sitting (noon, day 13)
On the table, as of the evening of day 11:
- The elder seats: the real vote on the eighteen-lamp draft.
- **Kitwin:** Halda required him produced "as a boy, not a banner." Coldwell agreed to bring him on the morning of the first sitting, in the Pyrehouse's presence, and not before.
- **The forge ward:** Halda will ask the lamps to fund the trial burn ward from the Watch's purse, and to allow six cots if the month goes well.
- **Captain Rook's inquiry**, and the Choir's flame-stamped silver.
- **How a king is eventually named** once the Coal is broken or found: not decided. The Coal's whereabouts are officially unknown.

## DM secrets
- Coldwell has not accepted the regency's terms; the Pyrehouse lamp is his lever.
- **The day after the first sitting** (day 14), Marda Ironside gives Halda her sealed record that Lorard is Pellot Rook's son, not Ivoard's, unless Chadickith intervenes. The Marshal's lamp and the army's faith sit on it.
- Oselle's **Saltwater Compact** with House Mostana (brimstone monopoly, a yearly levy of five hundred labourers, three hundred Calishite sell-swords due by galley) and her **ember-glass** for faking a kindling are both unknown to the lamps (see oselle-velun).
- With army pay now guaranteed from the treasury, House Mostana's loans to Lorard matter less, and Seipora Mostana will want a new hook (see house-mostana).
