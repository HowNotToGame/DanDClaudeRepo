The thirteen-lamp interim regency of Ossmaar adopted at the noon council on day 10: seats, voting, the seal's delay-only final reading, powers, who holds what, and what is still unsettled.

**Update (session 1):** ## Adopted terms (day 10, noon, Collarhouse long hall)
Voted three to one (Halda, Lorard, Oselle for; Coldwell against) as a regency until the Heartcoal is broken or found and a king is named.

### The thirteen lamps
- Five guild lamps: Oilwrights (Master Wilmund Redfern), Alumners (Master Samwyn Quarry), Dyers (Master Tobbton Greenbottle, halfling), Brimstone Brokers (Master Orsikgar Gorunn, dwarf), Moneychangers (Master Bryados Weary, tiefling).
- Three old-house lamps: Lady Norelle Whitlock, Lord Junard Ashdown, Wynley Fairweather.
- Two Watch lamps: Watch-Captain Halda Barrowe and Captain Marda Ironside.
- One Marshal's lamp: Lord-Marshal Lorard Hask (or an officer he names).
- One Pyrehouse lamp: a warden named by the High Pyrewarden (Coldwell dissented; seat unfilled until named).
- One Ashward elder: chosen by the street (unnamed).

### Voting
Laws pass by lamp-vote. Each lamp carries one vote; a guild's vote may be weighted up to a cap of two by the coin it holds in the Keep treasury. The Lady holds the royal seal and a final reading on every law: a delay only, once, of up to a tenday. Nine of the thirteen lamps (counted by lamps, not weighted votes) can overrule it.

### Who holds what
- Oselle Velun: the seal, the chancery, the mint, the treasury (about 41,000 gp at the last audit), the Keep guard (150).
- Lorard Hask: command of all arms (field army 1,200 plus 340 March garrisons), paid from the treasury on time; 300 of his men inside the walls, the rest camped outside.
- Kharun Watch (Halda): the gates, the city, the Collarhouse and the gaol.
- Pyrehouse: the rites and the burning of the dead; no claim to the crown.

### Unsettled
- Elder seats (hearth-voted, rotating): tabled to the first sitting in three days; whether the Watch guards the hearths is Halda's to decide.
- Kitwin: Halda requires him produced to the council before the first sitting.
- How a king is eventually named once the Coal is broken or found: not yet decided.
- The Coal's whereabouts: officially unknown.
