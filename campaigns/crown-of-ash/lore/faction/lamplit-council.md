The thirteen-lamp interim regency of Ossmaar adopted at the noon council on day 10: seats, voting, the seal's delay-only final reading, powers, who holds what, and what is still unsettled.

**Update (session 1):** ## Adopted terms (day 10, noon, Collarhouse long hall)
Voted three to one (Halda, Lorard, Oselle for; Coldwell against) as a regency until the Heartcoal is broken or found and a king is named.

### The thirteen lamps
- Five guild lamps: Oilwrights (Master Wilmund Redfern), Alumners (Master Samwyn Quarry), Dyers (Master Tobbton Greenbottle, halfling), Brimstone Brokers (Master Orsikgar Gorunn, dwarf), Moneychangers (Master Bryados Weary, tiefling).
- Three old-house lamps: Lady Norelle Whitlock, Lord Junard Ashdown, Wynley Fairweather.
- Two Watch lamps: Watch-Captain Halda Barrowe and Captain Marda Ironside.
- One Marshal's lamp: Lord-Marshal Lorard Hask (or an officer he names).
- One Pyrehouse lamp: a warden named by the High Pyrewarden (Coldwell dissented; seat unfilled until named).
- One Ashward elder: chosen by the street (unnamed).

### Voting
Laws pass by lamp-vote. Each lamp carries one vote; a guild's vote may be weighted up to a cap of two by the coin it holds in the Keep treasury. The Lady holds the royal seal and a final reading on every law: a delay only, once, of up to a tenday. Nine of the thirteen lamps (counted by lamps, not weighted votes) can overrule it.

### Who holds what
- Oselle Velun: the seal, the chancery, the mint, the treasury (about 41,000 gp at the last audit), the Keep guard (150).
- Lorard Hask: command of all arms (field army 1,200 plus 340 March garrisons), paid from the treasury on time; 300 of his men inside the walls, the rest camped outside.
- Kharun Watch (Halda): the gates, the city, the Collarhouse and the gaol.
- Pyrehouse: the rites and the burning of the dead; no claim to the crown.

### Unsettled
- Elder seats (hearth-voted, rotating): tabled to the first sitting in three days; whether the Watch guards the hearths is Halda's to decide.
- Kitwin: Halda requires him produced to the council before the first sitting.
- How a king is eventually named once the Coal is broken or found: not yet decided.
- The Coal's whereabouts: officially unknown.

**Update (session 1):** ## Elder seats: draft plan (for the first sitting, day 13)
Drafted at the noon council on day 10 by Halda, Lorard and Oselle after Chadickith's proposal. Not law until the first sitting votes it.
- Four new lamps for the regions: Lowfields, Cinder Hills, Ossar Shore, South March. The Ashward keeps its own elder. Total: seventeen lamps.
- Override: twelve of seventeen lamps (about the same share as nine of thirteen).
- Election: each hearth casts one vote at its village moot, the head of the hearth (any head) voting; the 1488 Hearth-Count is the roll, with hill steadings added on the reeve's oath.
- Eligibility: a hearth-holder resident ten years; no serving officer, priest, warden or guild member; two-year term; no consecutive terms.
- Guarding the vote: each polling moot is watched by one warden (or the reeve's man where there is no Watch) and one Marshal's sergeant, who must stand where each can see the other. Halda commits the Watch to Kharun and the Lowfields only; Lorard commits his sergeants to the March, hills and shore.
- Interim: for the first sitting the reeves of each region name one elder pro tem by acclamation; the real vote follows within thirty days.
- Coldwell's objection: hearths can be bought with bread. Answer recorded: bread is also what the Keep's granaries will be handing out; a vote bought with the Ashward's own grain is a vote the Ashward may still keep.

**Update (session 1):** ### Draft amendment (noon council, day 10): a Kharun commons lamp
At Chadickith's question, the draft gains a fifth elder seat: Kharun commons (Steamside, the Quay and the middle-terrace households that are neither guild members nor Ashward), separate from the Ashward's own lamp. The Lowfields already had its own elder in the draft. Result: eighteen lamps; override thirteen of eighteen; a tied vote fails (the law does not pass); the Kharun commons elder is chosen by hearth-vote like the others, with the same eligibility bars. Oselle agreed on condition that guild members may not vote or stand in that moot; Lorard approved; Halda approved (Steamside is Watch-friendly); Coldwell objected that the Ashward is the Pyrehouse's flock, and Halda answered that a flock may have a shepherd and still have a vote. Still a draft until the first sitting.
