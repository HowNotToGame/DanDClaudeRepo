# Okton's hunters (name pending)
*The player may name this order. Update the name and aliases when they do.*

## Player canon
"Okton has also been infected, but to a lesser degree. For a while, he has been working on himself, trying to find a way to defeat the beast. Finally, a ritual has been created. Using his blood, he may now create hunters of our order." Chadickith speaks of "our order," so he belongs to it.

## What it is
A tiny, very new order founded by Okton at his forge in Steamside. Its purpose is to hunt the Gallowsbird, and with it the gladness (Ashblood) the roc carries. Okton has finally finished a ritual that uses his own blood to make hunters: people strengthened by a giant's fire and able to stand against what the infection has made.

- **Founder and blood-source:** Okton.
- **Members:** Chadickith counts himself one. *Whether he has undergone the ritual is not yet decided (to be asked of the player).* There may be one or two candidates or none; see DM secrets.
- **Public standing:** almost no one knows the order exists. The Watch knows Okton has "been working on something." Rumour in Steamside says the giant has been bleeding into his quench-tubs.
- **What it wants:** the Gallowsbird dead. After that, the gladness ended wherever it is found.

## DM secrets
**The ritual (the Quenching).** This is homebrew narrative only. It never changes the PC's 2014 class features or stats.
- The candidate is bled, then "quenched" in Okton's blood heated at the forge. It is agony. A fire giant's blood carries fire and strength, and it also carries Okton's Ashblood.
- **The gift:** a hunter can sense the gladness. Near a Glad creature they feel heat behind the eyes and can tell its rough direction (the Gallowsbird from a few miles off). They can smell Ashblood on a carrier, and that includes heirs and priests. The giant's fire "tempers" the taint, so in a hunter it comes out as fury rather than joy. That is a natural fit for a Barbarian's rage.
- **The price to the hunter:** every hunter is **Touched** from the moment of quenching, though tempered. The temper holds as long as the hunter doesn't kill for pleasure and keeps away from the loose Heartcoal. Joyful killing and time spent near the coal wear it down. Use the Ashblood track and the optional homebrew save in ashblood.
- **The price to Okton:** his blood only works when the gladness in it is lit, so before every Quenching he feeds his own warmth at pyres and deathbeds. **Each hunter made moves Okton a step deeper.** The order grows by spending its founder.
- **Blood calls to blood:** hunters feel Okton's state at a distance as he nears the edge, which gives them an early warning. If he goes Glad, his hunters will feel the pull to follow.
- **The sense cuts both ways:** Glad creatures, the Gallowsbird above all, can feel a hunter too, most strongly when the hunter rages. A raging hunter carrying the Heartcoal is a bonfire on a dark hill.

**Ties to the Crown of Ash**
- All Ashblood flows from the Heartcoal, so every hunter is a thread back to it. Hunters feel the loose coal like a lodestone. For a hunter, carrying it is like holding a small sun: pleasant, then necessary.
- **The Ember Law trap:** the coal stirs (a single slow red pulse) in the hands of a Touched hunter. For a **Kindled** hunter it kindles fully. A hunter who slips far enough becomes, by Hask's own law, a rightful claimant to the throne. The Pyrewardens' Glad Choir would do almost anything to get one into the crown.
- **The ritual is a prize in itself.** If Lorard learns of it, he will want an army of giant-blooded hunters, and each one would cost Okton a step toward Glad. The Glad Choir would want Okton's blood to make its own hunters with no temper at all. Oselle would want the ritual destroyed and the giant with it.
- **Destroying the Heartcoal ends the order's gift.** The sense and the temper vanish, and Touched hunters are freed and grieve the warmth. The PC's class features are untouched. The order would outlive its purpose only as people, not as hunters. Okton suspects this and has not said it.

**Okton's unspoken charge.** The order's last duty, which Okton has not yet spoken: when its founder turns Glad, the hunters kill him. Save this line for the moment it hurts most.

**If Chadickith has undergone the ritual:** he is Touched (tempered). The Gallowsbird can feel him when he rages. The coal pulses for him. Kitwin will notice the ember-shine in his eyes and feel a kinship, which is terrifying. *Flavour fit:* his Storm Herald (Desert) aura can be the giant's fire in his blood burning off him as heat when he rages, with no change to RAW. It scorches friends as well as foes, so the people who stand closest to him pay for his strength.
**If he has not:** Okton has refused, or put it off, because he loves him and knows the cost on both sides. Offering the Quenching during the campaign (most likely once the coal and the roc arrive together) is a strong decision point. It gains Chadickith power and the hunter's sense, and it costs both him and Okton. Pre-ritual, the Desert aura is simply his own; after a Quenching it could flare hotter in description (never in numbers).

**Possible further candidates (optional; ask the player before adding any companion):** Sergeant Wilash Redhand of the Watch, who wants the strength to protect his brother but is also Lorard's informer. A grief-hardened Hadrun survivor. A deserter from the Ash Companies who has seen the gladness in battle.
