# House Mostana
A Calishite merchant house trading brimstone, alum, dyes, silk and debts across the Lake of Steam. Its warehouses crowd Brimstone Quay. Two of its galleys, the *Sixth Daughter* and the *Patient Gull*, ride at anchor in Kharun harbour, waiting.

## Public face
Polite, generous, apolitical. The house extends credit to the Lamp Market, gives alms in the Ashward, and says it wants only "stability and open gates."

## Key people
- **Factor Seipora Mostana** (human, Calishite, 40s): the house's third daughter and its factor in Kharun. Plump, bejewelled and unhurried, always eating candied dates from a silver box and offering them round. When she's thinking she counts under her breath in Calishite. *Wants:* the brimstone monopoly, and to go home to Calimshan with a prize big enough that her mother names her heir. *Fears:* going home empty-handed. *Quirk:* calls every deal a "friendship" and every threat "a misunderstanding between friends."

## What they can offer the PC
Coin, safe passage off a sealed coast, information, and a buyer for anything. Every offer has a price written somewhere in small Calishite script.

## Since the king died
They have not shown their hand. The galleys still ride at anchor. The Lamplit Council regency (day 10) puts the treasury, and the army's pay, under the lamps' eye, with Oselle holding the seal and the Brimstone Brokers' Master Orsikgar Gorunn holding a guild lamp.

## DM secrets
- **They hold the Saltwater Compact** signed by Oselle (see oselle-velun): a brimstone monopoly plus a yearly levy of five hundred labourers. The original is in Seipora's strongbox aboard the *Sixth Daughter*.
- **They supplied the pale tincture** (DMG p.258) that weakened the king.
- **They are backing Lorard too**, lending him gold for his soldiers' pay. Whoever wins owes them.
- **The Heartcoal:** a syl-pasha in Calimshan who collects relics of dead gods has offered a fortune for it. Seipora suspects what it is. She would sell it in a heartbeat and keep it well away from her own skin.
- Three hundred Calishite sell-swords are due by galley within the month of the king's death (for Oselle, under the Compact). When they land, the regency's balance tips: a third armed force inside a city that has only just opened its gates.
- **The regency squeezes them.** Army pay now comes from the Keep treasury, so their loans to Lorard buy less. The Saltwater Compact would have to survive lamp-votes if it ever comes to light. Seipora will want a new hook into whoever matters, and the forge (where everyone now knows the Coal once lay) is an obvious place to start asking.
- **Oselle's ember-glass**, made by the house, is still unused (see oselle-velun).
- **The Heartcoal is out of Kharun** (Oselle knows that much). Seipora does not know where. Her Calimshan buyer is still waiting.
