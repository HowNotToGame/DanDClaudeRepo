## Region
- [Ossmaar](region/ossmaar.md) [regency] — A small, brimstone-rich Border Kingdom on the steaming south shore of the Lake of Steam; its tyrant king is dead, and since day 10 a Lamplit Council governs as regency.

## Location
- [Sky Gallows](location/sky-gallows.md) [abandoned] — A basalt needle three miles south of Kharun where the condemned were chained, ash-anointed, for the roc; empty since the Quiet Year, and its roc is dead.

## Settlement
- [Dunmarrow](settlement/dunmarrow.md) [the Order's hall (founding)] — Razed castle town in the high Cinder Hills, burned by Ivoard Hask's Ash Company in 1463 DR; the Order of the Banked Fire's chosen hall, kept by the gravekeeper Ulric Vane.
- [Hadrun](settlement/hadrun.md) [partly ruined] — Chadickith's half-ruined home village in the Cinder Hills, gutted by the Gallowsbird in 1423 DR; its Roc Bell has been quiet since the bird died.
- [Kharun](settlement/kharun.md) [gates open; regency] — Ossmaar's walled capital on steaming basalt terraces, where kings are crowned and burned and pyre-ash falls like snow; gates open since day 9, seat of the regency; Chadickith grew up here at Okton's forge.

## Faction
- [House Mostana](faction/house-mostana.md) [active] — A Calishite trading house from across the Lake of Steam that buys Ossmaar's brimstone, lends to every heir, and wants the Heartcoal for a buyer in Calimshan.
- [Kharun Watch](faction/kharun-watch.md) [active] — Kharun's city guard, the 'Redcollars' in Okton-forged gorgets: ruled the Coal did not choose Kitwin, opened the gates on Halda's terms on day 9, and holds the city and two lamps under the regency.
- [Lamplit Council](faction/lamplit-council.md) [regency; first sitting day 13] — Ossmaar's interim regency from day 10: the thirteen lamps, the Lady's seal with a delay-only final reading, who holds what, the eighteen-lamp elder-seat draft, and the first sitting on day 13.
- [Order of the Banked Fire](faction/order-of-the-banked-fire.md) [growing] — Okton's order of Blood Hunters, made through his blood rite (the Quenching): Chadickith and five former Watchmen so far; it shelters the burn-sick, denies the Choir and keeps the Coal's secret, with a hall rising at Dunmarrow.
- [Pyrewardens](faction/pyrewardens.md) [active] — Ossmaar's order of undertakers and pyre-priests, sworn to Kelemvor, who burn the dead, keep the Crown of Ash and back Kitwin; their inner circle, the Choir, serves something else.
