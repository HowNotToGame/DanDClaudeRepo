## Region

## Location
- [Sky Gallows](location/sky-gallows.md) [abandoned] — A basalt needle three miles south of Kharun where the condemned were chained, ash-anointed, for the roc; empty since the Quiet Year.

## Settlement
- [Hadrun](settlement/hadrun.md) [partly ruined] — Chadickith's half-ruined home village in the Cinder Hills, gutted by the Gallowsbird in 1423 DR; the survivors' children and grandchildren still ring the Roc Bell.
- [Kharun](settlement/kharun.md) [gates sealed] — Ossmaar's walled capital on steaming basalt terraces, where kings are crowned and burned, pyre-ash falls like snow, and Chadickith grew up at Okton's forge.

## Faction
- [House Mostana](faction/house-mostana.md) [active] — A Calishite trading house from across the Lake of Steam that buys Ossmaar's brimstone, lends to every heir, and wants the Heartcoal for a buyer in Calimshan.
- [Kharun Watch](faction/kharun-watch.md) [active] — Kharun's city guard, the 'Redcollars' in Okton-forged gorgets; they have sealed the gates and sworn to keep the peace until the Coal chooses.
- [Order of the Banked Fire](faction/order-of-the-banked-fire.md) [founding] — Okton's newborn order of Blood Hunters, made through his blood ritual (the Quenching) to destroy the Gallowsbird and the gladness; Chadickith is sworn to it but unquenched.
- [Pyrewardens](faction/pyrewardens.md) [active] — Ossmaar's order of undertakers and pyre-priests, sworn to Kelemvor, who burn the dead, keep the Crown of Ash and back Kitwin; their inner circle serves something else.

## Npc

- [Okton](npc/okton.md) [alive] — Ancient fire giant smith of Steamside who has armoured Kharun's guard for eighty years, drove off the Gallowsbird in 1423, raised Chadickith for 68 years, and founded the Order of the Banked Fire.
