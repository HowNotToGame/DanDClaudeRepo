# The Gallowsbird
A roc. Use the Monster Manual **roc** (MM p.260) with its statistics unchanged. Its wingspan can shadow a whole village square. Its feathers are dull rust and soot, matted at the throat. **Identifying scars:** the beak healed crooked where Okton split it, leaving a dark seam down the upper mandible. The pinions of its left wing grew back pale and brittle where they were scorched. It is the most feared thing in Ossmaar.

## Known history
- First seen over the Gallows Spire around 1460 DR, a few years after the Sky Gallows began. People said the king had tamed it, and he never denied it.
- For three decades it fed on the condemned chained to the Spire. It rarely troubled the countryside while the chains were full.
- Spring 1474 DR: it destroyed most of Hadrun. Okton drove it off, wounding it badly.
- Since the Quiet Year (1490 DR), when the king stopped the executions, it has been seen over the Cinder Hills and the Lowfields, and twice above Kharun's walls.

## Behaviour
Rocs kill to eat. This one kills past eating, scatters its prey, and comes back to circle places where people have died. Shepherds say it screams when a battle starts, from miles away.

## Playing it
At CR 11 it is far beyond a 3rd-level barbarian in a straight fight. Until the party has Okton, the Answer (Okton's spear) or an army behind them, use it as a looming terror, a chase or a set piece to survive, not a fight to win.

## DM secrets
- It is the most deeply infected creature alive: fully **Glad** (see ashblood). For thirty years it ate flesh anointed with pyre-ash from the coal's victims.
- **It can sense the Heartcoal now that the coal is out of the crown.** The crown's cold-iron band muffled the coal; loose, it is a beacon. *Suggested homebrew trigger:* each time a creature dies within 30 ft of the coal, the Gallowsbird learns its direction and rough distance and arrives within 1d4 days, sooner if many die. Violence brings the bird, so every fight the player picks while carrying the coal tightens the noose.
- Its eyrie is in the crater of a dead cone the hill-folk call **Old Wound**, a six-hour climb above Hadrun. The nest is lined with bones, rusted chains torn from the Spire, and trinkets of the condemned: signet rings of the Velun Plot nobles and a dented Watch gorget.
- If the Heartcoal is destroyed, the Gallowsbird dies with it and falls out of the sky wherever it is.
