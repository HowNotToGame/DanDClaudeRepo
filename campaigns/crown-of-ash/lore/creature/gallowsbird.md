# The Gallowsbird
A roc (Monster Manual **roc**, MM p.260), the most feared thing in Ossmaar for nearly ninety years. Its wingspan could shadow a whole village square. Its feathers were dull rust and soot, matted at the throat. **Identifying scars:** the beak healed crooked where Okton split it in 1423, leaving a dark seam down the upper mandible; the pinions of its left wing grew back pale and brittle where they were scorched.

**It is dead.** Okton killed it at the Great Pyre on the night of day 2.

## Known history
- First seen over the Gallows Spire around 1405 DR, a few years after King Uzhar Velun raised the Sky Gallows. People said the Velun kings had tamed it. Later they said the same of Ivoard Hask, and he never denied it.
- For most of a century it fed on the condemned chained to the Spire. It rarely troubled the countryside while the chains were full.
- Spring 1423 DR: after a mass hanging, it destroyed most of Hadrun. Okton drove it off, wounding it badly.
- Since the Quiet Year (1490 DR), when the king stopped the executions, it was seen over the Cinder Hills and the Lowfields, and twice above Kharun's walls.

## Its last night (the Great Pyre, night of day 2)
- It stooped on the dais during Kitwin's crowning, veered away from the boy and struck the pyre instead, then raked Pyre Square for about two hours while the Watch refused to open the Saltgate to the army's carts.
- Chadickith, Lorard's shield-men and the Watch funnelled the crowd into guildhalls, cellars and the Collarhouse, and saved many. About a hundred townsfolk still died in the square, along with Ash Company Sergeant Dace and eleven crew at the bolt-throwers. It killed Chadickith, and Okton's blood rite brought him back.
- Lorard's four light bolt-throwers, Okton's thrown rocks and his greatsword wore it down, and in the eighth round Okton killed it.
- Its blood carried the burn into the wounded. Of about forty, nineteen died within a night and seven were confined raging (see ashblood).
- Up in the hills, the Roc Bell at Hadrun has been quiet ever since, and the charcoal-burners have heard rumours the bird is gone.

## DM secrets
- **It was a Berserk host of the burn** (the v1 "Glad" and joy-in-killing framing is retired). For nearly ninety years it ate flesh anointed with pyre-ash. Once the Coal was loose it was drawn to it; it veered from Kitwin because it sensed the burn in him as kin.
- **When it died,** newly quenched Chadickith felt a flash of heat and fury he first mistook for pleasure, and Okton felt it more strongly and held it down: red eyes, grey veins at the wrist.
- **Its eyrie** is in the crater of a dead cone the hill-folk call **Old Wound**, a six-hour climb above Hadrun. The nest is lined with bones, rusted chains torn from the Spire, and trinkets of the condemned: a Burners' Rising charcoal-burner's iron token, signet rings of the Velun Plot nobles, and a dented Watch gorget.
