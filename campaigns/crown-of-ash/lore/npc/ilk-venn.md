Ilk Venn is a barrel-chested dye-worker, the Blue Hands' best thrower in the Linen. After Dorrik Pell went out on day 12, the dye-workers' money went on him.

- **Day 12, 1491:** in the quarterfinal Joss Marl slipped his grip twice, got behind him, and rode him down for Rosa's three fingers. The rail groaned over the dye-workers' wasted wagers.
