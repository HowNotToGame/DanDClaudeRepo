Ashward woman with the burn at its first stirring; first patient admitted to the forge ward (night of day 10), weighing the Quenching

**Update (session 1):** Correction: the second patient admitted to the forge ward (night of day 10), after her daughter Branda. After Okton explained the risks plainly she chose NOT to be quenched yet: she will learn to hold the heat with Isine and decide for her children when Branda can answer clear-headed. Okton promised the Order will shelter the little ones if she dies. Terrified of losing her place at the pits.
