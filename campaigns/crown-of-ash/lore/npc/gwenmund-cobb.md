# Gwenmund Cobb
An Ashward pit-hand's widow in her thirties, broad-handed and blunt, with the dry pyre-cough of everyone who grew up under the Long Smoke. Her husband was one of the Gallowsbird's wounded. Her daughter **Branda** (14) nursed him and touched his blood, and he died of the burn fever raging and bared-toothed about four days before the ward opened.

## The Cobbs in the forge ward
(See also branda-cobb and tam-cobb.)
- **Branda Cobb** (14) was the ward's first patient (evening, day 10), brought with a warden's seal and her mother's signed consent. She has the burn at Stirring, edging toward Frenzied: grey veins climbing from the wrists, heat in her palms, a temper that snaps and then shames her. On the morning of day 11 she tried to bleed the heat off, failed, broke a water jug against the wall and wept. She told Chadickith about her father's last night: he begged her not to let the heat take her, and she is ashamed she was glad when it ended. Isine says Branda has days, not weeks, unless the heat is bled off, and that she should be asked clear-headed, within two days, whether she wants the Quenching.
- **Gwenmund** was admitted the same night, with the burn at its very first stirring plus ordinary pyre-cough. She had hidden her flush. By midday on day 11 she was a little better: a looser cough and a fading flush.
- **Tam Cobb** (9) touched his father's sickbed linen and showed the first flush; a warden brought him in on day 11, filling the ward's third cot.
- **Mina Cobb** (6) is clear and stays with a neighbour.

## Her choice (evening, day 11)
Okton explained, in plain words and without numbers, what the Quenching risks and what going without it risks. Gwenmund chose **not to be quenched now**. She will learn to hold the heat with Isine, and will decide for her children when Branda can answer clear-headed. She asked what becomes of Tam and Mina if she dies; Okton promised the Order will shelter them.

- **Wants:** her children alive, and her place at the pits kept. Isine told her the Watch will hear it is sickness, not crime.
- **Fears:** dying and leaving Tam and Mina with no one; becoming what her husband became.

## DM secrets
- All three Cobbs in the ward are level 0. If the burn runs its course they die at its end, unless it is held at Stirring or they are quenched (see the locked rules block for the Quenching and for children).
- Chadickith's Insight failed on Branda: what she is holding back about her father's last night, and whether she wants the Order or only wants to live, is still open.
