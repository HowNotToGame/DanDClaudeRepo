Fourteen-year-old pit-hand's daughter, first patient in the forge ward; the burn at its first stage and climbing.

**Update (session 1):** Nursed her father, a roc-wounded pit-hand, and touched his blood; he died raging four days ago and she is ashamed she was glad it ended. Temper snaps then shames her; she tried to bleed the heat off and failed on day 11. Has days, not weeks, without help. If quenched she would be a ward of the Order, not a soldier (race to be asked or rolled d1000 on the Kharun table).
## DM secrets
She is still holding something back about her father's last night.
