# Oselle Velun, the Lady of Lamps
Thirty-five. Tall, fine-boned and dark-haired, with the faintly pointed ears of her elf-blooded Velun grandmother. Always dressed in grey silk and long kid gloves. She took back her mother's name the morning after her father died.

## Claim and power base
- **Claim:** the only child of the king's temple-blessed marriage and the last of House Velun, which ruled Ossmaar for three centuries. Under the old Velun law, which many nobles still swear by, the crown passes through Velun blood.
- **Weakness:** the army despises her as a creature of the old regime, and everyone who cheered the Velun Plot's failure calls her "Hask's daughter."
- **Power:** the Ember Keep, the treasury (about 41,000 gp), the chancery, the mint and the royal seal; a household guard of about 150; the surviving noble houses; the Lamp Market guilds; and Calishite gold through House Mostana.

## Public face
Gracious, reasonable, a reformer. She promised a **Lamplit Council** of guilds and nobles to share rule, an end to the Sky Gallows, forgiveness of crown debts, and grain from the Keep's stores. The Lamp Market adores her.

## Want, fear, voice
- **Wants:** to restore her mother's line and name, and never again to live at a tyrant's whim.
- **Fears:** cages. Being kept, owned and displayed, as her mother was for twenty-seven years. Also fire.
- **Voice and quirk:** speaks softly, so people lean in, and never raises her voice. Always gives a small gift (a ring, a date, a secret) before asking for anything. Opens with "Let us be honest with one another," sometimes before a lie.

## Sympathetic / monstrous
- She grew up a hostage in her father's court, shielding her mother, and her reforms are sincere.
- She will pay for them with other people's lives and liberty, and she has already started (see below).

## What has happened since the king died
- **Session 1:** her man Jorson (wiry, a silver key pin, a blade at the small of his back) offered Chadickith a dawn audience, 200 gp, a safe-conduct and the Keep's infirmary for Isine in exchange for what the old woman carried. Her people took the beaten watcher Garford Whitlock off Chadickith's hands.
- **The Ember Keep audience (morning, day 9):** Chadickith came to the Keep with his greataxe peace-bonded at the gate. She gave her gift first, a full alchemist's kit in a lacquered case, with no conditions. He told her the Coal was "long gone" from Kharun, and that the Watch protects civilians whatever comes. She accepted it, took the Watch as neutral, and sent a message to Halda: the Keep will open its granaries to the Ashward whatever happens. (Halda now sends the grain carts out under Watch guard.)
- Asked why she was glad the Coal was gone, she **showed him the scarred inside of one wrist**, burned to the bone in 1480 pulling her mother from the Crown of Ash, and said it is dangerous and "it feeds." She wants the crown, not the Coal. (The kit, its case and her silver key token are free of curses; Chadickith checked them with Grim Psychometry.)
- **The noon council (day 10):** she laid out the Lamplit Council. Pressed by Chadickith on her quiet veto, she conceded in public that her "final reading" is a delay only, which nine of thirteen lamps can override, and that no guild may carry more than two votes, explaining that the reading exists so no mob or priest can cage her as her mother was caged. She keeps the seal. She resisted Chadickith's regional elder seats until it was settled who chooses them, then accepted hearth-voted elders guarded by the Watch and the Marshal's sergeants together, with a Kharun commons seat closed to guild members. When Isine gave the king's last words, *"Break it,"* she went white, asked whether the king suffered, and declined to ask for Isine.
- **Now (evening, day 11):** she holds the seal under the regency (see lamplit-council), waiting on the first sitting (day 13).

## DM secrets
- **She poisoned her father** with pale tincture (DMG p.258) supplied by House Mostana. For months a bribed taster slipped it into his wine. She believes she killed him. She didn't (see ivoard-hask), but she would do it again. The taster is now at the bottom of the harbour. Isine tasted from the same cups, which is the slow poison in her.
- **What she knows now:** the Coal is out of Kharun, and Halda's wardens opened a door for it. She does not know where, with whom, or about Dunmarrow, and she does not know Rook was exposed as the leak. Chadickith's Insight caught a hairline hesitation when she said she wouldn't need the Coal to be crowned.
- **Her plan for the Ember Law:** House Mostana has made her an alchemical ember-glass that glows red when warmed. She can fake a kindling with it at the rite. She'd prefer the real Coal locked away and fed "humanely" on the already-condemned: a gentler tithe, as she puts it. **Unrevealed.**
- **The Saltwater Compact:** a treaty with House Mostana signed in her own hand. It grants a forty-year monopoly on Ossmaari brimstone and a yearly "debt levy" of five hundred labourers from the Ashward and the pits, shipped across the Lake of Steam to Calishite works. It is slavery under a merchant's seal. In return she gets gold and three hundred Calishite sell-swords, due by galley within the month of the king's death. The original is in Factor Seipora Mostana's strongbox, and a copy is in the Keep's chancery. With laws now passing by lamp-vote, it will be hard to keep hidden for long; she has said nothing of it.
- She blames Okton for her mother's death because he set the coal. She does not know that her own great-grandfather brought the coal to Kharun.
- **Her dynasty's guilt (decided: she does not know; flip it if you want her complicit).**
  - The Heartcoal came to Ossmaar around 1398 DR under her great-grandfather, **King Uzhar Velun**, not with her father.
  - Uzhar raised the Sky Gallows and had the Pyrewardens' Ash Anointing begun in order to feed it. The Velun kings knelt before it in secret for half a century.
  - Uzhar's mass hanging after the Burners' Rising in 1423 sent the roc into the frenzy that destroyed Hadrun. **Her family's executions killed Chadickith's parents.**
  - Her mother Ylia was shown the coal at sixteen, as every Velun heir was, and knew all of this. Ylia's attempt to destroy it in 1480 was atonement, and she never told her daughter.
  - The proof is the **Ember Rolls** in the Pyrehouse crypt, whose early pages carry Uzhar's and Tazhar's seals (see pyrewardens). Coldwell knows.
  - Oselle's promise to end the Sky Gallows is a promise to tear down her own family's work. Learning this could break her, or harden her.
