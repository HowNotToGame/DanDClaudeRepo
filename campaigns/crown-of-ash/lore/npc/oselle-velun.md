# Oselle Velun, the Lady of Lamps
Thirty-five. Tall, fine-boned and dark-haired, with the faintly pointed ears of her elf-blooded Velun grandmother. Always dressed in grey silk and long kid gloves. She took back her mother's name the morning after her father died.

## Claim and power base
- **Claim:** the only child of the king's temple-blessed marriage and the last of House Velun, which ruled Ossmaar for three centuries. Under the old Velun law, which many nobles still swear by, the crown passes through Velun blood.
- **Weakness:** the army despises her as a creature of the old regime, and everyone who cheered the Velun Plot's failure calls her "Hask's daughter."
- **Power:** the Ember Keep, the treasury, the chancery and the royal seal; a household guard of about 150; the surviving noble houses; the Lamp Market guilds; and Calishite gold through House Mostana.

## Public face
Gracious, reasonable, a reformer. She promises a **Lamplit Council** of guilds and nobles to share rule, an end to the Sky Gallows, forgiveness of crown debts, and grain from the Keep's stores (she has already opened one granary in the Ashward). The Lamp Market adores her.

## Want, fear, voice
- **Wants:** to restore her mother's line and name, and never again to live at a tyrant's whim.
- **Fears:** cages. Being kept, owned and displayed, as her mother was for twenty-seven years. Also fire.
- **Voice and quirk:** speaks softly, so people lean in, and never raises her voice. Always gives a small gift (a ring, a date, a secret) before asking for anything. Opens her lies with "Let us be honest with one another."

## Sympathetic / monstrous
- She grew up a hostage in her father's court, shielding her mother, and her reforms are sincere.
- She will pay for them with other people's lives and liberty, and she has already started (see below).

## DM secrets
- **She poisoned her father** with pale tincture (DMG p.258) supplied by House Mostana. For months a bribed taster slipped it into his wine. She believes she killed him. She didn't (see ivoard-hask), but she would do it again. The taster is now at the bottom of the harbour.
- **The Saltwater Compact:** a treaty with House Mostana signed in her own hand. It grants a forty-year monopoly on Ossmaari brimstone and a yearly "debt levy" of five hundred labourers from the Ashward and the pits, shipped across the Lake of Steam to Calishite works. It is slavery under a merchant's seal. In return she gets gold and three hundred Calishite sell-swords, due by galley within the month. The original is in Factor Seipora Mostana's strongbox, and a copy is in the Keep's chancery.
- **Her gloves hide burns** to the bone, from 1480, when she pulled her mother off the Crown of Ash. She knows the coal burns and feeds on deaths, and she will not touch it herself. **She wants someone else to carry it**, and she will offer Chadickith a fortune to keep holding it for her.
- **Her plan for the Ember Law:** House Mostana has made her an alchemical ember-glass that glows red when warmed. She can fake a kindling with it at the rite. She'd prefer the real coal locked away and fed "humanely" on the already-condemned: a gentler tithe, as she puts it.
- She blames Okton for her mother's death because he set the coal. She will know the giant's ward has it before she shows her hand.
- **Her dynasty's guilt (decided: she does not know; flip it if you want her complicit).**
  - The Heartcoal came to Ossmaar around 1398 DR under her great-grandfather, **King Uzhar Velun**, not with her father.
  - Uzhar raised the Sky Gallows and had the Pyrewardens' Ash Anointing begun in order to feed it. The Velun kings knelt before it in secret for half a century.
  - Uzhar's mass hanging after the Burners' Rising in 1423 sent the roc into the frenzy that destroyed Hadrun. **Her family's executions killed Chadickith's parents.**
  - Her mother Ylia was shown the coal at sixteen, as every Velun heir was, and knew all of this. Ylia's attempt to destroy it in 1480 was atonement, and she never told her daughter.
  - The proof is the **Ember Rolls** in the Pyrehouse crypt, whose early pages carry Uzhar's and Tazhar's seals (see pyrewardens). Coldwell knows.
  - Oselle's promise to end the Sky Gallows is a promise to tear down her own family's work. Learning this could break her, or harden her.
