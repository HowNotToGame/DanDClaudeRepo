# Ivoard Hask, the Ash King
King of Ossmaar from 1453 to 1491 DR. Born a nobody somewhere in Tethyr, he captained the Ash Company of sellswords by thirty and was king by thirty-two. Big, bald and soft-voiced, with a long scar through one eyebrow. He never shouted, and he never needed to.

## The reign (as people remember it)
- The last Velun king hired him to break the raiders of the South March. He broke them, then turned on his employer in the **Night of Cinders** (1453 DR). The old palace burned, the Velun king and his sons died, and the Velun princess Ylia became his queen.
- He was crowned with the **Crown of Ash**, and no head but his could make its burning Heartcoal kindle. He then proclaimed the **Ember Law**: no one wears the crown unless the Coal kindles for them.
- **What people credit him with:** walls for Kharun, the Watch, safe roads, an end to raiding, honest weights in the markets, brimstone wealth.
- **What they whisper about:** the **Sky Gallows**, built by the Velun kings but made into a machine by Hask, where hundreds or thousands (it depends who's counting) were chained for the roc. Purges of the old nobility, above all the Velun Plot of 1474. The Thornwater Tithe. A queen who died of "fever" in 1480.
- **The Quiet Year:** in Flamerule 1490 he suddenly stopped every execution in the realm and was seen less and less. People assumed he was ill.

## Family
- **Lorard** (born 1450 DR), son of his camp-wife Mara, a sellsword quartermaster (died 1489).
- **Oselle** (born 1456 DR), daughter of Queen Ylia Velun (died 1480).
- **Kitwin** (born 1472 DR), never acknowledged, son of a Pyrehouse acolyte named Doret (died 1473).

## How he died (public story)
He died in his sleep on the night of 26 Eleint 1491 DR, "taken by the fever of age." The High Pyrewarden says that with his last breath he named Kitwin his heir. When the Pyrewardens lifted the crown from his pillow at dawn, the Heartcoal was gone. He lay in state in the Pyrehouse and burned on the Great Pyre on Highharvestide.

## Since his death
- **His last words are out.** At the noon council on day 10, his cupbearer Isine Kettle testified before Halda, Lorard, Oselle and Coldwell that the king died in the night, that Coldwell reached the chamber only afterwards, and that his last words were *"Break it. Not them, none of them. Break it."* He named no heir. Lorard repeated "Break it?" and fell silent; Oselle went white and asked only whether he suffered. Coldwell called Isine a liar but let his demand for her drop.
- **No king.** The same council adopted the **Lamplit Council** as a regency "until the Coal is broken or found and a king is named" (see lamplit-council). The Ember Law stands, unsatisfied.
- **The Sky Gallows** he made infamous has stood empty since the Quiet Year, and the Gallowsbird that fed there is dead (night of day 2).

## DM secrets
- **The truth of his death: the Heartcoal killed him.** He wore it for 38 years and fed it a death-tithe through the Sky Gallows, and it kept him hale far beyond his years. By 1490 he understood what it was, what it was making of him, and what Kitwin (for whom it had kindled) would become. The Quiet Year was his attempt to starve it. Starved, it burned him from the inside, leaving blackened lungs and a heart cooked in his chest. The Pyrewardens' "fever of age" covers this up.
- **Oselle's poison** (pale tincture, DMG p.258, supplied by House Mostana) had been weakening him for months. She believes she killed him. She didn't, though she hastened his death. His cupbearer Isine tasted from the same cups some nights, which is the slow poison Chadickith found in her.
- **His true last words** were heard only by his cupbearer, Isine Kettle: *"Break it. Not them, none of them. Break it."* He never named Kitwin. High Pyrewarden Tamius Coldwell reached the chamber after the king was dead and invented the deathbed designation.
- **What he knew about Lorard:** that the boy was not his blood (see lorard-hask). He acknowledged him anyway, out of love for Mara, and never told anyone. It may be the only wholly decent thing he ever did. Since day 11, Chadickith, Captain Marda Ironside and Captain Hesard Rook know it too; Halda does not yet.
- **Where the coal came from:** not from Hask. The Velun kings had kept it hidden in the Pyrehouse crypt since about 1398 (see heartcoal). The Pyrewardens' Glad Choir judged the last Velun king too sparing with his executions, so they told the sellsword about it and opened the Pyrehouse to him on the Night of Cinders. Hask let everyone believe the coal was his own.
- **Ylia's death (1480):** the queen knew her own Velun forebears had fed the coal for half a century. She tried to pry it out of the crown and destroy it. It burned her, and she died of the burns over nine days. Hask called it fever and sent the physicians who knew the truth to the Spire. Oselle was there and burned her hands pulling her mother away.
