Gorm Halfhand (born about 1436) is a half-orc porter on Brimstone Quay who served eight years in the Ash Companies on the South March before he lost two fingers of his left hand at Fort Vell. Grey-tusked and broad, he hums Shaaran riding songs, and he is the Hawk's grand old man in Kharun.

- **The Hawk:** he learned it from Shaaran horse-traders at Fort Vell: an open start, falls on an elbow, knee or back, leg grabs, and the hawk dance, arms spread like wings.
- **His Opens:** he won three (1462, 1463 and 1465), dancing on the chalk after every one. Wenna made him buy a round of cider for taunting each time, and he bought it gladly.
- **His part in the Seven Roots slate:** he gave Rosa the Hawk's rules for the Rope, and he insisted there be no weight classes: "the hawk doesn't ask the hare how much it weighs."
- **Manner:** slow, courteous and amused. He calls everyone "rider" and claps once, softly, for a good throw by anyone.

## DM secrets
- A few of Lorard's soldiers inside the walls wrestle the Hawk, and they have asked him whether soldiers may enter in 1492. He hasn't told Rosa yet. He remembers the Sergeant's Fall, and he isn't sure what he'd say to her.
