Dorrik Pell is a journeyman at the Steamside dye-works, blue to the elbows from the vats, barrel-built and loud. Every year since he was sixteen he has boasted that he can throw anything on two legs.

- **How he wrestles:** the Linen with the Blue Hands, and the Open for the rail.
- **The Bellow:** he won it three years running (1487–1489) before the Quiet Year stopped it, and he wants it back.
- **Day 12, 1491:**
  - He was drawn against Chadickith in the quarterfinal, at 6 to 1.
  - He charged and lost the grip, was driven over the chalk, and sat down on the rope.
  - He laughed, wheezed and slapped the ash twice: "Giant's boy! Hold the gate, I'll buy you a cider after!" He bought two.
- **Manner:** good-humoured. He grins, wheezes when winded, and yields without sulking. He's friends with Ilk Venn.
