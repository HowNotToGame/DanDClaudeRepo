Hesta Crall (born 1441), called Big Hesta, was a paid hand in the guild brimstone pits for thirty years and retired at 45 when her left knee went. She is broad across the shoulders and thick through the middle, with grey-streaked braids pulled back, forearms like ship's cable and a missing front tooth.

- **The Open's great champion:** 22 titles from 1466 to 1490 (1466, 1468–1475, 1477–1481 and 1483–1490, the last in silence).
  - She lost the 1476 final to Marda Ironside (Out).
  - She lost the 1482 final to Tess Aldin (Down). Nobody put her on her back again until 1491.
  - She lost the 1491 semifinal to Chadickith (Down).
  - She lifted the Old Woman in 1478, the last to do it.
- **How she wrestles:** a Catch wrestler with a Push stance. She plants her right foot, waits, and drives people out; she beat Tess in five finals, all by Out. She is silent before a bout and watches her next opponent all morning.
- **Day 12:**
  - She watched Chadickith all morning and beat Corra Nettle in the quarterfinal.
  - In the semifinal he suplexed and pinned her. She let her fistful of ash fall rather than foul, then offered it to him: "That's for you, to keep. Don't say I never gave you anything."
  - She took 2 crowns and a cider.
- **Voice:** few words and a wheeze of a laugh. She calls people by their trade ("pit-hand", "dyer", "giant's boy").

## DM secrets
- **Her tells**, planted in the day-12 narration for the player to catch:
  - her left knee is old and wrapped, so she keeps her weight off it and wrestles from her right, and fighting her left side or forcing her to plant on it costs her;
  - she tongues the gap in her teeth just before she commits to a lunge, which gives a half-second's warning.
- **The children:** she has raised her dead sister's five children since 1484. The purse has fed them for years, and she has sold every Factor's Cup House Mostana sent her. Losing this year hurts.
- **Her last Open:** the knee is going, this was probably her last Open, and she knows it. She'd be glad of honest work for a strong back that can't kneel. She'd like to be named the champion's challenger once, for the honour.
