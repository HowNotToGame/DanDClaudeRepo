Teo Varrow (born 1436), a porter on Brimstone Quay, is the son of a Calishite mother and a Kharun porter. He is grey, bow-legged and soft-spoken, with cauliflower ears and a habit of lying down on any floor to show you something.

- **The Knot:**
  - He learned it from Calishite galley-hands and caravan guards in the 1450s and 1460s.
  - When the Strangle (1467) got the Knot barred from the Open, he kept it alive in the back of a Mostana-leased warehouse on the Quay.
  - He teaches it there to anyone who brings a cinder.
- **1486:** he reached the Open final by fighting off his back. Hesta sat on him with his legs wrapped round her, and Rosa counted three: the Varrow ruling.
- **His pupils:** Joss Marl, a dozen Quay porters, and the odd Redcollar who won't admit it.
- **On teaching Chadickith:** he would. "Big men learn the Knot fastest. They've never had to."

## DM secrets
- **The house hears what he hears.** The warehouse foreman pays him an ember a tenday to keep his ears open, and what he hears goes to Factor Seipora's people "as friendship".
- **If Chadickith trains with him,** the house will know what the forge talks about on the mats.

**Update (session 2):** DM secrets addendum: Teo lobbied Rosa for twenty years for a bout where the guard is honoured, and wrote most of the Ash ground of her Seven Roots slate (the Long Open), including the two lists of locks. He is the one who decides who counts as knotted.
