Old retired pit foreman and rag-picker of the Ashward, the man who lowered his torch at Okton's door and seized the agitator; Okton and Isine's guess for the Ashward elder.

**Update (session 1):** Thirty years a pit foreman, buried half his street.

**Update (session 2):** **Vent Games:** 'the Anvil', eight-time champion of the Open (1454-1456, 1458-1461, 1464) and the Ashward's greatest Catch wrestler. He lifted the Old Woman stone in 1459. In 1464 he drove the raging Ash Company sergeant Brannoc Hale over the chalk and sat on him until his own men dragged him off (the Sergeant's Fall). See the-vent-games.

**Update (session 2):** **In the pit:** human (Tethyrian), 67 (born 1424). Once huge, now stooped, still thick through the shoulders, with hands like shovels and the Ashward's pyre-cough. He has wrestled the Catch since boyhood and has had sixty years of the Belt from the hill-folk in his street, but he has never wrestled long. Pit numbers: STR 14, DEX 10, CON 11; Athletics +6 (a lifetime's expertise), Acrobatics +0. Old bones: after his second exchange of any bout he has disadvantage on Athletics checks until he rests. He would ask for the Old Rope, or the Catch alone.
