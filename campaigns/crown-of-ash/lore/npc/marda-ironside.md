Watch captain, Halda's most trusted second, holds the cells and the guard; sits as a Watch lamp on the Lamplit Council.

**Update (session 1):** Stood with Halda at the first hearing, took Isine to and from the council, guards the ward's lane door with two wardens, and wrote down Rook's talk with Lorard. Holds the sealed parentage record in her own strongbox for three days at Chadickith's request; she will not lie to Halda if asked.

**Update (session 2):** **Vent Games:** won the 1476 Open as a 20-year-old Redcollar, wrestling the Watch's upper-body style (the Gorget), by driving Big Hesta over the chalk in the final. She is the only Watch officer ever to win it; her spoon hangs on the Hollis Spoon Wall. See the-vent-games.

**Update (session 2):** **In the pit:** human (Chondathan stock), 35 (born 1456), about 5 ft 10, rangy and long-armed, dark hair cropped short with a white streak at the left temple, and a nose broken in the 1476 final. She has run the Collarhouse yard's recruit bouts for fifteen years and wrestles the Gorget, with some Linen; she has never wrestled the Ash or the Knot. After her 1476 title she entered once more (1477, beaten by Hesta in the semifinal) and then the captaincy took her. Pit numbers: STR 15, DEX 14, CON 14; Athletics +6, Acrobatics +5.

**Update (session 2):** **Champion's Grudge (day 12):** Chadickith named her as his challenger for the first bout of the 1492 Vent Games, and Rosa chalked it on the slate's top line. Marda chooses the form (long, short, or one root); the news reaches the Collarhouse before the curfew bell.
