# Halda Barrowe, Watch-Captain of Kharun
Human, in her fifties: an iron-grey braid, a broken nose, a voice like gravel. She commands the Kharun Watch (see kharun-watch) from the Collarhouse by the Saltgate. She has hired Chadickith for odd jobs for years on Okton's word, and still talks to the 76-year-old like a promising recruit.

- **Wants:** Kharun not to burn, her watchmen paid, and a pension somewhere far away.
- **Fears:** soldiers loose inside the walls; a priest or a mob deciding who rules.
- **Quirk:** chews clove-sticks and points with them. Calls everyone "lad" or "lass" regardless of size, species or rank.

## What she has done since the king died
- **The oath:** on the night the king died she sealed the gates and swore the Watch would keep the peace "until the Coal chooses."
- **Session 1 and day 2:** she held Brother Peret, the Choir knife-man Chadickith brought her, until the dusk bell as she had warned, then let Coldwell take him under Watch escort. She issued Isine a Saltgate pass and hired Chadickith for her rope line at the Great Pyre. During the Gallowsbird's attack she refused to open the Saltgate to Lorard's carts, letting only the bolt-throwers' crews through the wicket on foot.
- **Day 3:** shown the Choir's ash wafers and the brothers Vesan and Wyn, she put them before her six captains at noon. The captains ruled that the Coal did **not** choose Kitwin, and the gates stayed sealed. She quarantined the seven Watchmen raging from the roc's blood in the Collarhouse cellar and let Okton treat them.
- **Days 4 to 5:** she found that someone on her own staff was leaking. After Chadickith's canary trap pointed at Captain Hesard Rook, she posted only her own wardens on the Saltgate postern for three nights and cleared the wall-walk, letting the Order's real run through.
- **Day 9:** woken at 1 a.m. by a giant owl in her yard, she agreed to open the gates on terms: the Watch keeps the Collarhouse, the Pyrehouse and the Ashward's safety; Lorard's men enter by column, with no pillage. At the dawn war-table Rook's orderly Fenia swore Rook had him listening at doors for coin. Halda relieved Rook and confined him, and the captains voted four to one to open the gates at noon. That night, after a rooftop crossbowman tried to silence the captured agitator Ashlo, she doubled the cell guard under Captain Marda Ironside.
- **Day 10:** at dawn she brought Ashlo, Fenia and the Choir's flame-stamped silver to the forge and broke Lorard's search. She chaired the noon council. She refused to hand Isine to the Pyrehouse ("no one is given up at my table, before or after"). She voted for the Lamplit Council as regency and holds a Watch lamp on it. She demanded Kitwin be produced "as a boy, not a banner". In the elder-seat draft she offered the Watch to guard the polling in Kharun and the Lowfields, but she has not yet committed it. She allowed a **trial burn ward** at the forge (three cots, one month): every patient comes with a warden, a signed family consent and her seal, a daily written report goes to her, and no one is quenched without a free yes witnessed by a warden. She keeps the two worst cases, Joris Marsh and Gwenet Marlow, in her own cellar. She will ask the first sitting to fund the ward from the Watch's purse, and to allow six cots if the trial goes well.
- **Day 11:** she took the Choir's ash line and flame-coin from the ward's door as evidence of intimidating the sick. She posted a Watch notice under her seal in the Ashward saying the ward is under Watch protection and that frightening the sick is a crime, and added two wardens to the lane door. Coldwell has agreed to produce Kitwin on the morning of the first sitting. She allowed Rook, who refuses to answer the inquiry, his request to see the Lord-Marshal alone.

## DM secrets
- **She commanded the Sky Gallows details for fifteen years** and personally anointed hundreds of the condemned with pyre-ash. She has nightmares she tells no one about. (The v1 note that this left her faintly Touched is retired: ash carries no burn under the v2 rules.)
- **The old v1 secret, not seen in play:** she was negotiating with all three heirs and House Mostana at once, her price being a year's pay for the Watch and passage across the Lake for herself. Her conduct since day 1 has been straight. If you keep it, play it as an old plan she has let lapse, or one Mostana will try to revive.
- She has seen the real Heartcoal (day 3) and knows it left Kharun by the Saltgate postern. Whether she was told it went to Dunmarrow is unclear: the memory of the redoubt choice says Hesby carried her "the whole message" before the run. Treat the destination as known only to the Order unless you decide otherwise.
- **She does not know Lorard's parentage.** Marda Ironside holds the record of Rook's confession sealed and will give it to Halda on day 14 unless Chadickith changes that (see lorard-hask).
