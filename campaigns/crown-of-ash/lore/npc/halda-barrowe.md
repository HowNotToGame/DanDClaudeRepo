Watch-Captain of Kharun, 50s, iron-grey braid, broken nose, gravel voice, chews clove-sticks; chair of the Lamplit Council's sittings and Chadickith's steadiest ally.

**Update (session 1):** Hired Chadickith for odd jobs for years on Okton's word. Sealed the gates the night the king died ('keep the peace until the Coal chooses'), let Peret go to Coldwell, was shown the real Heartcoal and kept the secret, held the war-table that exposed Rook, opened the gates at noon on day 9 on terms, and chairs the regency. Granted the forge ward a one-month trial (three cots) with conditions. Demands Kitwin be produced to the council before the first sitting.
## DM secrets
She commanded the Sky Gallows details for fifteen years and anointed hundreds of the condemned with pyre-ash; she has nightmares and is faintly Touched. She was negotiating her own price with every heir. Marda holds the sealed parentage record for her; she learns Lorard's secret on day 14 unless the player changes it.
