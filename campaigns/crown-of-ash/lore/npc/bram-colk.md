Bram Colk is a stoker at the Third Furnace, the big alum-boiling furnace by the dye-works. He has a chest like a barrel, soot-ringed forearms and a belly that sways when he laughs. Before a bout he claps once, and the rail claps with him.

- **How he wrestles:** a Push man, with everything on the charge.
- **Day 12, 1491:** he lost his quarterfinal to Tess Aldin in about the time it takes to boil an egg: a sidestep, a heel-hook trip and a pin. He laughed through the ash and clasped wrists with her.
