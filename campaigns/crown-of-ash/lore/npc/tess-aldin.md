Tess Aldin (born 1457) is a paid hand in the guild brimstone pits on the Ossar Shore. She is lean and hard-jawed, with her scalp shaved to a blue stubble and her knuckles wrapped in grey linen, and she wrestles barefoot with her linen tied at the knee.

- **The Open:**
  - She was champion in 1482, at 25, by pinning Big Hesta in the final.
  - She was runner-up to Hesta in five finals after that, each lost by Out.
  - In 1491 she was runner-up to Chadickith, lost by Down. She cried "Ash" as the third finger came down.
- **How she wrestles:**
  - She tests the ash with her toes before she moves, and keeps her weight low.
  - She sidesteps a charge and hooks the leading ankle with her bare heel. That is how she put Bram Colk on his face.
  - She goes for the 2-on-1 and the outside trip.
  - Nobody had pinned her since she was a girl, until Chadickith.
- **Manner:** quiet. She doesn't raise her arms when she wins, she helps the loser up, and she watches the slate rather than the crowd. "Next year, giant's boy."

## DM secrets
- **Her brother:** she shaved her head in 1488, the day her younger brother Aldo went to the Spire and the Gallowsbird took him, and she has kept it shaved since. She's quietly glad the forge killed the bird, and would stand with the Order if asked plainly.
- **If named as challenger:** if Chadickith names her for next year, she'll call **the Push**. Her sidestep is deadliest there, and one Out ends it.
