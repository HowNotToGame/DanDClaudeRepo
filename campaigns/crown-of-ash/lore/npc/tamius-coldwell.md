# Tamius Coldwell, High Pyrewarden
Human, in his sixties: tall and stooped, his face hidden behind a veil of fine steel chain. He speaks only in the liturgical "we", and sifts ash through his fingers while he talks, reading it like tea leaves. He heads the Pyrewardens (see pyrewardens), keeps the Crown of Ash, and backs Kitwin, whom the Pyrehouse raised.

- **Wants (as he says it):** Kitwin crowned with the Coal, and the Pyrehouse's ancient rites honoured.
- **Fears:** a Doomguide from Kelemvor's true church arriving to ask questions.

## What he has done since the king died
- **The deathbed claim:** he testifies that the dying king named Kitwin his heir with his last breath.
- **Session 1 and day 2:** he demanded the Choir knife-man Peret back from the Watch by sunup, and took him at the dusk bell. At the Great Pyre rite he set the Crown of Ash, its socket packed with grey grains, on kneeling Kitwin, and the crown kindled after a killing on the dais.
- **Day 3:** at the dawn bell he proclaimed Kitwin "kindled." At noon the Watch's captains heard two Choir brothers swear the crown was lit with grains, and publicly refused his claim.
- **Day 9, dusk:** he lit the Long Smoke green-grey and proclaimed from the Pyrehouse steps that "the giant's smith and the Watch have stolen the Coal." An Ashward crowd led by Choir singers marched on Okton's forge, and turned against the Choir there.
- **The noon council (day 10):** he demanded the Coal's return and Kitwin's crowning. He denied knowing any servant, called Isine Kettle a thief and a liar in the giant's pay, and demanded her seizure; the demand died without a vote after her testimony. He voted alone against the Lamplit Council regency, sneered at "a forge-boy lecturing the lamps", and objected to hearth-voted elders ("hearths can be bought with bread"). Under the regency the Pyrehouse keeps the rites but no claim to the crown, and names one warden to a lamp. He has not agreed to the terms.
- **Day 11:** he agreed to produce Kitwin to the council on the morning of the first sitting (day 13), in the Pyrehouse's presence, and not before.

## DM secrets
- **He leads the Glad Choir,** the Pyrewardens' inner circle, about eleven strong, with Sister Ilsa as cantor (see pyrewardens). He serves Bhaal behind Kelemvor's mask.
- **He invented the deathbed naming.** He reached the king's chamber after Hask was already dead. Isine's testimony has made the lie known to the council's leaders.
- **The Last Ember:** he wears the key to the iron casket of grains (pyre-ash and dust of the Heartcoal) in the Pyrehouse crypt below the sanctum. The Choir's wafers come from it, and so did the grains that lit the crown.
- He was angry that the Choir's knife missed Isine at the pyre.
- **His hidden hands:** a grey-shawled woman of the Choir ran the signal lamp in the chandler's loft by the dye-works, paid Rook's orderly, and approached Lorard's quartermaster Unara with silver and a promise to open the Pyrehouse to the Marshal. The same flame-stamped silver paid the agitator Ashlo to carry the first torch at the forge. Who sent the rooftop crossbowman who tried to silence Ashlo is not settled; the Choir is the obvious answer. On the night of day 10 someone laid the ash line and the flame-coin at the ward's door.
- **He swore to Captain Hesard Rook** that the Pyrehouse would back Lorard for the crown if the Coal were recovered, while proclaiming Kitwin. He plays every side for the Coal.
- **The flame-stamped coins** each carry a thread of ash-mark tied to the Last Ember casket (Grim Psychometry, day 10), so the Choir can feel roughly where its coins are. Halda holds Ashlo's silver and the ward-door coin in the Collarhouse; one more sits at Okton's forge, where Halda left it with Okton (the coin Chadickith read on day 10).
- **The Ember Rolls,** the Choir's ledger of every anointed death since about 1401, lie in the crypt. Their early pages carry the Velun kings' seals (see oselle-velun). He knows what they prove.
