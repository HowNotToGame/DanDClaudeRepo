High Pyrewarden, 60s, tall and stooped behind a veil of steel chain; speaks only as 'we' and sifts ash through his fingers; leader of the Glad Choir.

**Update (session 1):** Champions Kitwin's crowning; demanded the Coal's return and Isine's seizure at the noon council and dissented from the regency. Agreed to produce Kitwin on the morning of the first sitting. Released Peret to himself on day 3. His men marked the ward's lane door with an ash line and a flame-coin.
## DM secrets
He invented the deathbed naming: he reached the king's chamber after Ivoard was dead (Isine can prove it). He leads the Glad Choir, which worships through the Heartcoal; his silver is tethered to the casket in the crypt.
