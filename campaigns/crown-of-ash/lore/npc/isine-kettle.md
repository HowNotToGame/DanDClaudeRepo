# Isine Kettle
Fifty-eight, small and grey, with a scullion's forearms and a courtier's posture. For thirty years she was King Ivoard's cupbearer and body-servant. She tasted his wine, dressed his wounds and kept his silences. On the night he died she vanished from the Ember Keep. Her left palm is wrapped over a deep burn shaped like a ridged lump of coal.

- **Wants:** to obey her king's last order, and then to sleep. Now also to heal: she would rather be a surgeon than a soldier.
- **Fears:** the Pyrewardens. She saw High Pyrewarden Coldwell arrive at the deathbed after the king was already gone.
- **Voice and quirk:** clipped and formal, keeping a servant's courtesy even when terrified ("If it please you, ser"). She folds and refolds any cloth in her hands.

## What has happened to her
- **Highharvestide (session 1):** a Choir knife cut her at the pyre. Chadickith carried her to the Watch's triage tent, where Sergeant Wilash Redhand guarded her.
- **Day 2:** Halda issued her a Saltgate pass, but the Gallowsbird closed the gates before she could use it. She stayed to nurse the roc's wounded at the Collarhouse triage tent, washing wounds with brine and keeping the raging apart. On day 3 she gave the pass to Chadickith to hold for someone who can leave.
- **Day 3:** she came to Okton's forge, where he began teaching her to bleed off the heat in the cellar, away from the hearth.
- **Day 9, dusk:** the Ashward elders who searched the forge found her alive in the cellar. She told them the Choir stabbed her at the pyre. It turned the street against the Choir's story of a stolen Coal.
- **Day 10, noon council:** she testified before Halda, Lorard, Oselle and Coldwell. She said the king died in the night and Coldwell reached the chamber only afterwards. She gave his last words, *"Break it. Not them, none of them. Break it."*, and said he never named Kitwin or anyone. While the wardens fetched water she pried the Heartcoal from the crown with the king's own knife and fled. Coldwell called her a thief and a liar in the giant's pay and demanded her seizure. Halda refused ("no one is given up at my table"). Lorard called her "the king's hand", not a thief. Oselle, white, asked only whether the king suffered ("He was tired. He said it like a man putting down something heavy"). Coldwell's demand died without a vote.
- **Now (evening, day 11):** she lives at the forge under Watch protection, with two guards at the door. She is **nurse-in-charge of the forge ward**: three cots, filled by Branda, Gwenmund and Tam Cobb (see gwenmund-cobb). She asked Okton what "break it" means and whether it can be done, and asked to be told when.

## Her health
- **The burn:** she carries it at Stirring, under control. She has bled the heat off with Okton's teaching and no longer hears the Coal's pulse since it left the city. It brings relief, and a strange emptiness.
- **Poison:** Chadickith's Medicine found a slow mineral poison given in small doses over weeks. It stopped when she left the Keep's table, and by day 10 she was about two-thirds recovered: tremor nearly gone, fever gone, appetite returning. Who poisoned her is not known to the party.
- **The Quenching:** she wants it, but asked to wait until she can stand a full day without shaking and the poison is gone, because she does not want to die on a cot. Okton agreed: in about a week, at the new hall if she can travel, with her yes freely given then.

## DM secrets
- **The poison is the king's own wine.** Oselle's bribed taster slipped pale tincture (DMG p.258) into Ivoard's cups, and Isine tasted from the same cups some nights (see oselle-velun and ivoard-hask). She knows the taster was bribed but has not connected it to her own illness.
- **She is an anomaly.** She touched the Heartcoal barehanded and lived, because she was willing: obeying her king's last order, and meaning it. She is level 0, so if the burn ever runs its full course it kills her at the end (see the locked rules block); holding it at Stirring, or the Quenching, are her ways out.
- In 1453 she was a young kitchen maid in the Keep and remembers the giant who made the crown. That is why she ran toward Okton.
- Chadickith's Insight failed on whether she misses the Coal's pulse more than she lets on, or wants it back.
