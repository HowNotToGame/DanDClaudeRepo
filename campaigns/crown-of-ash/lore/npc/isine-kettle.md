# Isine Kettle
Fifty-eight, small and grey, with a scullion's forearms and a courtier's posture. For thirty years she was King Ivoard's cupbearer and body-servant. She tasted his wine, dressed his wounds and kept his silences. On the night he died she vanished from the Ember Keep. Her left palm is wrapped in rags over a fresh, deep burn shaped like a ridged lump of coal.

- **Wants:** to obey her king's last order, and then to sleep.
- **Fears:** the Pyrewardens. She saw High Pyrewarden Coldwell arrive at the deathbed after the king was already gone.
- **Voice and quirk:** clipped and formal, keeping a servant's courtesy even when terrified ("If it please you, ser"). She folds and refolds any cloth in her hands.

## DM secrets
- She heard the king's true last words: *"Break it. Not them, none of them. Break it."* While the wardens fetched water to wash the body, she pried the coal from the crown with the king's own knife.
- She has no idea how to break it. In 1453 she was a young kitchen maid in the Keep, and she remembers the giant who made the crown. **She is trying to reach Okton.** The Pyrewardens, Oselle's household, Lorard's scouts and the Watch are all looking for her.
- She can testify that the king never named Kitwin. That would destroy the Pyrewardens' claim and could get her killed within a day.
- She knows Oselle's taster was bribed, because some nights she tasted the same wine. She is a little poisoned herself: she has been failing saves against pale tincture (DMG p.258) for weeks and is dying slowly.

**Update (session 1):** ## REWORK (v2, DM secret)
Isine pried the Heartcoal from the crown with her bare hand, which under the new rules should have killed her (class level 3 or lower). She lived, so she is an anomaly like Kitwin: infected (Stirring). She survived because she was willing: she was obeying her king's last order, and she meant it. Her left palm is burned in the Coal's shape; her temper flares in small ways she hides, and she is also slowly poisoned. She has no idea what she carries.
