Wenna Hollis (born 1413), Meg Hollis's daughter and Rosa's mother, kept the slate of the Vent Games from 1452 to 1480, through the Night of Cinders and most of the Ash Reign, until the steam took her eyes. Now she sits on a stool by the Third Vent in a heap of damp towels with a cup of cider, seeing only shapes and light.

- **She remembers everything:**
  - every final since she was a girl at the Wet Games (1423–1430);
  - the Sergeant's Fall (1464) and the Strangle (1467);
  - who threw whom, and how.

  For a cup she'll tell any of it, and she corrects anyone who tells it wrong.
- **Her rulings:**
  - the scald tin (1453);
  - the Soak ending at the curfew bell (1454);
  - the Fury Rule (1464);
  - the ban on strangles in the Open, and the Empty Spoon (1467).
- **Voice:** thin, dry and exact. She calls everyone by their mother's name ("You're Gwenna's girl"), taps her cane once for yes and twice for no, and says "I heard that" about things she saw.
- **On Chadickith:** she remembers the giant carrying a small grey boy down the lane in the spring of 1423. She wants to know why it took him sixty-eight years to step on the ash.

## DM secrets
- **The sergeant's eyes:** she saw Brannoc Hale's eyes catch red in 1464, and since the roc she has understood it was the burn.
- **The ledger:** she knows where the Dead-Washers' Ledger is and what its old pages say. She thinks the Pyrehouse would burn it, and she'd give it to someone who would read it instead.
- **Rosa's debt:** she knows about the debt to House Mostana. She heard Rosa counting coins and crying in Ches, and has said nothing.

**Update (session 2):** Wenna hates that strangles come back in the Long Open's third ground, even on a list with Rosa's spoon on the shoulder, and will say so loudly at the Soak. She agreed to be the bout's last word ('Ask Wenna') only so she can watch the Ash herself.
