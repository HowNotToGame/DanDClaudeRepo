# Okton
A fire giant eighteen feet tall and old even for his kind. His skin looks like cooled iron, his beard is dull red wire bound in brass rings, and his hammer-scarred apron is the size of a sail. For about eighty years he has kept a forge in a vent-cavern in Steamside, Kharun, first under the last Velun kings and then under Ivoard Hask. He forges plate and gorgets for Kharun's city guard (reorganised by Hask as the Kharun Watch, the "Redcollars") and rides out with them when something too big for soldiers comes down from the hills. Use the Monster Manual **fire giant** (MM p.154) for his statistics, unchanged.

## Backstory canon (from the player)
- Okton works closely with the city guards of Kharun, forging their armour and sometimes helping with large threats.
- In spring 1423 DR a monstrous roc attacked Chadickith's village (see hadrun), when Chadickith was eight. Okton was on patrol nearby. He traded blows with the beast until it fled. The village was saved, but not everyone: Chadickith's parents, Berov and Radanya, died.
- Okton decided to keep the boy and has raised him at his forge for **68 years**. Chadickith is 76 in 1491 DR.
- When Chadickith was older, Okton taught him about the roc. It carries some kind of infection that makes it enjoy death. Okton is infected too, to a lesser degree.
- For a while he worked on himself, trying to find a way to defeat the beast. **Finally, a ritual has been created. Using his blood, he may now create hunters of "our order"** (Chadickith's words). The order is the **Order of the Banked Fire**, and its members are called the Banked (see order-of-the-banked-fire).

## What people see
- Patient, dry, slow to speak and slower to anger. Hums tuneless forge-songs in Giant. Keeps time in "heats" rather than hours.
- Calls Chadickith **"Small Tusk"**, because a seven-foot loxodon is small to him, and seventy-six is young to a giant. Corrects him gently and constantly. He enjoys watching Chadickith be wrong, and enjoys it even more when he finally gets it right.
- Speaks Giant by preference and a heavy, careful Common. Kneels to no one. Took no side when the king died.
- For years he worked through the night with the forge doors shut and came out smelling of hot blood as well as hot iron. On burning days he has been seen standing at the back of Pyre Square, watching the flames a little too long.
- He has no ordning, no clan and no fire giant kin anywhere near Ossmaar, and he won't talk about why.

## Want, fear, quirk
- **Want:** to kill the Gallowsbird and close the wound he left open at Hadrun, and to leave behind the Banked to finish the job if he can't.
- **Fear:** becoming what the roc is, in front of the boy he raised.
- **Quirk:** tests every piece of metal by tasting it, then delivers a verdict on whoever made it.

## DM secrets
- **Older shame:** as a young warrior, Okton marched in the fire giant host of the Bhaalspawn Yaga-Shura during the Bhaalspawn Crisis (1369 DR). He deserted before the end, which is why he has no ordning. He wandered south and east for forty years. Around 1410 King Uzhar Velun gave him the vent-forge in return for armouring Kharun's guard. He never knew that his old warlord's burned-out heart lay in a cold-iron casket in the Pyrehouse crypt up the hill (see heartcoal).
- **1423:** the roc he fought at Hadrun was already Glad. He saw the wrongness in its eyes without understanding it, and its blood didn't take in him. He was not infected then.
- **He forged the Crown of Ash, and that is when he was infected.** In 1453, fresh from the Night of Cinders, Ivoard Hask brought him the coal in its old Velun reliquary and ordered a crown. Okton recognised Yaga-Shura's heart. Refusing would have cost him the forge, the guard's goodwill and Hask's protection, and his 38-year-old loxodon ward would have gone to the Spire. He set the coal in a band reforged from the reliquary's cold iron, believing it would bind the hunger to a single wearer. The coal touched him while he worked. Only afterwards, feeling the warmth in himself, did he understand what he had seen in the roc, and that is when he began teaching Chadickith about the infection. **Chadickith does not know Okton forged the crown** (DM-confirmed).
- **The ritual's roots:** he built the Quenching from fire-priest rites he watched in Yaga-Shura's host, the same craft that wrapped Yaga-Shura's heart in flame. A candidate is "quenched" in his heated blood the way steel is quenched in oil. **The Quenching is how someone becomes a Blood Hunter** (see order-of-the-banked-fire). He knows better than anyone how dangerous its lineage is.
- **The ritual is his slow corruption.** His blood only works if the gladness in it is "lit." Before each Quenching he has to let the warmth rise by standing at pyres and deathbeds and feeding it, which is why he haunts Pyre Square. **Each of the Banked he makes moves him a step deeper on the Ashblood track.** He is at the edge of Kindled now. After two or three more Quenchings he will be Kindled, and after a handful more, Glad.
- **Why Chadickith hasn't been quenched:** Okton keeps putting him off. He says the boy isn't ready. The truth is that he can't bear to pour into his son the gladness he caught from a crown he forged.
- **His unspoken charge to the order:** he made the Banked partly so that someone will be able to kill him when he turns. He hasn't said it aloud yet, but he will, at the worst possible moment.
- **He can unmake the coal.** A fire giant's forge, fed with a fire giant's own heart's blood, can destroy what fire giant fire made. Destroying the coal ends all Ashblood. The Banked keep their abilities, but no new Banked can ever be made. The deeper Okton has gone by then, the worse it is for him.
- If the Heartcoal is brought to him, the gladness in him *wants* it. Play one terrible beat: he reaches out a little too eagerly, then masters himself. "Put it down, Small Tusk. On the anvil. Not in my hand."
- He is fond of Lorard, who respects craft, and wary of Kitwin, because he smells the gladness on the boy. He thinks Oselle hates him, and he is right: she blames the giant who set the coal for her mother's death. She does not know that her own great-grandfather brought the coal to Kharun.

**Update (session 1):** ## Stat block (v2, player-approved): Fire giant + Blood Hunter 18 (Order of the Pale Moon), progenitor of the Banked Fire
**Base:** MM fire giant (Huge giant, AC 18 plate, speed 30). PB +6 (higher of giant +4 / Blood Hunter +6).
**HP:** 162 + half the Blood Hunter levels (9 x (7+6) = 117) + Tough (+36) = **315**.
**Abilities:** STR 25, DEX 9, CON 23, INT 10, WIS 14, CHA 13 (feats instead of ASIs).
**Saves:** CON +12, CHA +7, DEX +5; Hardened Soul adds Rite Die (d10) to INT/WIS/CHA saves; Dark Augmentation adds Rite Die to STR/DEX/CON checks and saves.
**Skills:** Athletics +13, Perception +8, Survival +8, Insight +8, Arcana +6 (Occult Lore). Smith's tools +6. Immune to fire.
**Feats:** Tough, Sentinel, Great Weapon Master, Alert (+5 initiative).
**Fighting Style:** Great Weapon Fighting (each greatsword die counts as at least 3).
**Actions:** Multiattack (two greatswords, +13 to hit, 6d6+7; GWM option -5/+10) or Rock +13, 4d10+7. Blood Rites replace one attack with a bonus-action weapon attack (Extra Attack).
**Rite Die d10, Rite save DC 20.** Rites: Binding (empowered works on any size), Agony, Dread, Exposure, Marking, Blindness. Vital Sacrifice rolls twice (Vital Control), takes the lower.
**Crimson Offering:** fire (+1d10), bonus action + Vital Sacrifice. **Crimson Brand** once per rest free; **Crimson Anchor** (Brand teleport/move needs a CHA save or 4 Rite Dice necrotic).
**Hunter's Bane:** Occult Lore (Arcana), Sinister Insight (CON check, DC 8+CR), Grim Psychometry (identify/legend lore rituals).
**Pale Moon:** Feral Senses (Rite Die to Perception/Survival), Lycanthropic Curse (progenitor exception: **Roc**, Gargantuan, fly 120, uses the giant's HP, mental scores, skills, fire immunity and class features; natural weapons use the d10 Rite Die size and Rite DC 20), Hybrid Form (+10 ft speed, climb, Rite Die claws), Lycan Warrior (Shifted: magical attacks; resistance to non-magical, non-silvered B/P/S), Feral Prowess (regain CON mod HP at turn start when Shifted and under half HP), Lycanthropic Mastery (Shift at will, indefinitely, advantage vs Branded creatures).
**DM secret:** Okton fought the Gallowsbird as a plain fire giant because every Rite and Vital Sacrifice feeds the gladness (Ashblood) in his blood; he has been holding it back for decades. Given the full kit, he would end an encounter like the Pyre fight in a round or two, so he keeps it sealed and the player must earn its use.
