# Okton
A fire giant eighteen feet tall and old even for his kind. His skin looks like cooled iron, his beard is dull red wire bound in brass rings, and his hammer-scarred apron is the size of a sail. For decades he has kept a forge in a vent-cavern in Steamside, Kharun. He forges the plate and red-brass gorgets of the Kharun Watch (the "Redcollars") and rides out with them when something too big for soldiers comes down from the hills. Use the Monster Manual **fire giant** (MM p.154) for his statistics, unchanged.

## Backstory canon (from the player)
- Okton works closely with the city guards of Kharun, forging their armour and sometimes helping with large threats.
- When Chadickith was a child, a monstrous roc attacked his family's village (see hadrun). Okton was on patrol nearby. He traded blows with the beast until it fled. The village was saved, but not everyone: Chadickith's parents died.
- Okton decided to keep the boy and raised him at his forge.
- When Chadickith was older, Okton taught him about the roc. It carries some kind of infection that makes it enjoy death. Okton is infected too, to a lesser degree.
- For a while now he has been "working on himself", trying to find a way to defeat the beast.

## What people see
- Patient, dry, slow to speak and slower to anger. Hums tuneless forge-songs in Giant. Keeps time in "heats" rather than hours.
- Calls Chadickith **"Small Tusk"**, because a seven-foot loxodon is small to him. Corrects him gently and constantly. He enjoys watching the boy be wrong, and enjoys it even more when the boy finally gets it right.
- Speaks Giant by preference and a heavy, careful Common. Kneels to no one. Took no side when the king died.
- Lately he works through the night with the forge doors shut. He comes out smelling of hot blood as well as hot iron. On burning days he has been seen standing at the back of Pyre Square, watching the flames a little too long.
- He has no ordning, no clan and no fire giant kin anywhere near Ossmaar, and he won't talk about why.

## Want, fear, quirk
- **Want:** to kill the Gallowsbird and close the wound he left open at Hadrun.
- **Fear:** becoming what the roc is, in front of the boy he raised.
- **Quirk:** tests every piece of metal by tasting it, then delivers a verdict on whoever made it.

## DM secrets
- **He forged the Crown of Ash.** In 1453 DR the new king, Ivoard Hask, brought him a smouldering coal and asked for a crown to hold it. Okton recognised the coal and set it anyway, in a band of cold iron he believed would bind its hunger. His price was the vent-forge, the king's protection and a place to belong. His mild infection began then, not with the roc. The roc's blood in his wounds at Hadrun (1474) only woke it. **Chadickith does not know any of this.**
- **Older shame:** as a young warrior, Okton marched in the fire giant host of the Bhaalspawn Yaga-Shura during the Bhaalspawn Crisis (1369 DR). He deserted before the end, which is why he has no ordning. He knows exactly whose heart the coal is (see heartcoal).
- **What "working on himself" really is (decided):** he is forging a great roc-killing spear he calls **the Answer**, and he quenches it in his own blood. He believes, correctly, that only steel carrying Ashblood can bite the Gallowsbird's heart. Every quenching feeds his own gladness. The spear needs about **three more quenchings**. Each one moves him one step deeper on the Ashblood track (see ashblood). After the last he will be fully Kindled and close to Glad. He means to kill the roc and then walk into the Lake of Steam. He has told no one.
- **He can unmake the coal.** A fire giant's forge, fed with a fire giant's own heart's blood, can destroy what fire giant fire made. Destroying the coal ends all Ashblood, and the deeper Okton has gone, the worse that is for him. Whether the player reaches him before or after the spear is finished is the question for the table.
- If the Heartcoal is brought to him, the gladness in him *wants* it. Play one terrible beat: he reaches out a little too eagerly, then masters himself. "Put it down, Small Tusk. On the anvil. Not in my hand."
- He is fond of Lorard, who respects craft, and wary of Kitwin, because he smells the gladness on the boy. He thinks Oselle hates him, and he is right: she knows who set the coal that burned her mother.
