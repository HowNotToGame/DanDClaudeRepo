# Okton
A fire giant eighteen feet tall and old even for his kind. His skin looks like cooled iron, his beard is dull red wire bound in brass rings, and his hammer-scarred apron is the size of a sail. For about eighty years he has kept a forge in a vent-cavern in Steamside, Kharun, first under the last Velun kings and then under Ivoard Hask. He forges plate and gorgets for Kharun's city guard (reorganised by Hask as the Kharun Watch, the "Redcollars") and rides out with them when something too big for soldiers comes down from the hills. Use the Monster Manual **fire giant** (MM p.154) for his statistics, unchanged.

## Backstory canon (from the player)
- Okton works closely with the city guards of Kharun, forging their armour and sometimes helping with large threats.
- In spring 1423 DR a monstrous roc attacked Chadickith's village (see hadrun), when Chadickith was eight. Okton was on patrol nearby. He traded blows with the beast until it fled. The village was saved, but not everyone: Chadickith's parents, Berov and Radanya, died.
- Okton decided to keep the boy and has raised him at his forge for **68 years**. Chadickith is 76 in 1491 DR.
- When Chadickith was older, Okton taught him about the roc. It carried an infection (the burn, see ashblood). Okton is infected too, to a lesser degree.
- For a while he worked on himself, trying to find a way to defeat the beast. **Finally, a ritual has been created. Using his blood, he may now create hunters of "our order"** (Chadickith's words). The order is the **Order of the Banked Fire**, and its members are called the Banked (see order-of-the-banked-fire).

## What people see
- Patient, dry, slow to speak and slower to anger. Hums tuneless forge-songs in Giant. Keeps time in "heats" rather than hours.
- Calls Chadickith **"Small Tusk"**, because a seven-foot loxodon is small to him, and seventy-six is young to a giant. Corrects him gently and constantly. He enjoys watching Chadickith be wrong, and enjoys it even more when he finally gets it right.
- Speaks Giant by preference and a heavy, careful Common. Kneels to no one. Took no side when the king died.
- For years he worked through the night with the forge doors shut and came out smelling of hot blood as well as hot iron. On burning days he has been seen standing at the back of Pyre Square, watching the flames a little too long.
- He has no ordning, no clan and no fire giant kin anywhere near Ossmaar, and he won't talk about why.

## Want, fear, quirk
- **Want:** once, to kill the Gallowsbird and close the wound he left open at Hadrun; that is done. Now: to deal with the Heartcoal, and to raise an Order strong enough to survive doing it.
- **Fear:** becoming what the roc was, in front of the boy he raised.
- **Quirk:** tests every piece of metal by tasting it, then delivers a verdict on whoever made it.

## What he has done since the king died
- **Session 1:** he kept the Heartcoal in a cold-iron box under his hearth slab after Chadickith brought it from Isine. He broke into the almshouse dead-house to back Chadickith against the Choir, and held the captured brothers Vesan and Wyn at the forge.
- **The Gallowsbird (night of day 2):** he fought it at the Great Pyre as a plain fire giant, with rocks and his sword alongside Lorard's bolt-throwers. Grappled and near death, he killed it in the eighth round.
- **The Quenching:** when the roc killed Chadickith, Okton performed the blood rite on him, and Chadickith woke a Blood Hunter. On day 3, in the Collarhouse cellar, he quenched five of the Watchmen raging from the roc's blood (see order-of-the-banked-fire). He will not quench anyone without a lucid, freely given yes.
- **Day 3 on:** he taught Isine to bleed off the heat in his cellar, kept the forge as the Order's first house, and helped plan the Coal's escape (days 4 to 8).
- **Day 9:** he stood at the forge door with Chadickith against a Choir-led crowd, then let five Ashward elders search the forge. He admitted the Coal had been kept there to stop the Choir feeding it, and that it is gone where the Choir cannot reach.
- **Day 10:** he answered the Lord-Marshal's search with "It was. It is not now. I will not say where." He sat at the noon council as Halda's guest. He agreed, with Isine, to a **trial burn ward** in his cellar (three cots, one month, under Halda's conditions; see gwenmund-cobb).
- **Day 11:** he wants the Choir's ash line and flame-coin at the ward door answered in the open, and would not touch the coin barehanded. In the evening he explained the Quenching's risks to Gwenmund Cobb in plain words and accepted her refusal, promising the Order would shelter her children if she dies.
- **His view of the regency:** hopeful. He likes hearth-votes, and distrusts the coin-weighting and the Lady's delay.

## DM secrets
- **Older shame:** as a young warrior, Okton marched in the fire giant host of the Bhaalspawn Yaga-Shura during the Bhaalspawn Crisis (1369 DR). He deserted before the end, which is why he has no ordning. He wandered south and east for forty years. Around 1410 King Uzhar Velun gave him the vent-forge in return for armouring Kharun's guard. He never knew that his old warlord's burned-out heart lay in a cold-iron casket in the Pyrehouse crypt up the hill (see heartcoal). **Chadickith does not know any of this.**
- **1423:** the roc he fought at Hadrun already carried the burn. He saw the wrongness in its eyes without understanding it, and its blood didn't take in him. He was not infected then.
- **He forged the Crown of Ash, and that is when he was infected.** In 1453, fresh from the Night of Cinders, Ivoard Hask brought him the coal in its old Velun reliquary and ordered a crown. Okton recognised Yaga-Shura's heart. Refusing would have cost him the forge, the guard's goodwill and Hask's protection, and his 38-year-old loxodon ward would have gone to the Spire. He set the coal in a band reforged from the reliquary's cold iron, believing it would bind the hunger to a single wearer. The coal touched him while he worked. **Chadickith knows** (Okton admitted it in session 1: he forged the band and set the coal). He still does not know the coal's Yaga-Shura origin or Okton's desertion.
- **He is stable and disciplined.** He carries the burn at a controlled Stirring and has held it level for decades. Using his Blood Rites and Vital Sacrifices can stir the rage, which is why he fought the Gallowsbird as a plain fire giant and keeps his full kit sealed (stat block below). There is no doom clock and no unspoken charge that the Banked must kill him: those v1 ideas are retired.
- **What the Quenching costs him** is set in the locked rules block; never put numbers in his mouth ("it takes a piece of my strength I'll not get back, and sometimes a year"). So far he has quenched six who lived (Chadickith, then the five Watchmen on day 3) and one who died (a dying Watch veteran last winter, held nine days, who died raging). His craft rank is 3.
- **The ritual's roots:** he built the Quenching from fire-priest rites he watched in Yaga-Shura's host, the same craft that wrapped Yaga-Shura's heart in flame. A candidate is "quenched" in his heated blood the way steel is quenched in oil. He knows better than anyone how dangerous its lineage is.
- **He can unmake the coal.** A fire giant's forge, fed with a fire giant's own heart's blood, can destroy what fire giant fire made, at a cost to himself. Destroying it stops new infections; existing hosts keep the burn, and the Quenching still works. His long goal is to do it once the Order can survive the aftermath. He told Isine it can be done one day, "not by one man alone."
- **The Coal now lies in the Dunmarrow vault** (since day 8; see heartcoal). Okton stayed in Kharun.
- If the Heartcoal is brought to him, the burn in him *wants* it. Play one terrible beat: he reaches out a little too eagerly, then masters himself. "Put it down, Small Tusk. On the anvil. Not in my hand."
- He is fond of Lorard, who respects craft, and wary of Kitwin, because he smells the burn on the boy. He thinks Oselle hates him, and he is right: she blames the giant who set the coal for her mother's death. She does not know that her own great-grandfather brought the coal to Kharun.
- He suspected Isine was being poisoned before anyone else did.

## Stat block (v2, player-approved): Fire giant + Blood Hunter 18 (Order of the Pale Moon), progenitor of the Banked Fire
**Base:** MM fire giant (Huge giant, AC 18 plate, speed 30). PB +6 (higher of giant +4 / Blood Hunter +6).
**HP:** 162 + half the Blood Hunter levels (9 x (7+6) = 117) + Tough (+36) = **315**.
**Abilities:** STR 25, DEX 9, CON 23, INT 10, WIS 14, CHA 13 (feats instead of ASIs).
**Saves:** CON +12, CHA +7, DEX +5; Hardened Soul adds Rite Die (d10) to INT/WIS/CHA saves; Dark Augmentation adds Rite Die to STR/DEX/CON checks and saves.
**Skills:** Athletics +13, Perception +8, Survival +8, Insight +8, Arcana +6 (Occult Lore). Smith's tools +6. Immune to fire.
**Feats:** Tough, Sentinel, Great Weapon Master, Alert (+5 initiative).
**Fighting Style:** Great Weapon Fighting (each greatsword die counts as at least 3).
**Actions:** Multiattack (two greatswords, +13 to hit, 6d6+7; GWM option -5/+10) or Rock +13, 4d10+7. Blood Rites replace one attack with a bonus-action weapon attack (Extra Attack).
**Rite Die d10, Rite save DC 20.** Rites: Binding (empowered works on any size), Agony, Dread, Exposure, Marking, Blindness. Vital Sacrifice rolls twice (Vital Control), takes the lower.
**Crimson Offering:** fire (+1d10), bonus action + Vital Sacrifice. **Crimson Brand** once per rest free; **Crimson Anchor** (Brand teleport/move needs a CHA save or 4 Rite Dice necrotic).
**Hunter's Bane:** Occult Lore (Arcana), Sinister Insight (CON check, DC 8+CR), Grim Psychometry (identify/legend lore rituals).
**Pale Moon:** Feral Senses (Rite Die to Perception/Survival), Lycanthropic Curse (progenitor exception: **Roc**, Gargantuan, fly 120, uses the giant's HP, mental scores, skills, fire immunity and class features; natural weapons use the d10 Rite Die size and Rite DC 20), Hybrid Form (+10 ft speed, climb, Rite Die claws), Lycan Warrior (Shifted: magical attacks; resistance to non-magical, non-silvered B/P/S), Feral Prowess (regain CON mod HP at turn start when Shifted and under half HP), Lycanthropic Mastery (Shift at will, indefinitely, advantage vs Branded creatures).
**DM secret:** Okton fought the Gallowsbird as a plain fire giant because every Rite and Vital Sacrifice stirs the burn (Ashblood) in his blood; he has been holding it back for decades. Given the full kit, he would end an encounter like the Pyre fight in a round or two, so he keeps it sealed and the player must earn its use.
