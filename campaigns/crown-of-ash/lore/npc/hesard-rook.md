Watch captain of the river wall, relieved and confined on day 9 for spying for the Choir; cousin of the Lord-Marshal in secret.

**Update (session 1):** Slipped out after the first hearing, recommended the two post-roc wall-walk men, sent his orderly Fenia to listen at doors and report to a woman in grey, and tried to have the forge searched. Exposed by the canary trap and Fenia's testimony at the war-table. In his cell he told Lorard he did it because Coldwell swore the Pyrehouse would back Lorard for the crown, and that he did not know the Choir meant to burn the ward.
## DM secrets
Hesard is the nephew of Pellot Rook, Lorard's true father, so he is Lorard's cousin. Only Chadickith, Marda, Lorard and Rook know.
