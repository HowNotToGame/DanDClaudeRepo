# Hesard Rook, Watch captain (relieved)
One of the Kharun Watch's six captains, commanding the river wall until day 9. He stood in Halda's half-ring at the noon hearing on day 3, looked uneasy, and left soon after.

## What is known
- **The leak:** on days 4 and 5, false routes for the Coal were fed to the Watch's command in a canary trap. The Choir's watchers turned up at the Weir Gate and then at the Mill Gate, the two routes only Rook's side had been told. Two men who joined the postern roster after the roc came on his recommendation, and one of them was watching the Saltgate wall-walk. Rook's men tailed the Order's people for days.
- **The war-table (dawn, day 9):** his orderly **Fenia** swore that Rook had him listen at doors and report, for coin, to a woman at the chandler's by the dye-works. Fenia thought it was for the Marshal. Rook denied it smoothly and called it coercion by the giant's people, but the captains believed Fenia. Halda relieved Rook of his post and **confined him to quarters pending inquiry**. He has not been charged. His door is guarded by men not drawn from his own company.
- **Day 11:** he refuses to answer the inquiry, but asked to see the Lord-Marshal alone, and Halda allowed it.

## DM secrets
- **He is Lorard's cousin.** His late uncle **Pellot Rook** was Lorard's real father (see lorard-hask). Hesard has kept the Rook family's secret for years.
- **His motive:** Coldwell swore the Pyrehouse would back Lorard for the crown if the Coal were recovered, and Hesard wanted his cousin crowned. He fed the Choir news of the forge and of Chadickith's movements. He did not know the Choir meant to burn the ward or the forge, and the flame-silver sickened him.
- **His confession to Lorard (day 11)** was overheard by Chadickith and written down by Captain Marda Ironside. Lorard did not forgive him, and did not call for the rope. Marda holds the record sealed until day 14.
