Joss Marl (born 1466) poles a flat ferry under Kharun's river wall. He is thin as a ferry pole, brown and stringy, with a rope-hauler's long, loose arms, and he laughs at himself.

- **His reputation:** "Nobody can hold him if he gets wet," the rail says.
- **How he wrestles:** he learned the Knot in Teo Varrow's warehouse. In the Open he slips grips and takes backs, which is how he rode Ilk Venn down in the 1491 quarterfinal.
- **Day 12, 1491:**
  - He beat Ilk Venn by Down.
  - He lost the semifinal to Tess Aldin when his wet-palmed grip slipped and he stumbled over the chalk.
  - He took 2 crowns, laughed on his back in the dust, and put his name down for the plank-walk after the pit.
- **His claim:** he says he descends from Old Marl Hollis through a ferryman's daughter. Rosa denies it, loudly, every year.

## DM secrets
- **Smuggling:** he ferries smuggled brimstone past the river wall by night for a Quay crew.
- **The river wall:** since Captain Rook was relieved, the wall's night watch has thin hours, and Joss knows exactly which ones.
