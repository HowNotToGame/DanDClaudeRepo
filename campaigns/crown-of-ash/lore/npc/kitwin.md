# Kitwin, the Pyre-Born
Nineteen. Slight and pale, with soft brown curls and his father's heavy-lidded grey eyes. He wears an acolyte's grey robe with the sleeves rolled to the elbow, and ash is ground into the creases of his hands. His mother, a Pyrehouse acolyte named Doret, died of fever when he was a year old, and the Pyrewardens raised him among the pyres.

## Claim and power base
- **Claim:** the High Pyrewarden testifies that the dying king named Kitwin his heir with his last breath. Rumour adds that the Heartcoal once glowed in the boy's hands.
- **Weakness:** unacknowledged for nineteen years. A bastard, a boy, and a priest's creature.
- **Power:** the Pyrewardens, who hold the Crown of Ash and perform the coronation rite; the devout; and the poor of the Ashward, who call him the **Gentle Heir** because he washes their dead with his own hands.

## Public face
Soft-spoken, humble, seemingly uninterested in power. He promises "mercy": an end to the Sky Gallows, an honoured burning for every pauper, and healing houses for the dying. When people kneel to him, he kneels back.

## Want, fear, voice
- **Wants:** to be good, to end suffering, and to be the son his father never once called him.
- **Fears:** the heat that comes over him when someone dies, and what it says about him.
- **Voice and quirk:** a near-whisper. He always asks your name and repeats it: "Chadickith. So I'll remember you, after." Hums funeral hymns under his breath. Smiles at the wrong moments, then apologises.

## What has happened to him
- **The Great Pyre rite (night of day 2):** Coldwell set the Crown of Ash on kneeling Kitwin, with grey grains packed into the empty socket. The grains glowed and guttered. Then Brother Garn of the Choir killed a smiling old Ashward volunteer on the dais while Kitwin held the man's hand, and the crown kindled orange. The crowd roared that the Ember had chosen him. Kitwin looked down at the blade and wept and smiled at once. When the Gallowsbird stooped on the dais it veered away from him. Sister Ilsa and another singer hurried him off toward the Pyrehouse.
- **Dawn, day 3:** the Pyrehouse bell tolled out of rhythm, and Coldwell proclaimed Kitwin "kindled".
- **Noon, day 3:** before Halda's six captains, two Choir brothers swore the crown was lit with grains from a casket in the crypt. The Watch ruled that the Coal did **not** choose Kitwin.
- **Since then** he has not been seen in public. His Ashward support dipped after the forge crowd on day 9, when the elders learned the Choir had lied about a stolen Coal and that its knife had cut Isine.
- **The regency (day 10)** gives the Pyrehouse the rites but no claim to the crown. Halda demanded Kitwin be produced to the council "as a boy, not a banner". Coldwell has agreed to produce him on the **morning of the first sitting** (day 13), in the Pyrehouse's presence, and not before.

## DM secrets
- **He is infected with the burn, an anomaly.** A child exposed that young should have died of it. He lived because he was **willing**: he truly believed he was chosen and welcomed the Coal (Hask's blood may have helped). The Choir holds his rage down with its hymns. He sits at Stirring, close to Frenzied. A rescue does not cure him: there is no cure, only management.
- **His mercies.** Over four years he has helped more than thirty of the dying in the Pyrehouse infirmary on their way, with a pillow or a cup of poppy. He calls it mercy, and he felt the heat rise each time and took it for grace. Some of them weren't dying fast enough. A few weren't dying at all. (The old "joy" framing is retired by the v2 rules: the burn gives rage, not gladness.)
- **The coal really did kindle for him.** During the Quiet Year the king summoned him in secret, put the crown in his hands and watched it glow, then sent him away horrified. Kitwin thinks it was a blessing.
- **He doesn't know about the Choir's killings** (Brother Wyn blurted that Kitwin "must never know"). He doesn't know the deathbed naming was invented by Coldwell, and believes it completely. Isine's testimony at the council has now made the lie public among the leaders; if it reaches him, it may break him, and his gentleness with it.
- **Where he is:** Hesby saw hooded Choir carry a crowned boy in through the Pyrehouse's north postern by the charnel wall at dusk on day 2. Garn said the crown would be "kept warm" in the crypt beside the Last Ember casket. The Choir doubled the north-postern guard after Hesby was seen.
- If the Heartcoal is destroyed, new infections stop, but Kitwin keeps the burn: destruction does not free or kill him.
