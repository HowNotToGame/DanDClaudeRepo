# Kitwin, the Pyre-Born
Nineteen. Slight and pale, with soft brown curls and his father's heavy-lidded grey eyes. He wears an acolyte's grey robe with the sleeves rolled to the elbow, and ash is ground into the creases of his hands. His mother, a Pyrehouse acolyte named Doret, died of fever when he was a year old, and the Pyrewardens raised him among the pyres.

## Claim and power base
- **Claim:** the High Pyrewarden testifies that the dying king named Kitwin his heir with his last breath. Rumour adds that the Heartcoal once glowed in the boy's hands.
- **Weakness:** unacknowledged for nineteen years. A bastard, a boy, and a priest's creature.
- **Power:** the Pyrewardens, who hold the Crown of Ash and perform the coronation rite; the devout; and the poor of the Ashward, who call him the **Gentle Heir** because he washes their dead with his own hands.

## Public face
Soft-spoken, humble, seemingly uninterested in power. He promises "mercy": an end to the Sky Gallows, an honoured burning for every pauper, and healing houses for the dying. When people kneel to him, he kneels back.

## Want, fear, voice
- **Wants:** to be good, to end suffering, and to be the son his father never once called him.
- **Fears:** the warmth that comes over him when someone dies, and what it says about him.
- **Voice and quirk:** a near-whisper. He always asks your name and repeats it: "Chadickith. So I'll remember you, after." Hums funeral hymns under his breath. Smiles at the wrong moments, then apologises.

## Sympathetic / monstrous
- His compassion is real. He has sat with more dying paupers than every other priest in Kharun put together.
- So is his gladness (see below).

## DM secrets
- **He is Kindled, close to Glad.** He has breathed the Long Smoke since infancy, and the Ashblood runs deep in him. Over four years he has helped more than thirty of the dying in the Pyrehouse infirmary on their way, with a pillow or a cup of poppy, and felt joy each time. He calls it mercy. Some of them weren't dying fast enough. A few weren't dying at all.
- **The coal really did kindle for him.** During the Quiet Year the king summoned him in secret, put the crown in his hands and watched it glow, then sent him away horrified. Kitwin thinks it was a blessing. By the Ember Law he may well be the "true" king, which tells you everything about the Ember Law.
- **He doesn't know the deathbed naming was invented** by High Pyrewarden Tamius Coldwell, and he believes it completely. Proving the lie would break him, and might break his gentleness too.
- If the coal is destroyed while he is Glad, he dies. If it is destroyed while he is only Kindled, he lives, hollow, and for the first time he will know which of his mercies were murders.
- *Optional tie:* as a boy he ran errands from the Pyrehouse to Okton's forge for iron fittings, so he and young Chadickith know each other's faces. Kitwin remembers the loxodon boy fondly. Okton never let the acolyte inside.
