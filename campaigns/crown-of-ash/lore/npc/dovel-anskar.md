Pyrewarden who has burned the Watch's dead for nineteen years. Named by High Pyrewarden Tamius Coldwell as the Pyrehouse's lamp at the first sitting (day 13). Seat passed 9-3, name read aloud and entered on the roll; Oselle holds the final reading for up to a tenday so the moots hear the name first. DM only: chosen because he is publicly clean and owes the Pyrehouse for a sick child, which makes him steerable; he is not openly sworn to the Choir.
