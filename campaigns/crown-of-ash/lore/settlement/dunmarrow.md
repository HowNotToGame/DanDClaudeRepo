Razed castle town in the high Cinder Hills, burned by Ivoard Hask's Ash Company in 1463 DR; the Order of the Banked Fire's chosen redoubt for the Heartcoal and future order hall.

**Update (session 1):** ## Description
A walled castle town on a basalt shoulder in the north-eastern Cinder Hills, about four days' walk from Kharun and well beyond Hadrun and the roc's old eyrie. Dunmarrow was held by Lord Dunmar, who refused Ivoard Hask's tithe in 1463 DR. Hask's Ash Company burned the town and its keep in a single night and scattered the survivors. Nobody has rebuilt it in twenty-eight years: roofless houses, a split gatehouse, a keep with intact lower vaults and a dry cistern, charcoal-burners' camps at the edges, goat-scrub over the streets. Shepherds avoid it as a place of ghosts.

## Order plans (player direction)
The Order of the Banked Fire will turn Dunmarrow into its order hall: repair the keep, reinvigorate the town with its own forces and followers, and keep the Heartcoal in the iron-lined vault below. It sits far from any village, has defensible walls and lies well away from Lorard's army and the Choir's reach.

## DM secrets
- The Choir does not know the site. Hask's old survivors do; some of Dunmar's blood may still live in the hills.
- The keep vaults were never found by Hask's looters.

**Update (session 1):** ## Gravekeeper
Ulric Vane, an old stooped man who survived the burning as a stonemason's boy and has tended the graves of Dunmarrow ever since, living in the ruined chapel with a goat. He keeps the headstones free of moss and says the names aloud. Wary of strangers, lonely, and tells it straight.
