# Dunmarrow
A razed castle town on a basalt shoulder in the eastern high cones of the Cinder Hills, about fifty miles south-east of Kharun. Lord Dunmar held it until 1463 DR, when he refused Ivoard Hask's tithe. Hask's Ash Company burned the town and its keep in a single night and scattered the survivors. Nobody has rebuilt it in twenty-eight years: roofless streets, a split gatehouse, a keep with intact lower vaults and a dry cistern, charcoal-burners' camps at the edges, goat-scrub over the streets. Shepherds avoid it as a place of ghosts, and the chancery lists it as abandoned.

## Getting there
From Kharun, the Gallows Road runs past the Sky Gallows toward Cinder Ford (a Marshal's picket held the ford, with a toll, in the days of the siege; a dry gulley bypasses it). Above the ford, a charcoal-burners' camp under the headman **Maeis Hollis** sits at the foot of the old **Dunmar Road**: about forty-five miles of overgrown track, two days on foot, said to be haunted. The whole journey is about four days on foot.

## The gravekeeper
**Ulric Vane**, old, lean, grey and stooped, survived the burning as a stonemason's boy and has tended Dunmarrow's graves ever since. He lives in the ruined chapel by the keep with a tethered goat, scrapes the moss off the headstones and says the names aloud. He is wary of strangers, lonely, and tells it straight. He showed the Order the master mason's mark (three rising lines in a ring) that found the way into the keep's sealed vault.

## The Order's hall (from day 7)
Chadickith told Ulric the party were an order of hunters looking for a place for an order hall. Ulric agreed on three conditions, and the Order accepted them:
- never build over the graves;
- keep the names;
- let him stay as keeper.

The Order of the Banked Fire means to make Dunmarrow its hall: to repair the keep, bring the town back to life with its own people and followers, and train its Raw there. It is far from any village, has defensible walls, and lies well away from Lorard's army and the Choir's reach. Gwenis and Kita Dunmore are there now. Hesby, Samson, Cassmund and Warden Kaselle Redfern are on their way, and Isine may follow for her Quenching.

## The vault
Beneath the keep, behind a fallen stair and a beam in the lower hall, lies a door that Lord Dunmar sealed with his own hand and that Hask's looters never found. The Order opened it on the morning of day 8. Inside is a dry stone strongroom banded with iron, with an inner iron door, a drain to the dry cistern and one air shaft. It held a stack of old weapons rusted through, and an iron strongbox of Lord Dunmar's letters about Hask's tithe.
