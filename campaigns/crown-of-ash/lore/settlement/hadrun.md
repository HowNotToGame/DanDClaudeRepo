# Hadrun
A shepherd and charcoal-burner village high in the Cinder Hills, two days' walk inland from Kharun: terraced barley, goat-folds, charcoal mounds smoking on the slopes. Before 1474 DR it held about two hundred people. Most were human, with a handful of loxodon families whose great-grandparents came north out of the Shaar, so long ago that even they argue about why. Chadickith was born into one of those families.

## The Day of the Shadow (spring 1474 DR)
The roc the hill-folk now call the Gallowsbird came down on Hadrun out of a clear sky. It tore roofs off and carried off livestock and people, killing far beyond what it could eat. Okton was on patrol in the hills. He reached the village before the slaughter was finished and fought the beast in the burning square until it fled, one wing scorched and its beak split. About sixty villagers died, among them Chadickith's parents. Okton took the orphan to Kharun.

## Hadrun now
- Around eighty people. Half the houses are roofless and have been made into stone cairns for the dead.
- **The Roc Bell**, an old bronze pot-bell on the ridge, is rung whenever the great shadow is seen. Children are taught to run for the root-cellars at its first stroke.
- At the village edge stands a crude statue of a giant, built of charcoal-blackened stones. Some villagers leave offerings at it. Others spit at it, because Okton came too late for their families.
- **Dobrila**, the old loxodon weaver who swaddled infant Chadickith, still lives here. She keeps a box of his parents' things: a carved tusk-comb, a clay cup, and a letter she cannot read.
- The bell has rung four times this season. Since the Sky Gallows went quiet, the Gallowsbird hunts the high cones again. The headman has sent runners to Kharun begging for Okton, and the sealed gates have turned them back.

## DM secrets
- **The 1474 attack was not random.** That spring the king crushed the Velun Plot, an attempt by nobles to restore the old dynasty. He sent forty of the condemned to the Sky Gallows in a single tenday, all anointed with pyre-ash. The roc gorged on Ashblood-tainted flesh and went into its first true frenzy, and Hadrun was the nearest village to its eyrie. **The tyrant's executions killed Chadickith's parents.**
- The letter in Dobrila's box is from Chadickith's mother to a cousin in Thornwater. It is one ordinary page about the weather and a sick goat, plus a line saying the great bird had been "circling the Spire every day this month, fat and wrong." His parents are unnamed; name them with the player.
- Hadrun's charcoal fed the Pyrehouse fires for decades. The village sold the fuel that burned the ash that made the monster.
