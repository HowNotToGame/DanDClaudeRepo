# Kharun
The capital of Ossmaar and its only real city, home to about 9,000 people. It climbs in tiers up black basalt terraces above the steaming Ossar Shore. The lower city smells of sulphur, the upper city of woodsmoke and pyre-smoke. Most days, fine grey ash drifts down from the Pyrehouse chimney. Locals call it "the king's snow" and keep their shutters closed.

Chadickith has lived here for 68 years, since Okton brought him from Hadrun at age eight. He grew up at Okton's forge in Steamside and knows the city better than almost anyone alive. (He has done odd jobs and hired-sword work for the Watch.)

## Districts
- **The Ember Keep** (top terrace): a squat fortress-palace of black basalt with red-glazed roof tiles. It holds the throne hall, the treasury, the chancery and the royal seal. Oselle Velun's household guard has held it since the king died; weapons are peace-bonded at its gate. Its granaries now feed the Ashward under Watch guard.
- **The Pyrehouse:** the Pyrewardens' domed temple-crematorium beside the Keep, under the "Long Smoke," a chimney that has burned for nearly ninety years. The Crown of Ash lies here under guard, and the Great Pyre stands in Pyre Square in front of it. Kitwin lives inside and has not been seen in public since the night of the rite. A walled cemetery surrounds it; the north postern by the charnel wall is the singers' door.
- **The Lamp Market** (middle terrace): the guild quarter, home to lamp-oil sellers, alum dyers, moneychangers and brimstone brokers. These are Oselle's people. Hanging lamps burn day and night to keep off the ash-gloom.
- **The Collarhouse:** the Kharun Watch's fortified hall and gaol, beside the Saltgate. Its long hall is where the noon council of day 10 set up the regency, and where the Lamplit Council's first sitting meets (day 13). Its cellar holds the Watchmen still lost to the burn.
- **The Ashward** (lowest terrace): the poorest district, directly under the smoke. Rag-pickers, pit-labourers and coughing children live here. Kitwin is beloved in the Ashward, though less since the forge crowd of day 9 learned the Choir had lied. The **almshouse** and its dead-house are here, where the Choir brought its smiling dead. A Watch notice under Halda's seal now protects the forge ward's patients.
- **Steamside (the Vents):** the shore district where hot vents break through the basalt. It is full of bathhouses, dye-works and smithies that use the earth's heat. **Okton's Forge** is a cavern-smithy dug into the largest vent, with doors big enough for a giant, and the whole district rings with his hammer. Since day 10 its vent-heated cellar holds a trial ward for the burn, with its own lane door, two Watch guards on the main door and wardens on the lane. In the dye-works lanes nearby, a chandler's loft flashed the Choir's signal lamp until day 9.
- **Brimstone Quay and the Saltgate:** the harbour, with yellow-stained barges, Calishite galleys and House Mostana's warehouses.
- **The Lowfields:** outside the landward wall. Lorard's army camp stretches to the horizon in tents and cookfires; three hundred of his men have been inside the walls since noon on day 9.

## Things to do
- Steam baths in Steamside, where everyone talks too much, Watch officers and Calishite factors included.
- Watch-sanctioned pit fights in the Collarhouse yard. The Watch recruits the winners.
- Pyre Square on burning days: crowds, pickpockets, and preachers for all three heirs.
- The Gallows Road south to the Sky Gallows, now overgrown and avoided.

## Law and order right now (evening, day 11)
- **The gates are open** since noon on day 9, on Halda's terms: the Watch keeps the city, the gates, the Collarhouse, the Pyrehouse and the Ashward's safety, and Lorard's men entered by column with no pillage. While the gates were sealed (from the king's death to day 9) a Watch pass was needed to leave or enter.
- **The regency:** the Lamplit Council governs until the Coal is broken or found (see lamplit-council). Its first sitting is at noon on day 13.
- On the upper two terraces, weapons must be peace-bonded.
- A loaf reached 4–5 cp while the gates were sealed and has eased to about 3 cp; alchemist's supplies went for 80 gp in the Lamp Market at the height of it.

## DM secrets
- Okton's forge is the one place in Kharun every faction hesitates to raid, because no one wants to fight a fire giant inside the walls. That makes it a refuge, and it becomes a target the moment someone decides the giant is in the way.
- The Long Smoke never goes out because the Pyrewardens' inner circle keeps it fed. The Ashward's "pyre-cough" is ordinary lung-rot. (The v1 idea that the smoke carries a taint of Ashblood is retired: ash and smoke carry no burn under the v2 rules.)
- **The plague road:** a bricked tunnel behind the lye barrels in the almshouse dead-house cellar runs to an iron gate under the Pyrehouse crypt. Chadickith has Peret's key to the gate.
- **The Choir has marked the forge ward** with an ash line and a flame-stamped coin. A forge that every faction once hesitated to raid has become a place the Choir wants watched, and a coin left there tells it roughly where.
- An old Velun-era tunnel runs from the Ember Keep's cellars down to the Saltgate warehouses. Oselle knows about it, and House Mostana uses it.
