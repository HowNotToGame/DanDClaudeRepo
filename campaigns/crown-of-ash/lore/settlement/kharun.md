# Kharun
The capital of Ossmaar and its only real city, home to about 9,000 people. It climbs in tiers up black basalt terraces above the steaming Ossar Shore. The lower city smells of sulphur, the upper city of woodsmoke and pyre-smoke. Most days, fine grey ash drifts down from the Pyrehouse chimney. Locals call it "the king's snow" and keep their shutters closed.

Chadickith grew up here, at Okton's forge in Steamside, and knows the city well. (He has done odd jobs and hired-sword work for the Watch.)

## Districts
- **The Ember Keep** (top terrace): a squat fortress-palace of black basalt with red-glazed roof tiles. It holds the throne hall, the treasury, the chancery and the royal seal. Oselle Velun's household guard has held it since the king died.
- **The Pyrehouse:** the Pyrewardens' domed temple-crematorium beside the Keep, under the "Long Smoke," a chimney that has burned for 38 years. The Crown of Ash lies here under guard, and the Great Pyre stands in Pyre Square in front of it. Kitwin lives inside.
- **The Lamp Market** (middle terrace): the guild quarter, home to lamp-oil sellers, alum dyers, moneychangers and brimstone brokers. These are Oselle's people. Hanging lamps burn day and night to keep off the ash-gloom.
- **The Collarhouse:** the Kharun Watch's fortified hall and gaol, beside the Saltgate.
- **The Ashward** (lowest terrace): the poorest district, directly under the smoke. Rag-pickers, pit-labourers and coughing children live here. Kitwin is beloved in the Ashward.
- **Steamside (the Vents):** the shore district where hot vents break through the basalt. It is full of bathhouses, dye-works and smithies that use the earth's heat. **Okton's Forge** is a cavern-smithy dug into the largest vent, with doors big enough for a giant, and the whole district rings with his hammer.
- **Brimstone Quay and the Saltgate:** the harbour, with yellow-stained barges, Calishite galleys and House Mostana's warehouses.
- **The Lowfields:** outside the landward wall. Lorard's army camp stretches to the horizon in tents and cookfires.

## Things to do
- Steam baths in Steamside, where everyone talks too much, Watch officers and Calishite factors included.
- Watch-sanctioned pit fights in the Collarhouse yard. The Watch recruits the winners.
- Pyre Square on burning days: crowds, pickpockets, and preachers for all three heirs.
- The Gallows Road south to the Sky Gallows, now overgrown and avoided.

## Law and order right now
The Watch has sealed the gates, and a pass is needed to leave or enter. Curfew falls at the Pyrehouse's evening bell. On the upper two terraces, weapons must be peace-bonded. Each heir's agents work the streets openly.

## DM secrets
- Okton's forge is the one place in Kharun every faction hesitates to raid, because no one wants to fight a fire giant inside the walls. That makes it a refuge, and it becomes a target the moment someone decides the giant is in the way.
- The Long Smoke never goes out because the Pyrewardens' inner circle keeps it fed. Its ash carries a faint taint of Ashblood. The Ashward's "pyre-cough" is mostly ordinary lung-rot, but children raised under the smoke are slightly more prone to the gladness (see ashblood).
- An old Velun-era tunnel runs from the Ember Keep's cellars down to the Saltgate warehouses. Oselle knows about it, and House Mostana uses it.
