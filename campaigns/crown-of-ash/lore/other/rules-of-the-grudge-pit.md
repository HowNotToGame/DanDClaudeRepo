Rosa's wrestling slate for the Vent Games comes in two forms that share one set of moves:
- **The Short Open** (Wenna's curfew rules, since 1454): one quick bout, decided by Out, Down or the yield. Rosa runs the afternoon bracket this way, and day 12 of 1491 was wrestled short.
- **The Long Open** (the Seven Roots bout): three grounds in a fixed order (the Rope, the Collar, the Ash), one running tally of marks, and a sudden-death Last Fall if it's tied. Rosa drafted it from 1489 with every root of Kharun's wrestling. From 1492 it decides the Open's final and the Champion's Grudge, and any grudge may be called long.

Everything follows the 2014 grapple rules where they apply (contested checks, grappled, prone), with Rosa's slate on top. Her ruling is final, and her spoon comes down on her tub when a bout is decided.

## 2. The pit
- **The salt ring:** five paces across (15 ft), laid fresh in Saltreach salt at the centre before each Long bout. It decides Out in the Rope and the Last Fall.
- **The chalk:** the old ten-pace circle (30 ft across, 15 ft from the centre). It decides Out in the Short Open, the Collar and the Ash.
- **The rope fence:** about a pace beyond the chalk. Nothing scores out there; Rosa freezes the bout and restarts it at the centre.
- **The toe-lines:** two chalk ticks a pace apart at the centre, used for the Rush and every standing restart.
- **Footing:** packed ash and sand; normal footing; barefoot.

### Dress (the Long Open)
- **The smock:** a dye-works linen jacket, sleeves to the wrist, with belt loops at the hips. It's the grip for the Linen, the Knot and the Hawk.
- **The Strap:** a salt-hauler's heavy belt worn over the smock, with a loop round each thigh. It's the grip for the Push and the Belt. A rust sash marks the first-named wrestler and a blue sash the second.
- **Breeches** to mid-thigh with no pockets, bare legs and bare feet. Nails short, no rings, hair tied back, no oil.
- **The slops chest:** Rosa keeps one full of smocks and Straps. A wrestler too big for any of them, a seven-foot loxodon say, wrestles in a sail-cloth smock Nan Grell stitches in an hour.
- The Short Open needs only linen and bare feet.

### Officials
- **Rosa** is the referee. She starts and stops action and calls falls, positions, counts and yields.
- **Two judges** sit at opposite corners of the rope with a rust rag and a blue rag. By custom one is from Steamside (usually Annet Sallow) and one from the Ashward (an elder, if one is at the rail). A call stands when Rosa and at least one judge agree. The Short Open has no judges.
- **Pip Hollis** keeps the marks and the spoon-taps on the slate, **Nella Hollis** keeps the kettle, and **Mother Seld** waits on the step.

### Who wrestles
- **Size:** Medium and Small folk, under the standard grapple size rule. Rosa keeps no weight classes ("the ash has no scale"), in the tradition of the Push and the Hawk.
- **Age:** children under twelve wrestle only in the Ladle Pit. Over-forties may ask for **the Old Rope**, a Long bout with each ground one exchange shorter.
- **The knotted:** a wrestler with two years in the Knot, vouched for by Teo Varrow or a Keeper, may use the knotted list of locks (section 7).
- **Extra limbs** (day-12 ruling): a trunk or a prehensile tail is a hand. It may grip, lift and hold but never strike, and it gives no extra hold or action.
- **No shapes:** Beast and Hybrid forms are a change of shape and fall under the Fury Rule.

## 3. The moves (every form)
### Turns and exchanges
- A bout opens with a free **start**: the opening Grip, the Rush, the Strap or the Hawk. Then turns alternate. An **exchange** is one turn each, about ten heartbeats in the fiction.
- On your turn you may move (if nobody holds you) and take **one pit action**.
- Advantage never stacks, and advantage and disadvantage cancel.
- A natural 20 on a check isn't an automatic success. Bouts deal no damage.

### Standing (nobody holds you)
- **Grip** (collar tie, underhook, body lock, 2-on-1, Strap or smock grip): contested Strength (Athletics). The winner takes the hold: they're the grappler, and the other is grappled (speed 0). A Grip never puts anyone down. Ties re-grip.
- **Rush** (the charge): a Grip at a run. Win and you also drive them 5 ft toward the line. Lose and you're Overcommitted.
- **Sidestep** (answer to a Rush): your Dexterity (Acrobatics) against the rusher's Athletics. Win and they stumble 5 ft past you, Overcommitted, and they're Out if that carries them over the line.
- **Thrust** (open palms to the chest or shoulders; the Rope only): Shove out without a hold.
- **Shot** (single-leg, double-leg, ankle pick): your Athletics against their Athletics or Acrobatics. Win and you take the hold and they're down. Lose and you're caught in the Kneel with them holding you from above. No Grappler advantage.
- **Pull Guard** (standing; the Ash only): sit to the ash and wrap your legs round them. Your Acrobatics or Athletics against their Athletics; win and they're in your guard. Either way, the other wrestler scores 1.

### When you hold them
- **Throw** (any throw, trip, sweep, lift or carry; or a turn on a wrestler already down): contested Athletics, with the defender using Athletics or Acrobatics. Win and they're down, and you keep the hold. How much you win by decides what it's worth (the throw table). Lose by 10 or more and it's turned on you: you're the one who touches.
- **Shove out** (drive, force-out, Strap lift-out): contested Athletics. Win and they move 5 ft toward the line. In the Rope, holding the Strap, a win by 5 or more drives them 10 ft.
- **Tighten** (an extra limb, body triangle, tight waist, hooks in): a DC 12 Athletics check. Success gives them disadvantage on their next break-free only. Once per round, and it uses your action.
- **Hold** (ride, pin): keep what you have, and the count goes on.
- **Pass** (when you're on top in their guard): contested Athletics against their Athletics or Acrobatics. Win and you're past the legs, holding them down in side control.
- **Knee** (from side control): contested. Win and your knee is on their belly.
- **Climb** (from side control): contested. Win and you take the mount or the back. While you keep it, their break-free, sweep and Pull Guard all have disadvantage.
- **Lock or Strangle** (armbar, kimura, triangle, rear strangle; from guard, side control, the mount or the back): contested Athletics against their Athletics or Acrobatics. Win and they're **Caught**: on their next turn they must escape (the same contest) or yield, and Rosa may stop it first. Only in the Ash, and only from your list.
- **Release:** let go.

### When they hold you
- **Break free** (stand-up, sit-out, peel the grip): your Athletics or Acrobatics against their Athletics. Win and the hold ends and the count resets.
- **Counter** (single-leg, duck-under, arm drag, switch; from underneath, a sweep or reversal): contested Athletics, with no Grappler advantage. Win and they go down and you hold them.
- **Bridge** (when you're held down): a DC 12 Athletics or Acrobatics check. Rosa counts no finger this round, but you're still held.
- **Pull Guard** (held down on your back): instead of breaking free, wrap your legs round them, contested as above. In guard there's no count, and from guard you may sweep (a Counter), lock or strangle.
- **Yield:** two slaps of a hand on the ash, a slap of the foot, or a cry of "Ash!" ends the bout at once.

### The throw table
| Throw wins by | The Rope | The Collar and the Ash | The Short Open |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–4 | **Touch:** 1 mark | **Takedown:** 2 marks | Down, hold kept |
| 5–9 | **Fall:** 2 marks | **Big throw:** 4 marks | Down, hold kept |
| 10+ | **Clean fall:** 3 marks (never decisive) | **Full fall: win** | **Full fall:** Winded, 4 marks |
| Tie | **"Again!"** No mark; same start | No score | No score |

### The count
- At the end of the held wrestler's turn, if they're still held down and no break-free, Counter, Bridge or Pull Guard succeeded, Rosa counts a finger. **Three fingers is a pin.**
- A break-free or a Counter resets the count. A Bridge only pauses it.
- **Not down, not counted:** a wrestler held standing, or in the Kneel, is never counted.
- **In the Short Open,** face up or face down counts ("ash is ash"), and wrapped legs don't stop it (the Varrow ruling, 1486).
- **In the Long Open,** Rosa counts only a wrestler on their back or side, with the holder past the legs. Face down is ridden, not pinned, and guard or half guard is never a pin.

### Conditions
- **Grappler feat** (table rule, day 12): advantage on the checks you make to take or use a hold: Grip, Rush, Throw, Shove out, Tighten, Pass, Knee, Climb, Lock and Strangle, and when you defend your hold against a break-free. It doesn't apply to a Shot, a Counter, a break-free, a Bridge, Pull Guard, or resisting someone else's throw.
- **Down and free:** you have disadvantage on checks to resist a Grip, and standing costs half your movement.
- **Overcommitted:** you have disadvantage on your next check to resist, until the end of your next turn.
- **Winded** (after a Short Open full fall) and **Tightened:** you have disadvantage on your next break-free.
- **The Kneel:** on hands and knees. Not down, and never counted. The wrestler on top turns them with a Throw.

## 4. The Short Open (the afternoon bracket)
Wenna cut the Open to one quick bout in 1454 so the bracket finished before the curfew bell.
- **Win by:**
  - **Out:** any part of you over the chalk; a heel on the chalk is over it;
  - **Down:** three fingers;
  - **Yield;**
  - the other wrestler's **major foul**.
- **The kettle** sings at 20 exchanges. Then marks decide, and a tie goes to sudden ash (the next mark wins):

| Mark | Worth |
|---|---|
| Takedown | 2 |
| Full fall | 4 |
| Reversal | 2 |
| Escape | 1 |
| Each finger | 1 |
| The other's minor foul | 1 |

- **Legal:** every move in section 3 except Thrust, Pull Guard, Pass, Knee, Climb, Lock and Strangle.
- **Major fouls** lose the bout, and the stake goes to the tin:
  - biting;
  - ash, salt or sand to the face;
  - strikes, including chops and a trunk swung as a blow (an open palm to the chest is a push);
  - strangles and locks;
  - magic, the Rite, shapes, rage or fire;
  - help from the rail.
- **Minor fouls:** the first gives a mark to the other wrestler and a restart, and the second loses the bout:
  - hair, bending fingers, grabbing the rope;
  - backing out of reach for two turns without an attack.

## 5. The Long Open (the Seven Roots bout)
Three grounds, always in this order, with one running tally of marks and one ladder of spoon-taps. Nothing resets between grounds. Each ground starts standing at the toe-lines, with a breath and a cup in between.

| | The Rope | The Collar | The Ash | Last Fall |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Roots** | Push, Belt, Hawk | Gorget, Linen | Catch, Knot | Push |
| **Exchanges** | 3 | 4 | 5 (6 in the final) | up to 4 |
| **The Old Rope** | 2 | 3 | 4 | up to 4 |
| **Ends the bout** | nothing (falls only) | full fall, pin | yield, pin, full fall | first mark wins |

The order is deliberate: standing work comes first, while both wrestlers are fresh, and locks come last, so a Knot-man can't end it before the others have had their ground.

### Ways to win
| Way | When |
|---|---|
| Yield to a lock or strangle | the Ash |
| Pin (three fingers past the legs) | the Collar and the Ash |
| Full fall (a throw won by 10+) | the Collar and the Ash |
| **The long and short of it:** a 12-mark lead | any ground, at the next stoppage |
| Most marks | when the Ash's kettle sings |
| The Last Fall | tied after the Ash |
| The criteria | the Last Fall still unscored |
| A disqualifying foul, an injury, a forfeit, or Rosa's spoon | any time |

### Ground one: the Rope (Push, Belt, Hawk)
Standing only, wrestled in short **falls**. Each fall ends the instant someone leaves the salt ring or touches the ash with anything but their feet. Pip marks it, and both restart at the toe-lines.
- **Starts:** the first fall opens with a Rush. After that, whoever conceded the last fall picks the next start:
  - **the Rush** (Push): both crouch at the toe-lines with both fists on the ash and charge on Rosa's spoon (Rush or Sidestep);
  - **the Strap** (Push, Belt): chest to chest, right hand on the Strap at the back of the hip, left hand on the other's right thigh loop. Both are holding, so roll contested Athletics; the winner acts first as the grappler. No Sidestep;
  - **the Hawk** (Hawk): an arm's length apart with no grips, fighting for them (the opening Grip).
- **Marks** (see the throw table):
  - **Out** of the salt ring: 1;
  - **Touch** (a hand or one knee): 1;
  - **Fall** (an elbow, both knees, a hip or the back): 2;
  - **Clean fall** (thrown flat, with the thrower in control): 3. A clean fall never ends the bout in the Rope.
- **First touch loses the fall,** even if you were winning the exchange.
- **The dead-weight rule:** once they're falling past saving, your touch while finishing doesn't count. A Shot drops your knee to the ash and concedes a Touch, unless you win it by 5 or more.
- **Again!:** a tied throw means both touch together. No mark, and the same start again.
- **Allowed:** grips anywhere, legs included; throws, trips, hooks, lifts and carrying them out; Thrust.
- **Not allowed:**
  - groundwork of any kind (action stops at the first touch);
  - dropping your full weight on someone you've thrown (a spoon-tap);
  - driving anyone head-first (disqualification).
- **Posture** (from the Belt):
  - hunching over without attacking for a whole exchange draws a spoon-tap;
  - if both stand on grips without attacking for an exchange, Rosa calls **"Step!"**, and if it goes on, the less active wrestler is tapped;
  - backing to the salt line without engaging is a tap.
- A fall that starts before the kettle counts.

### Ground two: the Collar (Gorget, Linen)
This ground is about throwing. No hands below the Strap while standing, though the feet may sweep.
- **Legal grips:** the smock (collar, lapels, sleeves, back), the Strap's waist belt, arms, head-and-arm, body locks, underhooks and overhooks.
- **Illegal grips:** hand or arm on the legs, breeches or thigh loops while standing, so no Shots and no Counters that grab a leg. It's a spoon-tap, and any mark it won is struck off.
- **Legs may attack legs:** foot sweeps, reaps, hooks and trips are legal. It's the Gorget's one concession to the Linen.
- Sitting or pulling to the ash without a throw is a spoon-tap.
- **Standing marks:** push-out over the chalk 1, takedown 2, big throw 4, and a full fall **wins** (the throw table). A Counter that throws the holder scores the takedown for whoever ends on top; if neither does, nothing scores.
- **The ground window:** anything that takes a wrestler to the ash opens two exchanges of groundwork, and hands may go to the legs on the ground.
  - **Reversal** (from underneath to on top, in control): 1.
  - **Turn** (a Throw that rolls a downed wrestler's back toward the ash, such as a gut wrench, tilt or lift): 2 per turn.
  - **Hold-down:** Rosa's count past the legs. The **second finger** scores 2, and the third is a pin.
  - The window stays open while a count runs and closes when it breaks. If no count is running when it closes, Rosa restarts both standing.
- **No locks or strangles:** Rosa stops it and taps whoever tried.

### Ground three: the Ash (Catch, Knot)
Everything is legal: legs, groundwork, and the locks and strangles on your list.
- **Standing:** every grip except inside the breeches or sleeves. Push-out 1, takedown 2, big throw 4, and a full fall wins. Pull Guard from standing is legal, but the other wrestler scores 1. If both pull at once, both are tapped and restart standing.
- **On the ash:** a position scores when you take it, if they don't escape it on their next turn.
  - **Sweep or reversal** (a Counter from underneath or from guard): 2.
  - **Turn** (their back rolled toward the ash): 2.
  - **Knee on the belly:** 2.
  - **Pass** to side control: 3.
  - **Mount, or the back** with both hooks or a body triangle: 4.
  - **Pin** (three fingers past the legs): **win**. The second finger scores nothing here, since positions already pay.
  - **Yield** to a lock or strangle: **win**.
- **One action, one mark:** a move that fits two rows scores the higher.
- **No repeats:** a position scores again only after they've escaped it and you've taken it back.
- **Off the chalk on the ash:** Rosa freezes both and restarts them at the centre in the same position.
- **Fleeing:** leaving the chalk on purpose to escape a position or a lock gives the other wrestler 2.
- **Stalling:** if neither advances for two exchanges, Rosa calls **"Work!"**. If it goes on, it's a spoon-tap.

### The Last Fall
If the tally is level after the Ash, both wrestlers go back to the salt ring for one fall under Rope rules, opening with a Rush. The first mark of any kind wins, even a Touch, and the Last Fall is capped at four exchanges. If it's still unscored, Rosa and the judges decide by:
1. the highest-value single action in the bout;
2. fewer spoon-taps;
3. whoever scored last in the grounds;
4. a majority of Rosa and the judges.

## 6. Spoon-taps and disqualification (the Long Open)
Rosa marks a caution with a tap of her spoon on the wrestler's shoulder. One ladder runs through all three grounds.

| Tap | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1st | Warning |
| 2nd | The other wrestler scores 1 |
| 3rd | The other wrestler scores 2 |
| 4th | Disqualified |

| Ground | Tapped for |
|---|---|
| All | Stalling, dodging grips, false attacks, illegal grips, leaving the chalk to avoid contact, fixing your smock without leave, taunting |
| The Rope | Hunching, static grips after "Step!", hugging the salt line, dropping your weight on a thrown wrestler |
| The Collar | Hands below the Strap while standing, sitting or pulling down without a throw, trying a lock or strangle |
| The Ash | Double guard pull, stalling after "Work!", reaching for a lock that isn't on your list (without pressure) |

**Disqualified at once** (you lose the bout, and your stake goes to the burn-sick tin):
- strikes, except the Rope's open-palm thrusts to the chest and shoulders, including a trunk or tail swung as a blow;
- **slams** (lifting someone in your guard or in a lock and driving them into the ash) and **spikes** (landing anyone on the head or neck, or diving head-first to finish a throw);
- the scissor takedown;
- biting; going for the eyes or groin; pulling hair; fish-hooking; scratching; twisting ears or noses; gripping fewer than four fingers; gripping inside breeches or sleeves; smothering a face with the smock;
- ash, salt or sand to the face (the Anointing rule);
- putting pressure on a lock that isn't on your list, or holding a lock after the yield or Rosa's spoon;
- magic, the Rite, a change of shape, rage, burning or breathing fire (the Fury Rule);
- help from the rail;
- serious ill conduct toward a wrestler or an official.

## 7. The lists (locks and strangles; the Ash only)
| Lock or strangle | Green (everyone) | Knotted |
|---|---|---|
| Strangles: rear, collar, triangle, arm-triangle | Yes | Yes |
| Arm locks: armbar, kimura, americana, omoplata | Yes | Yes |
| Straight ankle lock | Yes | Yes |
| Wrist locks | No | Yes |
| Kneebar, toe hold, calf and biceps slicers | No | Yes |
| Heel hooks | No | No |
| Neck cranks and spine locks | No | No |

A tap of the hand or foot, a cry of "Ash!" or a cry of pain ends it. Rosa may stop any lock or strangle herself, and that counts as a yield. Wenna banned strangles from the Open after 1467, and they come back only here: in the Ash, with Rosa's spoon on the shoulder.

## 8. Reviews, injuries and manners
- **The huddle:** on a close fall in the Rope or the Last Fall, either judge may wave their rag to stop play and confer.
- **Ask Wenna:** once per bout, each corner may ask Old Wenna to rule on a call. She hears falls better than most people see them. If she rules against the corner that asked, the other wrestler scores 1.
- **Mother Seld's minute:** each wrestler may call her once per bout, with extra time to stop blood. A wrestler who can't go on loses, unless a foul hurt them, in which case they win.
- **Manners:**
  - salt and stamp before stepping in;
  - shake at the centre before the Rope and after the result (the Ash Rule);
  - Rosa raises the winner's hand. After the closing salute the winner may do their root's gesture: a hawk dance, a Push squat and clap, a dyer's bow. Taunting is a spoon-tap;
  - seconds sit on their barrel and speak only while the kettle is off.

## 9. Grudges and called bouts
- **A grudge:** two people with a quarrel bring it to Rosa before the Open. The one who brings it calls the bout: **long** (the Long Open), **short** (the Short Open) or in **one root**.
- **One root:** the root's home ground alone, with its own finish.
  - **The Push, the Belt or the Hawk:** one fall under Rope rules; the first mark wins.
  - **The Gorget or the Linen:** Collar rules for 5 exchanges.
  - **The Catch:** Ash rules for 6 exchanges, with no locks or strangles.
  - **The Knot:** Ash rules for 6 exchanges, with no pins.
- **After a grudge:** the loser drops the quarrel, and both soak side by side at the Long Soak.
- **The Champion's Grudge:** the champion names one person to wrestle them in the first bout of next year's Games, and the one named chooses the form (long, short or one root). From 1492 the Open's final is wrestled long.
- **No purse:** grudges carry only the rail's bets.

## 12. The circuit (from 1492)
See the-grudge-circuit for the calendar, seats, venues and prizes.
- **Qualifiers and the Open:** every bout is long. In a qualifier, the host root's home ground runs one exchange longer.
- **Rest:** in the Open, every wrestler rests at least one wave between bouts. The final follows an interval.
- **The relay Long Open** (the Quintet's knockout):
  - Each yard seals a line-up with Rosa: one wrestler for the Rope, one for the Collar, one for the Ash, one for the Last Fall, and a reserve. Each wrestler wrestles only their ground.
  - The tally carries across the swaps, and a pin, yield, full fall or 12-mark lead ends the whole relay.
  - Nobody wrestles the same ground in two rounds running.
- **Dual meets** (the Quintet's pools): five Short Open bouts, seat against seat. Team points are 6 for a pin, yield or full fall, and 3 for a win on marks.
- **Quick bouts** (DM only, for bouts between non-player wrestlers):
  - In each ground, each wrestler makes one contested check: Athletics, or Acrobatics where the Linen allows it. The margin sets the marks by the throw table.
  - In the Collar or the Ash, a margin of 10 or more is a finish.
  - A tie after the Ash goes to one contest for the Last Fall.
  - In the Short Open, one contest decides the bout, and a margin of 10 or more is a pin.

## 13. Rosa's day-12 rulings (in force)

- Not down, not counted: a wrestler on hands and knees isn't down.
