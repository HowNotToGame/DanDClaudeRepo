# Ashblood (the burn)
A contagious curse that bleeds out of the Heartcoal. It does not give joy; it gives **rage**. Hill-folk call it **the burn**, because the first sign is a fever-heat in the blood and a short fuse. Scholars of the Pyrehouse call it **Ashblood**.

## What people say
- It spreads like a disease: a soldier who guarded the Coal sickens, then his wife, then the man who bound his wounds.
- The sick run hot. Their eyes catch red in poor light and grey veins show at the wrists.
- Most of the weak who catch it die in a few days. Hill-folk call that "the burning-off."
- There is no cure. Priests of Kelemvor have tried. Some of the infected live whole lives by keeping their tempers cold.

## Rules (player-approved canon, v2)
**Source.** The Heartcoal, a sliver of the Lord of Murder's essence. Its overflow is the curse.

**Exposure (disease-like).** A creature is exposed by (1) touching the Heartcoal, (2) contact with the blood of someone who carries Ashblood, or (3) close contact with anyone who is, or recently was, in contact with the overflow (wounds, sharing a bed, tending the sick). Ash, wafers and smoke carry no risk by themselves.
- **Touching the Coal:** automatic infection, with the stages arriving fast (hours, not days).
- **Blood or close contact with a carrier:** CON save DC 12 (DC 10 if two steps removed). Fail: infected (the stages below).

**Stages (symptoms: bloodlust and rage, no joy). They affect everyone, in order.**
- **Stirring:** a hot temper. After a kill, or when dropped below half HP, WIS save DC 10 or fly into a rage for 1 minute. You can still choose to hold back.
- **Frenzied:** DC 13. On a failure you attack the nearest creature, friend or foe. Fits in combat; harder to hide.
- **Berserk:** the rage never leaves. No saves, no choices, and the host is effectively lost.
- A stage rises when you lose a rage save, or when you kill for the rage's sake; discipline, rest and calm can lower it by one stage per week.

**The final step (player-corrected canon, day 11).** Every infected creature passes through the stages in order. The **final step of Berserk is death**, but only for creatures of **class level 3 or lower, or CR 1/2 or lower**: they are the ones the overflow drowns. Creatures of **class level 4+ or CR 1 or higher** are not overwhelmed by it, so they do not die at the end of Berserk; they stay lost in the rage until something breaks it. Commoners and children are level 0, so they die at the end if the burn runs its course. Hill-folk call that "the burning-off."

**No cure, only management.** Hosts can hold the burn at Stirring through discipline, ritual and the hymns of the Choir. A Blood Hunter of the Banked Fire can bleed the rage off with a Vital Sacrifice: it drops the stage by one for 24 hours.

**If the Heartcoal is destroyed.** The overflow ends: no new infections. Existing hosts keep it for life. The Quenching still works, because Okton's blood already carries the burn.

## The Quenching (Okton's rite)
A non-deadly conversion for people who would otherwise die of the overflow, performed with Okton's blood. It makes **Blood Hunters** (the Banked).
- **Survival check:** d20 + character level (or CR) + **willingness** vs DC 18 - Okton's **craft rank** (minimum DC 8). Willing +10, wavering +4, unwilling -6. A natural 1 kills; a natural 20 always succeeds. Willingness matters far more than level.
- **Craft rank:** Okton begins at 1. Each survivor teaches him; rank rises by 1 per three survivors (max 10).
- **Cost to Okton:** each Quenching permanently costs him 1 maximum hit point and one year of his life. Tiny, but it adds up across a large order.
- **Result:** the recruit lives as an infected host at Stirring, but with Banked advantages (see the Order's entry).
- **Willingness by state (player-approved):** Stirring or Frenzied hosts are fully willing (+10). **Hosts lost in the rage (Berserk) use a separate roll:** d20 + their level (or CR); **10 or higher converts them**, lower means they die. This replaces the willingness table for them; Okton still waits for a lucid moment and a yes before attempting it. Unwilling non-Berserk recruits use -6 as above.

## Known history
- The burn is old. Hask's soldiers carried it for decades, and the Gallowsbird and the late king were its worst cases.
- Okton, a fire giant, carries it at a controlled Stirring. He has held it level for decades.
- Kitwin is infected in secret (see his entry).
- **Retcon note:** earlier drafts called this "the gladness" and had it give joy in death. That was removed.
