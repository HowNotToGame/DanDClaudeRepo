# Ashblood (the gladness)
A slow taint, not a fever. Hill-folk call it **the gladness** because its first sign is a smile at the wrong time: a shepherd who grins as a lamb dies, or a soldier who laughs over a corpse and can't say why. Scholars of the Pyrehouse call it **Ashblood** and say little else in public.

## What people say
- The infected feel warm when something dies near them. At first the warmth is pleasant. Later it becomes necessary.
- In dim light their eyes catch red like coals, and after a kill their veins can show grey at the wrists.
- They seek out fires, pyres, battlefields and deathbeds. They don't fear death much, their own or anyone else's.
- It passes through blood, from the bite or wound of an infected creature. Some claim it also comes from the ash and smoke of the royal pyres. Others blame carrion birds, bad water or the gods' displeasure.
- There is no known cure. Priests of Kelemvor have tried prayer. Some of the infected are strong enough to master it for years.

## Known carriers
- **The Gallowsbird**, the worst of all.
- **Okton** the fire giant, mildly. He has spent years looking for a way to beat it.
- Whispered: a few veteran soldiers, a Pyrehouse acolyte or two, and (the gossip says) the late king himself.

## DM secrets
- **What it really is:** the overflow of the Heartcoal's hunger. The coal drinks the deaths of those killed in the crown's name, and what it can't hold leaves as a taint in the ashes of the dead. The Pyrewardens burned the king's victims and, as a royal rite, dusted the condemned at the Sky Gallows with that pyre-ash (the "Ash Anointing"). The gladness could pass to anything that ate those bodies, breathed the ash deeply for years, or took a carrier's blood into its own. At root it is a sliver of the Lord of Murder's essence (see heartcoal), diluted almost to nothing in each host, but never quite to nothing.
- **Stages (homebrew narrative track):**
  1. **Touched:** warmth and a shiver of pleasure at death, easily hidden. Okton spent most of the last 38 years here. Lorard is faintly here after battles, and so is Watch-Captain Halda Barrowe. Every hunter of Okton's order is Touched from the moment of quenching, though tempered toward fury rather than joy (see okton-s-hunters-name-pending).
  2. **Kindled:** craves death and finds joy in killing; ember-eyes; little fear. Okton is on the edge of this now. Kitwin is here. The late king was here for decades.
  3. **Glad:** death is the only joy left, and the mind narrows to it. The Gallowsbird is Glad, and Kitwin is close.
- **The Ember Law's dirty secret:** the coal kindles only for a Kindled or Glad bearer. The "rightful king" the Coal chooses is always the most death-soaked claimant in the room.
- **Ending it:** destroying the Heartcoal ends all Ashblood. Touched hosts are freed and grieve the lost warmth like addicts. Kindled hosts survive hollowed and scarred, and some won't want to live. Glad hosts die where they stand. That means the Gallowsbird, very likely Kitwin, and Okton if the hunters he has made have carried him that far. It also ends the hunters' gift (see okton-s-hunters-name-pending). This victory always costs something.
- **Barbarian hook (no mechanics needed):** if Chadickith rages within 30 ft of the coal, describe the warmth and how good it feels. *Suggested homebrew, if you want it to have teeth:* after any long rest in which he carried the coal and killed something, he makes a DC 10 Wisdom save or moves a step toward Touched. Tell the player only what he feels.
