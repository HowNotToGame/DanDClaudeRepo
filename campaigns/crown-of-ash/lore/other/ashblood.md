# Ashblood (the burn)
A contagious curse that bleeds out of the Heartcoal. It does not give joy; it gives **rage**. Hill-folk call it **the burn**, because the first sign is a fever-heat in the blood and a short fuse. Scholars of the Pyrehouse call it **Ashblood**.

## What people say
- It spreads like a disease: a soldier who guarded the Coal sickens, then his wife, then the man who bound his wounds.
- The sick run hot. Their eyes catch red in poor light and grey veins show at the wrists.
- There is no cure. Priests of Kelemvor have tried. Some of the infected live whole lives by keeping their tempers cold.
- Since the Gallowsbird's attack, Kharun has seen it up close: the roc's blood carried it into the wounded on the night of the Great Pyre.

## How the burn runs (player-approved canon)
- **Source:** the Heartcoal. Its overflow is the curse.
- **Exposure is disease-like.** A creature is exposed by touching the Heartcoal, by contact with the blood of someone who carries the burn, or by close contact with anyone who is, or recently was, in contact with the overflow (wounds, sharing a bed, tending the sick). Ash, wafers and smoke carry no risk by themselves. Touching the Coal infects outright. Blood or close contact with a carrier calls for a CON save, DC 12 (DC 10 if two steps removed); on a failure the creature is infected.
- **Everyone infected passes through the stages in order:**
  - **Stirring:** a hot temper. After a kill, or when dropped below half HP, a WIS save (DC 10) or a rage for 1 minute. You can still choose to hold back.
  - **Frenzied:** DC 13. On a failure you attack the nearest creature, friend or foe. Fits in combat; harder to hide.
  - **Berserk:** the rage never leaves. No saves, no choices.
  - A stage rises when you lose a rage save, or when you kill for the rage's sake. Discipline, rest and calm can lower it by one stage a week.
- **The end:** the weak (common folk, children, green soldiers; in game terms class level 3 or lower, or CR 1/2 or lower) die at the end of Berserk. Okton says the lost ones fail within a few days if the rage never lets go. The strong never die of it, but stay lost in the rage.
- **No cure, only management.** Hosts can be held at Stirring by discipline and by bleeding the heat off. A Blood Hunter of the Banked Fire can bleed the rage off with a Vital Sacrifice, dropping the stage by one for 24 hours.
- **If the Heartcoal is destroyed:** the overflow ends, so there are no new infections. Existing hosts keep the burn for life, and the Quenching still works, because Okton's blood already carries it.
- **The exact Quenching rules** (survival, costs, children, convert races, beasts) are in the campaign's locked rules block.

## The Quenching (Okton's rite)
Okton's conversion for people the burn would otherwise kill or lose, performed with his own blood. It makes **Blood Hunters**, the Banked (see order-of-the-banked-fire). Willingness matters far more than strength. A host lost in the rage can still be converted, but Okton waits for a lucid moment and a clear yes. Each Quenching costs Okton something he does not get back. The recruit lives as a host at Stirring, with the Banked's advantages.

## Known history
- The burn is old. Hask's soldiers carried it for decades, and the Gallowsbird and the late king were its worst cases.
- Okton, a fire giant, carries it at a controlled Stirring. He has held it level for decades.
- **After the Gallowsbird (night of day 2):** of about forty people wounded by the roc, nineteen died of the burn fever within a night, and seven Watchmen were confined raging in the Collarhouse cellar. Isine Kettle kept the rest apart and washed wounds with brine. Okton quenched five of the seven; Joris Marsh and Gwenet Marlow are still lost.
- **The forge ward (from day 10):** a trial ward of three cots in Okton's cellar, under Watch protection, for the burn-afflicted who come with their family's consent. Its first patients are an Ashward family, the Cobbs (see gwenmund-cobb).
- **Retcon notes:** earlier drafts called this "the gladness" and had it give joy in death; that was removed. On day 11 the player corrected the death rule: the weak no longer die on exposure, but at the end of Berserk.

## DM secrets
- **The source** is a sliver of the Lord of Murder's essence inside the Heartcoal (see heartcoal).
- **Kitwin is infected,** an anomaly: he should have died as a boy, and lived because he was willing. The Choir holds him near Frenzied with its hymns (see kitwin).
- **Isine Kettle is infected** at Stirring. She touched the Coal and lived because she meant it; she is level 0.
- **The Choir's wafers** infect whoever eats them and tether the eater's rage to the Choir's hymn (see pyrewardens).
- **Lorard is not infected.** Battlefield pyres and ash carry no burn.
- **Open timing question:** the nineteen who died within a night of the roc predate the day-11 correction. The locked rules do not fix how fast the burn runs to its end. Decide it when it matters (a heavy dose of roc's blood is one answer), and record it.
