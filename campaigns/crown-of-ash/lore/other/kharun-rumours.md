# Rumours in Kharun (Highharvestide 1491 DR)
Hand these out in taverns, bathhouses, Watch barracks and bread queues.

1. "The king didn't die of age. The princess's Calishite friends poisoned him. Her taster hasn't been seen since."
2. "Ask any old Ash Company veteran about Pellot Rook and watch them go quiet. The Lord-Marshal walks just like him, they say, and it isn't the king's walk."
3. "The Gallowsbird's been over the Lowfields every evening since the hangings stopped. It circles like it's looking for something."
4. "The Pyre-Born boy sits with the dying, and every one of them dies smiling. Every one."
5. "The old giant at the Vents made the Crown of Ash with his own hands. Ask him what he put in it."
6. "Whoever holds the Coal can't be lied to. That's why the heirs are so desperate."
7. "The giant's been bleeding into his quench-tubs. Steamside says he's making something that isn't armour."
8. "The Sky Gallows is older than the Ash King. The princess's great-grandfather built it. Funny how she never mentions that."

## Rumours now (day 11)
9. "The giant makes monsters of the sick. Don't let them take your children to that forge."
10. "The Coal was never stolen from the Pyrehouse. It was in the giant's forge to keep it out of the singers' hands, and now it's gone where they can't reach it. The elders saw the scorch-mark."
11. "The old king's last words were 'Break it.' He never named the boy at all."
12. "Captain Rook sold the Watch to the Pyrehouse. That's why Halda took his collar."
13. "The Lord-Marshal put his own quartermaster in irons for paying a mob to burn the forge."
14. "They're bringing the Pyre-Born boy before the lamps on the morning of the first sitting. First anyone's seen him since the bird."

## DM secrets
- **1, partly true.** Oselle did poison him with Calishite pale tincture, but that isn't what killed him. The taster is at the bottom of the harbour.
- **2, true.** Lorard is Pellot Rook's son. Two veterans who remember are still alive in the Ash Companies. Since day 11, Chadickith, Captain Marda Ironside and Hesard Rook (Pellot's nephew) know it for certain; Marda's written record reaches Halda on day 14.
- **3, true at Highharvestide.** The roc was starving and drawn to the loose coal. It is dead now (night of day 2).
- **4, true**, and worse than it sounds (see kitwin).
- **5, true.** Chadickith now knows: Okton admitted forging the crown and setting the coal (session 1).
- **6, false.** Pure invention, but people will act on it.
- **7, true.** It's the Quenching, the ritual that makes the Banked (see order-of-the-banked-fire). Whoever hears this first starts asking questions at the forge.
- **8, true.** King Uzhar Velun raised it around 1401. The rumour doesn't know the worse part: why he built it, and what it did to Hadrun in 1423 (see oselle-velun).
- **9, a twisted truth.** The Choir's minders spread it from day 10. Okton's Quenching does make Blood Hunters of the burn-sick, but only with a lucid, free yes, a family's consent and a warden's witness.
- **10, true.** The Ashward elders who searched the forge on day 9 are spreading it. The Coal is in the Dunmarrow vault.
- **11, true.** Isine's testimony at the noon council of day 10. It is leaking from the leaders' households.
- **12, true enough.** Rook fed the Choir news because Coldwell promised to back his cousin Lorard for the crown (see hesard-rook).
- **13, true.** Unara sent Ashlo to make a spark at the forge (dawn, day 10). Lorard really did not know.
- **14, true.** Coldwell agreed to it on day 11 (see lamplit-council).
