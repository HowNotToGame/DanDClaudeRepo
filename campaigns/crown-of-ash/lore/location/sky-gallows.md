# The Sky Gallows
A black basalt spire two hundred feet tall, three miles south of Kharun at the end of the overgrown Gallows Road. A switchback stair cut into the rock climbs to a flat top ringed with iron posts and heavy chains. From 1458 to 1490 DR the realm's condemned were chained up there, dusted with pyre-ash by a Pyrewarden, and left "for the sky to judge." The sky usually sent the Gallowsbird.

- The stair is cracked and the chains are rusted. The roc tore several away. Bones lie in the scree below.
- Locals won't go near it. Shepherds say the roc still lands there to wait.
- **Things to do:** find a Velun Plot noble's last carved words; discover that the Ash Anointing was a ritual, not mere custom; bait the bird; or hide something where no one will look.

## DM secrets
- Near the top is an inscription scratched in 1472 by a condemned Pyrewarden acolyte: *"The ash is not for the dead. The ash is for the bird."* It is the first clue that the anointing was deliberate.
- Fresh death will lure the roc here. It is the one place a party could set a trap for it, with Okton and his hunters. Hunters can feel it coming.
