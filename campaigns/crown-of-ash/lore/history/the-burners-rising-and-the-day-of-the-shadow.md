# The Burners' Rising and the Day of the Shadow (1423 DR)

## Timeline
| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| c. 1405 | The roc is first seen over the Gallows Spire. |
| 1415 | Chadickith is born in Hadrun. |
| 1421 | A bad winter. King Uzhar doubles the **fuel levy** on the hill charcoal-burners, whose charcoal feeds the Pyrehouse fires and the vents. |
| Early 1423 | **The Burners' Rising.** Charcoal-burners and shepherds under a halfling charcoal-boss, **Verina Greenbottle**, refuse the levy and burn two toll-houses on the Gallows Road. |
| Early spring 1423 | The Watch crushes the Rising in three weeks. Greenbottle is hanged in Kharun. **Fifty of the condemned** go to the Sky Gallows in a single tenday, each anointed with pyre-ash by the Pyrewardens. |
| Spring 1423 | **The Day of the Shadow.** The roc, circling the Spire every day that month, comes down on Hadrun, the nearest village to its eyrie. About sixty die. Okton, riding patrol, reaches the burning square, splits its beak and scorches its wing, and it flees. |
| Spring 1423 | Okton carries the orphaned boy Chadickith, eight years old, to Kharun and raises him at his forge. |
| After | The Roc Bell is hung on the Hadrun ridge. Survivors raise a charcoal-stone giant at the village edge. |

## What the survivors say
- "The bird came for the burners." Some say the Rising brought the roc down as punishment; others that the king sent it.
- The statue of the giant: offerings from some, spit from others.

## DM secrets
- **The roc was not punishing the Rising.** The Anointing's fifty feeds were the most the coal had taken in one tenday, and the roc, which drinks what the coal drinks, went into a frenzy. See hadrun for the "fat and wrong" letter.
- Greenbottle's iron token lies in the nest at Old Wound (see gallowsbird).
- Uzhar blamed the villagers for provoking the bird and cancelled the next levy. It was his only act of mercy that decade.
- 1423 is the root of Okton's guilt toward Chadickith, though he was not infected then (see okton).
