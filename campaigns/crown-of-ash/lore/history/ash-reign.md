# The Ash Reign (1453–1491 DR)

## The Velun twilight (before the Ash Reign)
| Year (DR) | Event |
|---|---|
| c. 1394 | Uzhar Velun is crowned in Kharun. |
| c. 1401 | King Uzhar raises the **Sky Gallows** on the Gallows Spire. The Pyrewardens begin the "Ash Anointing" of the condemned. |
| c. 1405 | The roc is first seen over the Spire. |
| c. 1410 | Okton, a fire giant smith, takes the vent-forge in Steamside and begins armouring Kharun's guard. |
| 1415 | Chadickith is born in Hadrun to the loxodons Berov and Radanya. |
| 1423 | **The Burners' Rising** is crushed, and fifty condemned go to the Spire in a single tenday. The roc destroys most of **Hadrun**. Okton drives it off and takes in the eight-year-old orphan Chadickith. |
| 1431 | Tazhar Velun succeeds his father. |
| c. 1435 | Princess Ylia Velun is born. |

## The Ash Reign
| Year (DR) | Event |
|---|---|
| c. 1449 | King Tazhar hires the Ash Company under Ivoard Hask to break the raiders of the South March. |
| 1450 | Lorard is born in the Ash Company's camp. |
| 1453 | **Night of Cinders:** Hask turns on his employer, burns the old Velun palace, kills Tazhar and his sons, and takes Ylia as his queen. He is crowned with the Crown of Ash, forged by a giant smith, and proclaims the **Ember Law**. |
| 1454 | Hask reorganises Kharun's guard as the Kharun Watch and rebuilds the walls. The Pyrewardens are made sole keepers of the dead. |
| 1456 | Oselle is born. |
| 1458 | Hask expands the Sky Gallows from seasonal executions to monthly ones. |
| 1472 | Kitwin is born to the Pyrehouse acolyte Doret. |
| 1474 | **The Velun Plot** is crushed, and forty condemned go to the Spire in a single tenday. |
| 1480 | Queen Ylia dies, officially of fever. |
| 1484 | **The Thornwater Tithe:** Lorard hangs one man in ten of rebellious Thornwater. |
| 1489 | Mara, Lorard's mother, dies at Fort Vell. |
| 1490 (Flamerule) | **The Quiet Year** begins: the king halts all executions. |
| 1491, 26 Eleint | King Ivoard dies. The Heartcoal vanishes, and the Watch seals the gates. |
| 1491, Highharvestide | The king burns on the Great Pyre. Session 1 opens here. Chadickith is 76. |

## The interregnum (campaign days)
| Day | Event |
|---|---|
| 1 | The king burns. The cupbearer Isine Kettle is knifed at the pyre; Chadickith carries her to safety and learns what she carried. |
| 2 | **The Great Pyre rite:** Kitwin is crowned with grains in the empty socket; the crown kindles after a killing on the dais. **The Gallowsbird** attacks Pyre Square; about a hundred die; Okton kills it. Chadickith dies and is quenched. |
| 3 | The Watch's captains rule that the Coal did not choose Kitwin. Okton quenches five raging Watchmen: the Order of the Banked Fire gains its first company. |
| 4–5 | The Order finds the Choir's leak in the Watch (Captain Hesard Rook) and smuggles the Heartcoal out by the Saltgate postern. |
| 7–8 | The Order's runners reach the high Cinder Hills and put the Coal beyond the Choir's reach. |
| 9 | The gates open at noon on Halda's terms; three hundred of Lorard's men march in. At dusk the Choir's crowd at Okton's forge turns against the Choir. |
| 10 | Lorard's dawn search of the forge ends with his quartermaster in irons. At the **noon council**, Isine gives the king's true last words, and the **Lamplit Council** is adopted as regency. A trial burn ward opens at the forge. |
| 11 | The Choir marks the ward's door; Halda puts it under Watch protection. Captain Rook confesses to the Lord-Marshal in his cell. The first sitting of the Lamplit Council is two days off. |

## How people remember it
- Old folk in the Lamp Market: "Say what you like, the roads were safe and the scales were honest."
- The Ashward: "He fed us to a bird."
- The army: "He made us. Then he exiled the one son of his worth anything."
- The nobility: "Thirty-eight years under a sellsword's boot."
- The very old, quietly: "The Velun built the Gallows. Hask just kept it busy."

## DM secrets
- **c. 1398:** King Uzhar Velun bought Yaga-Shura's heart-coal from a Bhaalist fugitive, who went on to found the Pyrewardens' Glad Choir. The coal lay in a cold-iron reliquary in the Pyrehouse crypt for 55 years as "the Last Ember." The Sky Gallows and the Ash Anointing were built to feed it (see heartcoal).
- The Ember Law was Hask's own invention, not an ancient tradition, and the public believes Hask brought the coal. After 38 years, most people think the Law is as old as the hills.
- The Glad Choir backed Hask because King Tazhar fed the coal too sparingly. Every turning point since was a feeding: the Night of Cinders, the Velun Plot, the Thornwater revolt. The Choir quietly encouraged each one.
- Okton was not infected by the roc in 1423. He was infected when he set the coal in the crown in 1453. His blood ritual, which makes the Banked, came only in the last few years (see okton and order-of-the-banked-fire).
- **Days 7–8 (secret):** the Coal was sealed in the vault under Dunmarrow's keep (see heartcoal).
- **Day 11 (secret):** Rook's confession, overheard by Chadickith and written down by Marda Ironside, records that Lorard is Pellot Rook's son. Marda gives it to Halda on day 14 unless Chadickith intervenes.
