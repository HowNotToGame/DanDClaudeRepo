# The Ash Reign (1453–1491 DR)

| Year (DR) | Event |
|---|---|
| c. 1449 | Tazhar, the last Velun king, hires the Ash Company under Ivoard Hask to break the raiders of the South March. |
| 1450 | Lorard is born in the Ash Company's camp. |
| 1453 | **Night of Cinders:** Hask turns on his employer, burns the old Velun palace and takes Ylia Velun as his queen. He is crowned with the Crown of Ash, forged by a giant smith, and proclaims the **Ember Law**. |
| 1454 | The Kharun Watch is founded and Kharun's walls are rebuilt. The Pyrewardens are made sole keepers of the dead. |
| 1456 | Oselle is born. |
| 1458 | First executions at the **Sky Gallows**. |
| c. 1460 | The roc is first seen over the Gallows Spire. |
| 1472 | Kitwin is born to the Pyrehouse acolyte Doret. |
| 1474 | **The Velun Plot** is crushed, and forty condemned go to the Spire in a single tenday. The roc destroys most of **Hadrun**. Okton drives it off and takes in the orphan Chadickith. |
| 1480 | Queen Ylia dies, officially of fever. |
| 1484 | **The Thornwater Tithe:** Lorard hangs one man in ten of rebellious Thornwater. |
| 1489 | Mara, Lorard's mother, dies at Fort Vell. |
| 1490 (Flamerule) | **The Quiet Year** begins: the king halts all executions. |
| 1491, 26 Eleint | King Ivoard dies. The Heartcoal vanishes, and the Watch seals the gates. |
| 1491, Highharvestide | The king burns on the Great Pyre. |

## How people remember it
- Old folk in the Lamp Market: "Say what you like, the roads were safe and the scales were honest."
- The Ashward: "He fed us to a bird."
- The army: "He made us. Then he exiled the one son of his worth anything."
- The nobility: "Thirty-eight years under a sellsword's boot."

## DM secrets
- The Ember Law was Hask's own invention, not an ancient tradition. After 38 years, most people think it's as old as the hills.
- Every turning point of the reign was a feeding: the Night of Cinders, the Velun Plot, the Thornwater revolt. The Pyrewardens' Glad Choir quietly encouraged each one.
- Okton forged the crown in 1453, and his ritual for making hunters came only in the last few years (see okton and okton-s-hunters-name-pending).
