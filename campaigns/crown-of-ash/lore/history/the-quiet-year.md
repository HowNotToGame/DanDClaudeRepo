# The Quiet Year (Flamerule 1490 - Eleint 1491 DR)

## Timeline
| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| Flamerule 1490 | Without warning, Hask halts all executions in Ossmaar. The Sky Gallows empties; the condemned are held. The anniversary kindling of the crown is quietly skipped. |
| Autumn 1490 | The king is seen less and less. The court says he is ill. The prisons fill and the Pyrehouse is idle. |
| Winter 1490-91 | The Gallowsbird is seen over the Cinder Hills and the Lowfields, and twice above Kharun's walls. The Roc Bell at Hadrun rings. |
| Spring 1491 | Grain prices rise and the brimstone barges run light. The Lamp Market says the king has gone soft, or found religion. |
| Summer 1491 | Hask keeps to his chambers with his cupbearer Isine Kettle. |
| 26 Eleint 1491 | The king dies in the night. |

## How people read it
- The Lamp Market: "He was ill." The nobility: "He was afraid of the Velun." The Ashward: "He had a dream." The army: "He'd done with killing."
- The Pyrewardens call it a time of mercy and rest.

## DM secrets
- **It was Hask's attempt to starve the coal** he had at last understood. Unfed, it burned him from within (see ivoard-hask). The Quiet Year is also when the coal kindled in secret for Kitwin, by the Choir's design.
- The Choir wanted the executions back and began, in the Quiet Year, to feed the coal in a small way through the "gladly going" wafers given to Ashward paupers (see pyrewardens). Hask never knew.
- The Gallowsbird, starved of anointed flesh after nearly ninety years, roamed. Its death on the night of day 2 closes the thread.
- Hask's last act of the Quiet Year, the order "Break it" to his cupbearer, is the key to the interregnum.
