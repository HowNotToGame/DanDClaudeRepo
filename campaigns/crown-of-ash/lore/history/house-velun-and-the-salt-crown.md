# House Velun and the Salt Crown (c. 1190-1453 DR)
Ossmaar's ruling house for about three centuries. Dates before 1394 are chancery tradition and fuzzy; the Ash Reign page (ash-reign) has the fixed dates from Uzhar onward.

## Timeline
| Year (DR) | Event |
|---|---|
| c. 1190 | **Rheaot Velun**, a salt-reeve who farmed the tolls on the brimstone barges, is acclaimed "Reeve of the Shore" by the barge-guilds after he hangs a corrupt tax-farmer on the Kharun quay. The family keeps the title "Reeve" long after it becomes "King". |
| c. 1240 | The Grey Cough empties the lower town; the first wardens of the dead take the Kelemvor oath (see pyrewardens-origins). Velun grants them the pyre-ground that becomes the Pyrehouse. |
| c. 1290 | King Dhoram Velun (the third of the name-line to wear the **Salt Crown**, a circlet of white salt-glass and iron) walls the Ember Keep and takes the Lowfields from the hill-lords by marriage and debt. Kharun becomes the only walled city. |
| c. 1340-1390 | Slow decay: the brimstone tolls are farmed out, the hill-lords drift away, and Calishite houses start to lend to the crown. |
| c. 1394 | **Uzhar Velun** is crowned in Kharun, a hard, frightened man with a ruined treasury. |
| c. 1398 | Uzhar secretly acquires the Last Ember (see heartcoal; DM secrets below). |
| c. 1401 | He raises the **Sky Gallows** and, with the Pyrewardens, begins the Ash Anointing. |
| 1423 | The Burners' Rising and the destruction of Hadrun (see burners-rising-1423). |
| 1431 | **Tazhar Velun** succeeds his father. |
| c. 1435 | Princess Ylia Velun is born. |
| c. 1449 | Tazhar hires the Ash Company under Ivoard Hask. |
| 1453 | The **Night of Cinders**: Hask burns the old palace and the dynasty ends. Hask has the Salt Crown melted down. |

## How the Velun are remembered
- In the Lamp Market: grudging respect for the Reeve-kings who kept the barge scales honest.
- In the hills and the Ashward: tithes, fuel levies and the Spire. "The Velun built the Gallows. Hask just kept it busy."
- Oselle Velun, great-granddaughter of Uzhar, is the last of the old blood who holds anything.

## DM secrets
- Rheaot's line is real but much of the grandeur was manufactured by court chroniclers under Dhoram. The Velun were never mighty; they were good at being owed money.
- **Uzhar's desperation was the root.** He bought the Last Ember in 1398 from a Bhaalist fugitive because the Last Ember was said to make a king "unkillable in his own hall" and he had survived two assassination attempts. It kept him hale to a great age. The Sky Gallows fed it.
- Tazhar, his son, was shown the Ember at sixteen as every heir was. He disliked the Anointing and fed the coal sparingly, which is why the Glad Choir sold him out to Hask.
- Oselle does not know her line brought the coal to Kharun (see okton, ember-law). The Ember Rolls in the Pyrehouse crypt carry Uzhar's and Tazhar's seals.
