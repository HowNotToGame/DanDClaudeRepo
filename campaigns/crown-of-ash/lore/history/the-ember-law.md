# The Ember Law (1453-1491 DR)

## What everyone knows
- **Proclaimed by Ivoard Hask in 1453 DR** the day he was crowned: no one may wear the Crown of Ash unless the Coal kindles for them. The High Pyrewarden sets the crown on the claimant's head before the Great Pyre, and the Coal either kindles or does not.
- It stood for 38 years. The Watch swore (1454) to "keep the peace until the Coal chooses." Oaths, deeds, even marriage-bonds in Kharun came to be dated "by the Law".
- Nobody living remembers a coronation without it; common folk think it is as old as the hills. Chancery clerks know better: before Hask, the Velun kings were crowned with the Salt Crown and the Pyrewardens' blessing, and nothing was said of a coal.
- Under the Law, the Heartcoal's disappearance on 26 Eleint 1491 left the realm without a legal king, which is the root of the interregnum (see ash-reign).

## DM secrets
- **The Law was Hask's invention,** designed by the Glad Choir to make the coal the only source of authority, and to bind the realm's loyalty to what the Choir kept. Hask believed it was his own idea.
- **Earlier custody (c. 1398-1453).** The coal lay in a cold-iron **reliquary** in the Pyrehouse crypt, shown to each Velun heir at sixteen as "the Last Ember." It was fed through the Ash Anointing and the Sky Gallows from c. 1401. Only the king, the heir and the Choir's leader knew. In 1453, Okton melted the reliquary's iron into the crown band.
- **The Law was never a test of the coal's choice.** The coal kindles for whoever it can feed on and is fed by: Hask, then in secret during the Quiet Year Kitwin. The Choir can fake a glow with the casket's grains (see crown-of-ash).
- A ruling that broke the Law by fiat would not require the Coal at all: see the "option nobody has taken" in heartcoal.
