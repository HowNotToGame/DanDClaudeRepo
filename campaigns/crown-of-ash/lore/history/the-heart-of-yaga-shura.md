# The Heart of Yaga-Shura (1369-1491 DR)

## What is known
Nothing of this is known in Ossmaar. The wider world remembers the **Bhaalspawn Crisis** of 1369 DR in Tethyr: the siege of Saradush, where the children of the dead god of murder gathered, among them a fire giant warlord with a host of his kind. Border Kingdoms traders retell it as a distant tavern tale. Nobody in Ossmaar has linked it to the Coal.

## Timeline
| Year (DR) | Event |
|---|---|
| 1369 | **Saradush.** The fire giant Bhaalspawn Yaga-Shura leads a host against the city. He has cut out his own heart and keeps it wrapped in magical flame. Okton, a young warrior of the host, deserts before the end. |
| 1369 | Yaga-Shura falls and the heart is quenched, but a coal of Bhaal's essence survives in the ashes. |

| 1453 | Hask has Okton forge the Crown of Ash for it. |
| 1491 | The Coal is pried free. |

## DM secrets
- **The Heartcoal is the burned-out heart of Yaga-Shura** (see heartcoal for its properties). The wrapping fire was fire-priest craft, the same craft Okton built the Quenching from.
- **Okton deserted Yaga-Shura's host,** which is why he has no ordning. He recognised the heart in 1453 and has kept it from Chadickith since. **Okton knows; Chadickith does not.** How Okton tells him, or whether Chadickith learns it from the Coal itself, is the campaign's quiet reveal.
- Strakeln sold only the coal and kept a ring of the old flame-wrapping; DM option: it surfaces in the Choir's hands.
- The heart's pulse is why the coal "knows its king." It is hungry because the god behind it has risen again.
- Because the burn follows fire giant fire, only a fire giant's forge fed with a fire giant's own heart's blood can unmake it. Okton, a deserter who once served the warlord, is the only one who can.
