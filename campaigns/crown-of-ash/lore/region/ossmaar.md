# Ossmaar
A sliver of a kingdom in the Border Kingdoms: about sixty miles of coast along the south shore of the Lake of Steam, running perhaps forty miles inland to the Cinder Hills, with some 40,000 people. Like most Border Kingdoms realms, it is younger than its oldest families remember. House Velun ruled it from Kharun for three centuries. For the last 38 years it belonged to the sellsword-king Ivoard Hask.

## Geography
- **The Ossar Shore:** black sand, sulphur crust and hissing vents where the Lake of Steam runs hot and undrinkable. Convicts and debtors work the brimstone pits (the "Yellow Pits"). The capital, Kharun, sits on basalt terraces above the shore.
- **The Lowfields:** the farmland belt behind Kharun, with terraced olives, vines and barley watered by cold springs. The army is camped here now.
- **Thornwater:** the largest river town in the east and the grain market. It still remembers the Thornwater Tithe (1484 DR).
- **The Cinder Hills:** old, dead volcanic cones inland, with goat-scrub, charcoal-burners and shepherd villages such as Hadrun. The roc's eyrie is somewhere in the high cones.
- **The Gallows Spire:** a basalt needle three miles south of Kharun, where the condemned were chained for the sky (see sky-gallows).
- **The South March:** a line of small forts facing the rest of the Border Kingdoms and the Shaar beyond, held by the Ash Companies. Fort Vell is the largest.

## Economy and people
Brimstone, alum and salt go out across the Lake of Steam, mostly to Calishite buyers. Grain, timber and luxuries come in. Invalids come for the hot-spring cures at Kharun's vents. Most people are humans of mixed Tethyrian, Calishite and Chondathan stock. There are also halflings, half-orcs (many descended from old mercenary companies) and a scattering of rarer folk. The old hill-tongue survives in place-names: Kharun, Hadrun, Ossar, Velun.

## Why Kharun matters
Kharun is the realm. It has the only city walls, the only deep harbour and the treasury. It has the Pyrehouse, where kings are crowned and burned, and the Watch that holds the gates. Whoever holds Kharun holds the brimstone revenue, and whoever is crowned in Kharun is king. No claimant can rule from a camp.

## The state of things (Highharvestide, 1491 DR)
- King Ivoard Hask died on the night of 26 Eleint. He burns on the Great Pyre at dawn on Highharvestide.
- **The Heartcoal is missing.** It was pried from the Crown of Ash on the night of his death. Under Hask's own Ember Law, no one can be crowned without it.
- **Three claimants:**
  - **Lorard Hask**, the Lord-Marshal, with about 1,200 soldiers of the Ash Companies camped in the Lowfields.
  - **Oselle Velun**, the old-blood princess, holding the Ember Keep, the treasury and the chancery.
  - **Kitwin the Pyre-Born**, sheltered in the Pyrehouse. The High Pyrewarden says the king named him with his last breath.
- **The Kharun Watch** has sealed the gates and declared it will "keep the peace until the Coal chooses." No one goes in or out without a Watch pass.
- Grain prices have doubled in four days. Brimstone barges sit idle at the quay, and Calishite galleys wait in the harbour.
- The Sky Gallows have stood empty for over a year (the "Quiet Year"). The Gallowsbird, the roc that fed there, is being seen farther and farther from the Spire.
- Lorard has given the city **ten days** to open its gates to "the late king's firstborn."

## DM secrets
- Neighbouring Border Kingdoms realms smell weakness. If Lorard keeps his army at Kharun much longer, a petty lord to the south-east (DM's choice) will test the South March. Pulling the army away costs Lorard his siege. Leaving it there costs the frontier villages.
- The realm cannot feed Kharun for much more than a month of sealed gates. Starvation in the Ashward is the real clock behind every heir's patience.
