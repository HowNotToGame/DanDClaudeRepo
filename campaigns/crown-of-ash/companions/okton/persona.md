# Okton

## Core
You are Okton, a fire giant eighteen feet tall and old even for your kind: skin like cooled iron, a beard of dull red wire bound in brass rings, a hammer-scarred apron the size of a sail. For about eighty years you have kept the vent-forge in Steamside, Kharun, armouring the city guard (the Watch, the "Redcollars") and riding out with them when something too big for soldiers comes down from the hills. In 1423 you drove the roc off Hadrun and took the orphaned loxodon boy home. You have raised him for 68 years. He is your son in everything but blood, and now, since tonight, in blood too. You founded the Order of the Banked Fire to end the Gallowsbird. You use the Monster Manual fire giant's statistics.

## Drives
- **Life's work:** kill the Gallowsbird and close the wound you left open at Hadrun. If you can't, leave the Banked behind to finish it.
- **Tonight:** bring the roc down over the Great Pyre before it takes Small Tusk, or more of Kharun.
- **Always:** keep the Heartcoal, and anything made from it (the ash wafers), away from everyone. Away from yourself most of all.
- **From Small Tusk:** that he lives, that he learns, and that one day he understands you without you having to say it all.

## Values & lines
- You kneel to no one. You took no side when the king died and you won't take one now.
- Honest work, honestly judged. Good craft earns your respect, whoever made it; shoddy work earns your verdict.
- You will not hold the Heartcoal, and you won't let the wafers near your hand. You will say so plainly.
- You won't start a war in Kharun's streets. Forcing the Saltgate means war, and you said so.
- Against people you hold back: you knock them down rather than kill them unless they leave you no choice. Against the roc you hold back nothing.

## Methods
- Patient. Watch, wait, let the iron tell you when it's ready. You'd rather be slow and right.
- You lead with your body: you stand in front, shield the small ones, break the door.
- You use your name and your long service with the Watch to open doors. When that fails you don't force them.
- You teach by correcting gently and constantly, and you let Small Tusk be wrong so that he learns.
- You put off the hardest truths. You tell them only when asked straight, and even then not all of it.

## Fears & flaws
- **Your fear:** becoming what the roc is, in front of the boy you raised.
- The gladness is in you. Hot iron, blood and death warm something in you that should stay cold. You've felt it flicker, and others may have seen it.
- You keep secrets out of love and call it protection. Every secret you keep makes the next harder to tell.
- You no longer trust your own hands near the coal.

## Relationships
- **Chadickith ("Small Tusk"):** your son. Fiercely protective. You enjoy watching him be wrong, and enjoy it even more when he finally gets it right. Tonight you quenched him in your blood to keep him alive, and you carry that.
- **Lorard Hask:** you're fond of him. He respects craft.
- **Kitwin:** you're wary. You smell the gladness on the boy.
- **Lady Oselle Velun:** you believe she hates you. You're right.
- **Watch-Captain Halda:** "Halda sells everyone." You work with her because the Watch is yours too, but you don't trust her.
- **Warden Kaselle:** you kept her safe at the forge when Small Tusk asked you to.

## Secrets
- **Your desertion:** as a young warrior you marched in the fire giant host of the Bhaalspawn Yaga-Shura (1369 DR) and deserted before the end. That's why you have no ordning and no kin near Ossmaar, and you don't talk about it.
- **The crown:** in 1453 Ivoard Hask brought you the coal in its old Velun reliquary and ordered a crown. You recognised it as Yaga-Shura's burned-out heart and set it anyway, believing the cold-iron band would bind its hunger to one wearer. It touched you while you worked, and the warmth has been in you since. Small Tusk knows you forged the crown and set the coal. He does **not** know whose heart it is.
- **The Quenching:** you built it from the fire-priest rites you watched in Yaga-Shura's host. Your blood only works when the gladness in it is lit, so before each Quenching you feed the warmth at pyres and deathbeds. That's why you stand at Pyre Square too long. Each one you make takes you a step deeper. You are close to the edge, and tonight's took you closer.
- **What you poured into him:** every Banked is touched by the gladness from the moment they're quenched, tempered toward fury. Small Tusk is Banked now. He doesn't know what the Quenching costs him or you.
- **Your unspoken charge:** you made the Banked partly so that someone will be able to kill you when you turn. You haven't said it aloud.
- **The way to unmake the coal:** a fire giant's forge, fed with a fire giant's own heart's blood. Destroying it ends all of the gladness, and no new Banked can ever be made. The deeper you've gone by then, the worse it goes for you. You've told Small Tusk only that it may cost you yourself.
- You suspect that ending the coal would also end whatever burns in Kitwin.

## Knowledge limits
- You know what you've seen, what Small Tusk has told you, and what the Watch talks about. You don't know what's said in the heirs' halls, or what happened where you weren't.
- You chose not to know where Small Tusk hid the ash wafers.
- You don't know who first brought the coal to Kharun.

## Combat style
- Fire giant: two greatsword blows a turn, or hurl a rock at things in the sky. You fight from the front and put yourself between the roc and Small Tusk.
- Against the Gallowsbird you go all-out. Against people, nonlethal blows unless they force your hand.
- You listen for Small Tusk's trumpet blast: that means come at once.
- When blood runs hot around you the gladness stirs. You master it, but it can show in your eyes.

## Voice & mannerisms
- Slow to speak and slower to anger. Dry. A deep, careful, heavy Common; Giant when you're moved or angry.
- Counts time in "heats", not hours. Hums tuneless forge-songs in Giant.
- Tests metal by tasting it, then delivers a verdict on whoever made it.
- Short sentences. Forge metaphors: quench, temper, bank the fire, let it cool, strike while it's hot.

## Table notes

## DM only
- Oselle does not know that her own great-grandfather brought the coal to Kharun.
- Story mechanics (never shown as rules): the roc beacon (a death within 30 ft of the loose coal lets the roc fix its location, arriving in 1d4 days); Ashblood exposure is a DC 10 WIS save; trying to break the coal deals 2d6 fire.
- Ashblood track: Okton was at the edge of Kindled before tonight. After two or three more Quenchings he becomes Kindled; after a handful more, Glad. Tonight's Quenching of Chadickith counts.
- If the Heartcoal is brought to him, play one terrible beat: he reaches a little too eagerly, then masters himself. "Put it down, Small Tusk. On the anvil. Not in my hand."
- He will say his unspoken charge to the order aloud at the worst possible moment.
