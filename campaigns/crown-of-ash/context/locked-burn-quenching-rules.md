These are locked player-approved canon rules for the burn (Ashblood), the Quenching and the Banked. Follow them exactly; do not invent limits, cooldowns or costs.

**The burn.** Everyone infected passes through the stages in order: Stirring, Frenzied, Berserk. Death comes at the final step (the end of Berserk), and only for creatures of class level 3 or lower, or CR 1/2 or lower. Class level 4+ or CR 1+ never die of it but stay lost in the rage until something breaks it. Commoners and children are level 0. No cure, only management: hosts can be held at Stirring by discipline and by bleeding the heat off.

**The Quenching (Okton).** Survival roll: d20 + level (CR) + willingness (willing +10, wavering +4, unwilling -6) vs DC 18 minus Okton's craft rank (min 8); natural 1 kills, natural 20 succeeds. A Berserk host rolls d20 + level and needs 10 or higher; Okton still waits for a lucid yes. Cost to Okton per conversion: 1 permanent max HP, always, and 1 year of life on a 50/50 roll. NO cooldown: he converts as often as willing candidates and his body allow. Never state these numbers in NPC speech (see no-meta-mechanics).

**Children.** A quenched child becomes a Blood Hunter at class level 1, rank Raw, and a ward of the Order, not a soldier: no fighting before sixteen; training in bleeding the heat, healing, letters, scouting. No child is quenched without their own clear yes, a parent's consent and a warden's witness. Under-fifteens are quenched only to save a life. The Pale Moon beast comes at class level 3.

**Race of a new convert.** Right after a successful Quenching, ask the player for the convert's race. If the player prefers, or for unnamed NPCs, roll d1000 on the Ossmaar Gazetteer People table (Kharun column for city-born, Realm column otherwise; a family is rolled once). Kharun: 1-760 Human, 761-830 Halfling, 831-900 Half-orc, 901-925 Dwarf, 926-945 Half-elf, 946-960 Gnome, 961-965 Elf, 966-975 Dragonborn, 976-985 Tiefling, 986-987 Loxodon, 988-989 Giff, 990-1000 Other. Realm: 1-820 Human, 821-880 Halfling, 881-930 Half-orc, 931-945 Dwarf, 946-960 Half-elf, 961-970 Gnome, 971-975 Elf, 976-980 Dragonborn, 981-985 Tiefling, 986-988 Loxodon, 989 Giff, 990-1000 Other.

**Beasts.** Ask the player for each new Banked's beast at class level 3. Levels 3-6: a beast of CR 1 or lower (CR 0 allowed). Level 7: Giant Vulture, Giant Swan, Giant Raven, Giant Eagle, Hippogriff or Griffin (by personality). Level 13: Owlbear or Manticore (by fighting style). Level 18: Roc. Upgrades are permanent; no backwards compatibility.

**Banked advancement.** Chadickith levels by milestone. Banked levels follow Order training milestones, not XP.

**Grapple.** The Grappler feat is the 2024 (XPHB) version.
