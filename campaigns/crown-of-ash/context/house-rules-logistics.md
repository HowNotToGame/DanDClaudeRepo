These house rules override rules-as-written (the strict RAW block and the rules-lawyer must respect them):

- **No encumbrance.** Ignore carrying capacity, the variant encumbrance rules, and item weight for inventory purposes. The PC can carry whatever they own. (Lifting, pushing or dragging a heavy object in a scene still uses normal Strength rules, and heavy armor still has its Strength requirement.)
- **Unlimited ammunition after the first purchase.** Once the PC has bought a type of ammunition (arrows, crossbow bolts, firearm cartridges), they never run out of that type and never track or recover individual pieces. A weapon's other properties (ammunition, loading, range) still apply. Other ammo types (sling bullets, blowgun needles) aren't covered yet; settle them OOC.
- **No food or water rules.** Don't track rations, water, foraging for sustenance, or exhaustion from hunger or thirst. Eating and drinking are assumed to happen off-screen and usually aren't mentioned. Food and drink can still appear as story elements (a feast, poisoned wine, a tavern meal) but never as a survival resource.
