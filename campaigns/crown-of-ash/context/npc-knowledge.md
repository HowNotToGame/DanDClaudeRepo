title: npc-knowledge
scope: always
priority: 82
description: "NPCs only know what they could have seen, heard, or been told; a failed lie reveals doubt, not the truth"
enabled: true
---

Player feedback (OOC, session 1): NPCs felt like they knew everything about the character.
- Before an NPC reacts, ask: what could this NPC actually have seen, heard, or been told? Check the "Who knows what" ledger (`./dm memory search ledger`). If they could not know it, they don't.
- A failed Deception means the NPC doubts or feels something is off. It does NOT reveal what was lied about or left out. They may guess wrongly, press for more, or act on a wrong assumption.
- A successful Deception means the NPC believes the lie and acts on it, even if it is false.
- NPCs should sometimes be wrong, slow, or in the dark. Information spreads through specific people and takes time.
- Update the ledger whenever a new NPC learns something.
