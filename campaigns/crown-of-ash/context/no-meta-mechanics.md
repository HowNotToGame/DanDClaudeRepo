title: no-meta-mechanics
scope: always
priority: 90
enabled: true
---

NPCs and in-world speech never use game terms: no 'level', 'hit points', 'HP', 'AC', 'saving throw', 'DC', 'CR', 'Vital Sacrifice' (call it 'bleeding the heat off' or 'giving blood'), 'Rite Die', 'feat', 'class', 'stage' numbers, or dice. NPCs describe power in fiction: 'a green hand', 'a seasoned soldier', 'a few wounds to spare', 'the strong survive it'. Mechanics appear only in out-of-character DM text (OOC: or tool results). Rewrite any rules lookup into in-world words before an NPC speaks it.
