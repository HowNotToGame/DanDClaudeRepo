---
title: no-meta-mechanics
scope: always
priority: 90
enabled: true
---

NPCs and in-world speech, and the in-character narration of what they say and perceive, never use game terms or numbers. No 'level', 'hit points', 'HP', 'max HP', 'AC', 'saving throw', 'DC', 'CR', 'proficiency', 'ability score', 'strength 16', 'spell slot', 'Rite Die', 'Vital Sacrifice' (call it 'bleeding the heat off' or 'giving blood'), 'feat', 'class', 'stage' numbers, 'cooldown', 'once a month', or dice. Costs are told in fiction: 'it takes a piece of my strength I'll not get back', 'it can cost me a year', 'a seasoned soldier', 'a green hand', 'a few wounds to spare', 'the strong survive it'. Mechanics, numbers and rule names appear only in out-of-character DM text (OOC: lines and the roll results). Before an NPC speaks, rewrite any rules lookup or number into in-world words. When describing a character's own body or powers in narration, use sensation and result, not stat names or counts.
