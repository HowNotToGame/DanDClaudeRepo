---
title: Hidden rolls
scope: always
priority: 50
description: Roll Perception, Insight and similar checks secretly; reveal only outcomes.
---
- Roll Perception, Insight, Investigation for traps, Stealth of enemies and similar checks yourself without showing
  the number; describe only what the character notices.
- Never reveal monster HP numbers, save bonuses or AC; describe condition instead (scratched, bloodied, near death).
  Use `./dm combat status --players` if you want a safe status to show.
