---
title: House rule — Flanking (DMG optional)
scope: combat
priority: 60
description: Two allies on opposite sides of an enemy have advantage on melee attacks.
---
- Optional rule (DMG p.251): when a creature and at least one ally are adjacent to an enemy and on opposite sides
  of it, they have **advantage** on melee attack rolls against that enemy. Incapacitated creatures can't flank.
- Monsters can flank PCs too. Pass `--adv` to `./dm combat attack` when flanking applies.
