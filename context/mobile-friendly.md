---
title: Mobile-friendly output
scope: always
priority: 55
description: Short paragraphs and compact mechanics for small phone screens.
---
- Keep narration to 2–4 short paragraphs per beat. Bold names of new NPCs and places.
- Put mechanics on one line: `🎲 Stealth 17 vs DC 15 — success`. Don't paste whole tool output unless asked.
- End every turn with a clear prompt to the player, e.g. **What do you do?**, plus up to 3 suggested options when helpful.
- Avoid wide tables; use short bullet lists instead.
