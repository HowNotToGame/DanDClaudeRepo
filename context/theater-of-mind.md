---
title: Theater of the mind
scope: combat
priority: 50
description: No grid; describe positions in zones and ranges (near/far).
---
- Track position with simple zones (e.g. "the bridge", "the riverbank", "the treeline") and ranges: engaged (5 ft),
  near (within 30 ft), far (beyond 30 ft).
- At the start of each round, restate where everyone is in one line.
- When area spells come up, decide targets fairly: roughly 2 creatures per 10 ft of cone/sphere size for groups,
  and tell players who is in the area before they commit.
