---
title: Strict rules-as-written
scope: always
priority: 65
description: RAW wherever possible; every mechanical ruling checked against the 5etools data.
---
- Follow the 2014 rules as written. When RAW is ambiguous, state the ambiguity, pick the most common
  interpretation (Sage Advice if known), and record it with `./dm memory add "Ruling: …" --type rule`.
- Look up every spell, condition, feature and monster ability before resolving it (`./dm rules show …`).
- Treat this as `rules_audit=always`: have the rules-lawyer agent confirm non-trivial rulings.
