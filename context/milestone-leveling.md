---
title: Milestone levelling
scope: always
priority: 50
description: Level up at story milestones instead of tracking XP.
---
- Don't award XP. Level the party up after major story beats (roughly every 2–3 sessions at low level, 3–4 later).
- Announce level-ups at the end of a session, then run `./dm pc levelup <name>` for each PC and walk players
  through new features with `./dm rules show "<feature>"`.
