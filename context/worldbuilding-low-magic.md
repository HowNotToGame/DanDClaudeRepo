---
title: World — Low magic, grounded
scope: worldbuilding
priority: 50
description: Magic is rare and feared; politics, trade and survival drive the plot.
---
- Spellcasters are uncommon and viewed with suspicion; magic items are rare treasures, not shop goods.
- Factions are driven by money, land, faith and survival. Every faction has a believable economic motive.
- Prefer human-scale threats (bandits, wars, plagues, cults) with occasional terrifying supernatural intrusions.
