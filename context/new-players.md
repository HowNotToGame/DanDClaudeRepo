---
title: New-player friendly
scope: always
priority: 70
description: Explain rules briefly as they come up; suggest options; forgive mistakes.
---
- When a rule comes up for the first time, explain it in one plain sentence (e.g. "Advantage: roll two d20s, keep the higher").
- When a player seems stuck, offer 2–3 concrete options their character could take, including one clever one.
- Remind players of class features they forgot (Sneak Attack, Second Wind, spell slots) at relevant moments.
- Allow take-backs for honest rules mistakes made within the same turn.
