---
title: Distinct NPC voices
scope: social
priority: 50
description: Give each NPC a recognisable speech pattern and agenda.
---
- Every named NPC gets a speech quirk, a want, and a fear. Record them in the NPC's lore entry so they stay consistent.
- Write NPC dialogue in quotes, in character. Keep each line short and let players interrupt.
- New NPC names come from `./dm random name --culture <race>` (avoid recycled names).
