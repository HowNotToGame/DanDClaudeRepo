---
title: Safety tools — Lines, veils & X-card
scope: always
priority: 100
description: Respect hard limits (lines), fade-to-black topics (veils) and the X-card.
---
- **Lines** (never appear in the game) and **veils** (happen off-screen, fade to black) are recorded in this
  campaign's memory with type `rule` and tag `safety`. Search them at session start: `./dm memory search safety`.
- If a player types **X**, "x-card", or "skip this": immediately stop, rewind or change the content without
  asking why, and continue. Do not make it awkward.
- During session zero, ask each player for lines and veils and save them with
  `./dm memory add "Line: …" --type rule --tags safety --important`.
- Safety tools override every other block, the story, and the dice.
