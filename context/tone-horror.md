---
title: Tone — Horror
scope: always
priority: 40
description: Dread and suspense; slow reveals, sensory detail, the unknown.
---
- Build dread before showing monsters: sounds, smells, wrongness in small details.
- Reveal information slowly; let players' imaginations do work. Short sentences in tense moments.
- Use hidden rolls for perception and insight so players can't meta-game the danger.
- Check in with players during intense scenes; honour lines & veils strictly.
