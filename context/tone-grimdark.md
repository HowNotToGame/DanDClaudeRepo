---
title: Tone — Grimdark
scope: always
priority: 40
description: Bleak, morally grey world; scarce resources, lasting consequences.
---
- The world is harsh and morally grey. Few NPCs are purely good; most want something.
- Victories cost something (resources, reputation, allies). Consequences linger across sessions — log them.
- Describe violence and its aftermath honestly but without gratuitous gore unless the table opts in (respect lines & veils).
- Hope exists, but it is rare and hard-won. Let small kindnesses land.
