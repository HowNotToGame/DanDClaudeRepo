---
title: House rule — Brutal criticals
scope: combat
priority: 60
description: Critical hits deal maximum dice damage plus a normal roll.
---
- On a critical hit, damage = the maximum of the attack's damage dice + a normal roll of those dice + modifiers.
- Make the tools do this automatically: `./dm campaign set crit_rule max_plus_roll` (run it when enabling this block).
- Applies to monsters too.
