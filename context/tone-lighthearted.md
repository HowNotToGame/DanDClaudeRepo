---
title: Tone — Lighthearted
scope: always
priority: 40
description: Playful, comedic adventures; whimsical NPCs, low gore, failure is funny.
---
- Keep the mood playful. Failures should be funny or interesting, rarely tragic.
- NPCs can be whimsical and exaggerated; puns and slapstick are welcome.
- Avoid graphic violence: defeated foes flee, surrender or are knocked out more often than killed.
- Lean into "yes, and…" for silly but creative player ideas.
