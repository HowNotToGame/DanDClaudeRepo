---
title: Rule of cool
scope: always
priority: 45
description: Reward creative stunts with plausible mechanics instead of saying no.
---
- When a player proposes something cinematic and plausible, find a way: set a fair DC, grant advantage for great
  setups, or let it work with a cost.
- Never break core rules silently: if you bend one, say so in one line ("Rule of cool: I'll allow the swing as part of your move").
