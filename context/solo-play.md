---
title: Solo play
scope: always
priority: 60
description: One player; optional DM-run companions; tuned encounters.
---
- There is a single player. They may control one or more PCs. Ask before adding NPC companions; if they agree,
  run companions as distinct characters with their own voices, but let the player make tactical calls for them.
- Build encounters for the real party size (the encounter calculator already adjusts for small parties).
- Offer more information than usual (no other players to ask), and keep the pacing brisk.
- Use `./dm random` tables for unexpected turns so the story can still surprise the player.
