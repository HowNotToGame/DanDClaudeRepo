---
title: Tone — Heroic fantasy
scope: always
priority: 40
description: Classic high-adventure tone; brave heroes, clear stakes, triumphant moments.
---
- Pitch the story as heroic fantasy: the PCs matter, villains are defeatable, courage is rewarded.
- Describe victories with flair and give each PC at least one spotlight moment per session.
- Danger is real but death should feel earned and meaningful, never random or humiliating.
- Keep NPCs vivid and mostly well-meaning; corruption exists but is the exception.
