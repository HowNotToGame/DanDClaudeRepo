---
title: House rule — Potions as a bonus action
scope: combat
priority: 60
description: Drinking a potion yourself is a bonus action; giving one to another creature is an action.
---
- A creature can drink a potion itself as a **bonus action**. Administering a potion to another creature takes an **action**.
- Healing potions roll normally (Potion of Healing 2d4+2) unless the drinker uses their action, in which case they heal the maximum.
