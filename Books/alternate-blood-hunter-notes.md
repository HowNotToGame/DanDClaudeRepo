# Alternate Blood Hunter (laserllama, v2.2.0) — RoboDM working notes

Source: laserllama's *Alternate Blood Hunter* (GM Binder PDF, 19 pp; too large for the repo). These are my own
condensed notes of the numbers and rules the campaign needs, not a copy. The PDF is the authority if they disagree.

## Core
- d12 hit die; HP 12 + CON at 1st, then d12 (or 7) + CON. Saves: **CON, CHA**. Armor: light, medium, shields.
  Weapons: simple, martial. Tools: alchemist's supplies. Skills: choose 2 of Athletics, Acrobatics, Arcana, History,
  Insight, Investigation, Nature, Religion, Survival (no Perception or Intimidation).
- **Rite save DC** = 8 + PB + CON mod. **Rite Die**: d4 at levels 2-4, d6 at 5-10, d8 at 11-16, d10 at 17-20.
- Levels: 1 Fighting Style, Hunter's Bane · 2 Blood Rites (2 known), Crimson Offering · 3 Occult Order ·
  4 ASI · 5 Extra Attack, Grim Psychometry (identify as a Ritual with alchemist's supplies; learn if an object is cursed and the nature of the curse) · 6 Crimson Brand · 7 Order feature · 9 Improved Psychometry ·
  10 Dark Augmentation · 11 Improved Crimson Offering, Vital Control · 14 Crimson Anchor · 15 Hardened Soul ·
  20 Sanguine Mastery.

## Hunter's Bane (1st)
- **Occult Lore**: proficiency in Arcana, Nature or Religion (if already proficient, double PB on it).
- **Sinister Insight** (bonus action, 30 ft): CON check with the skill matching the creature type, DC 8 + CR.
  Arcana: aberrations, dragons, monstrosities · Nature: beasts, elementals, oozes, plants ·
  Religion: celestials, fey, fiends, undead. Success: learn one of AC / all special senses / resistances, immunities
  and vulnerabilities / highest ability score / lowest ability score / one trait or action. On a failure you can't
  retry on that target until a long rest or until you damage it.

## Blood Rites and Vital Sacrifice (2nd)
- Know 2 Rites. Each Rite is free once per short or long rest; afterward you must make a **Vital Sacrifice**.
- **Vital Sacrifice**: roll your Rite Die and reduce BOTH current and maximum HP by it (temp HP can absorb the
  current-HP loss, never the maximum). Max HP returns after a long rest. Empowering a Rite costs an extra sacrifice.
- Rites only target creatures with blood, but an empowering Vital Sacrifice lets you target any creature.
- Rites for any level: Agony, Anxiety, Binding (Large or smaller), Blindness (reaction), Confusion, Knowledge,
  Revelation, Siphoning (touch; 2 x Rite Die necrotic, gain that as temp HP).
- **Crimson Offering** (2nd): bonus action plus a Vital Sacrifice; choose acid, cold, fire, lightning or poison.
  For 1 hour the wielded weapon deals bonus damage of the chosen type equal to your Rite Die.

## Order list (3rd level)
Alchemists (Mutagens), Heretics, Pale Moon (lycanthropy), Salt & Iron (exorcists, Spectral State), Undying Thirst
(vampire hunters), Witch Knights (Pact Magic).

## Order of the Pale Moon (3rd level)
- **Feral Senses**: add your Rite Die to Perception and Survival checks that rely on sight, hearing or smell; add it to
  Sinister Insight checks against Beasts, Monstrosities and Shapeshifters.
- **Lycanthropic Curse**: pick one Beast of CR 1 or lower (permanent). Bonus action: Shift into Beast Form or Hybrid
  Form. One free Shift per short or long rest, then a Vital Sacrifice each time. Lasts up to 1 hour; ends early if you
  are Incapacitated or end it as a bonus action; a Vital Sacrifice extends it 1 hour.
- **Beast Form**: statistics become the Beast's, but you keep HP, alignment, INT/WIS/CHA, skill and save proficiencies
  (use the better when shared), class features and Blood Rites (if the Beast can physically do them). Natural weapons use
  your Rite Die as damage die and your Rite save DC. Equipment merges or falls (your choice). No special senses unless
  the Beast has them.
- **Hybrid Form**: keep your own statistics; +10 ft speed, climb speed = walking speed; unarmed strikes gain Finesse
  and deal Rite Die piercing/slashing; Wild Fury: bonus action unarmed strike or Dash (must move toward a Hostile you see).
- Campaign beast: **Giant Owl** (MM p.327: Large, AC 12, fly 60 ft, Flyby, talons 2d6+1, Keen Hearing and Sight,
  darkvision 120). Ruled that Flyby and the owl's traits apply in Beast Form. Hybrid Form keeps the trunk as a tail.

## House rulings recorded for this campaign
- A Vital Sacrifice cannot be made if it would reduce current HP to 0 or below.
- Class change from Barbarian to Blood Hunter is the price of the Quenching (m0056).
